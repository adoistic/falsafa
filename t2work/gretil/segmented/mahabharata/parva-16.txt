16.1.1	vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca ṣaṭtriṃśe tv atha saṃprāpte varṣe kauravanandanaḥ dadarśa viparītāni nimittāni yudhiṣṭhiraḥ
16.1.2	vavur vātāḥ sanirghātā rūkṣāḥ śarkaravarṣiṇaḥ apasavyāni śakunā maṇḍalāni pracakrire
16.1.3	pratyagūhur mahānadyo diśo nīhārasaṃvṛtāḥ ulkāś cāṅgāravarṣiṇyaḥ prapetur gaganād bhuvi
16.1.4	ādityo rajasā rājan samavacchannamaṇḍalaḥ viraśmir udaye nityaṃ kabandhaiḥ samadṛśyata
16.1.5	pariveṣāś ca dṛśyante dāruṇāḥ candrasūryayoḥ trivarṇāḥ śyāmarūkṣāntās tathā bhasmāruṇaprabhāḥ
16.1.6	ete cānye ca bahava utpātā bhayaśaṃsinaḥ dṛśyante 'har aho rājan hṛdayodvegakārakāḥ
16.1.7	kasya cit tv atha kālasya kururājo yudhiṣṭhiraḥ śuśrāva vṛṣṇicakrasya mausale kadanaṃ kṛtam
16.1.8	vimuktaṃ vāsudevaṃ ca śrutvā rāmaṃ ca pāṇḍavaḥ samānīyābravīd bhrātṝn kiṃ kariṣyāma ity uta
16.1.9	parasparaṃ samāsādya brahmadaṇḍabalātkṛtān vṛṣṇīn vinaṣṭāṃs te śrutvā vyathitāḥ pāṇḍavābhavan
16.1.10	nidhanaṃ vāsudevasya samudrasyeva śoṣaṇam vīrā na śraddadhus tasya vināśaṃ śārṅgadhanvanaḥ
16.1.11	mausalaṃ te pariśrutya duḥkhaśokasamanvitāḥ viṣaṇṇā hatasaṃkalpāḥ pāṇḍavāḥ samupāviśan
16.2.1	janamejaya uvāca kathaṃ vinaṣṭā bhagavann andhakā vṛṣṇibhiḥ saha paśyato vāsudevasya bhojāś caiva mahārathāḥ
16.2.2	vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca ṣaṭtriṃśe 'tha tato varṣe vṛṣṇīnām anayo mahān anyonyaṃ musalais te tu nijaghnuḥ kālacoditāḥ
16.2.3	janamejaya uvāca kenānuśaptās te vīrāḥ kṣayaṃ vṛṣṇyandhakā yayuḥ bhojāś ca dvijavarya tvaṃ vistareṇa vadasva me
16.2.4	vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca viśvāmitraṃ ca kaṇvaṃ ca nāradaṃ ca tapodhanam sāraṇapramukhā vīrā dadṛśur dvārakāgatān
16.2.5	te vai sāmbaṃ puraskṛtya bhūṣayitvā striyaṃ yathā abruvann upasaṃgamya daivadaṇḍanipīḍitāḥ
16.2.6	iyaṃ strī putrakāmasya babhror amitatejasaḥ ṛṣayaḥ sādhu jānīta kim iyaṃ janayiṣyati
16.2.7	ity uktās te tadā rājan vipralambhapradharṣitāḥ pratyabruvaṃs tān munayo yat tac chṛṇu narādhipa
16.2.8	vṛṣṇyandhakavināśāya musalaṃ ghoram āyasam vāsudevasya dāyādaḥ sāmbo 'yaṃ janayiṣyati
16.2.9	yena yūyaṃ sudurvṛttā nṛśaṃsā jātamanyavaḥ ucchettāraḥ kulaṃ kṛtsnam ṛte rāmajanārdanau
16.2.10	samudraṃ yāsyati śrīmāṃs tyaktvā dehaṃ halāyudhaḥ jarā kṛṣṇaṃ mahātmānaṃ śayānaṃ bhuvi bhetsyati
16.2.11	ity abruvanta te rājan pralabdhās tair durātmabhiḥ munayaḥ krodharaktākṣāḥ samīkṣyātha parasparam
16.2.12	tathoktvā munayas te tu tataḥ keśavam abhyayuḥ
16.2.13	athābravīt tadā vṛṣṇīñ śrutvaivaṃ madhusūdanaḥ antajño matimāṃs tasya bhavitavyaṃ tatheti tān
16.2.14	evam uktvā hṛṣīkeśaḥ praviveśa punar gṛhān kṛtāntam anyathā naicchat kartuṃ sa jagataḥ prabhuḥ
16.2.15	śvobhūte 'tha tataḥ sāmbo musalaṃ tad asūta vai vṛṣṇyandhakavināśāya kiṃkarapratimaṃ mahat
16.2.16	prasūtaṃ śāpajaṃ ghoraṃ tac ca rājñe nyavedayan viṣaṇṇarūpas tad rājā sūkṣmaṃ cūrṇam akārayat
16.2.17	prākṣipan sāgare tac ca puruṣā rājaśāsanāt aghoṣayaṃś ca nagare vacanād āhukasya ca
16.2.18	adya prabhṛti sarveṣu vṛṣṇyandhakagṛheṣv iha surāsavo na kartavyaḥ sarvair nagaravāsibhiḥ
16.2.19	yaś ca no 'viditaṃ kuryāt peyaṃ kaś cin naraḥ kva cit jīvan sa śūlam ārohet svayaṃ kṛtvā sabāndhavaḥ
16.2.20	tato rājabhayāt sarve niyamaṃ cakrire tadā narāḥ śāsanam ājñāya tasya rājño mahātmanaḥ
16.3.1	vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca evaṃ prayatamānānāṃ vṛṣṇīnām andhakaiḥ saha kālo gṛhāṇi sarveṣāṃ paricakrāma nityaśaḥ
16.3.2	karālo vikaṭo muṇḍaḥ puruṣaḥ kṛṣṇapiṅgalaḥ gṛhāṇy avekṣya vṛṣṇīnāṃ nādṛśyata punaḥ kva cit
16.3.3	utpedire mahāvātā dāruṇāś cā dine dine vṛṣṇyandhakavināśāya bahavo romaharṣaṇāḥ
16.3.4	vivṛddhamūṣakā rathyā vibhinnamaṇikās tathā cīcīkūcīti vāśyantyaḥ sārikā vṛṣṇiveśmasu nopaśāmyati śabdaś ca sa divārātram eva hi
16.3.5	anukurvann ulūkānāṃ sārasā virutaṃ tathā ajāḥ śivānāṃ ca rutam anvakurvata bhārata
16.3.6	pāṇḍurā raktapādāś ca vihagāḥ kālacoditāḥ vṛṣṇyandhakānāṃ geheṣu kapotā vyacaraṃs tadā
16.3.7	vyajāyanta kharā goṣu karabhāśvatarīṣu ca śunīṣv api biḍālāś ca mūṣakā nakulīṣu ca
16.3.8	nāpatrapanta pāpāni kurvanto vṛṣṇayas tadā prādviṣan brāhmaṇāṃś cāpi pitṝn devāṃs tathaiva ca
16.3.9	gurūṃś cāpy avamanyanta na tu rāmajanārdanau patnyaḥ patīn vyuccaranta patnīś ca patayas tathā
16.3.10	vibhāvasuḥ prajvalito vāmaṃ viparivartate nīlalohitamāñjiṣṭhā visṛjann arciṣaḥ pṛthak
16.3.11	udayāstamane nityaṃ puryāṃ tasyāṃ divākaraḥ vyadṛśyatāsakṛt puṃbhiḥ kabandhaiḥ parivāritaḥ
16.3.12	mahānaseṣu siddhe 'nne saṃskṛte 'tīva bhārata āhāryamāṇe kṛmayo vyadṛśyanta narādhipa
16.3.13	puṇyāhe vācyamāne ca japatsu ca mahātmasu abhidhāvantaḥ śrūyante na cādṛśyata kaś cana
16.3.14	parasparaṃ ca nakṣatraṃ hanyamānaṃ punaḥ punaḥ grahair apaśyan sarve te nātmanas tu kathaṃ cana
16.3.15	nadantaṃ pāñcajanyaṃ ca vṛṣṇyandhakaniveśane samantāt pratyavāśyanta rāsabhā dāruṇasvarāḥ
16.3.16	evaṃ paśyan hṛṣīkeśaḥ saṃprāptaṃ kālaparyayam trayodaśyām amāvāsyāṃ tāṃ dṛṣṭvā prābravīd idam
16.3.17	caturdaśī pañcadaśī kṛteyaṃ rāhuṇā punaḥ tadā ca bhārate yuddhe prāptā cādya kṣayāya naḥ
16.3.18	vimṛśann eva kālaṃ taṃ paricintya janārdanaḥ mene prāptaṃ sa ṣaṭtriṃśaṃ varṣaṃ vai keśisūdanaḥ
16.3.19	putraśokābhisaṃtaptā gāndhārī hatabāndhavā yad anuvyājahārārtā tad idaṃ samupāgatam
16.3.20	idaṃ ca tad anuprāptam abravīd yad yudhiṣṭhiraḥ purā vyūḍheṣv anīkeṣu dṛṣṭvotpātān sudāruṇān
16.3.21	ity uktvā vāsudevas tu cikīrṣan satyam eva tat ājñāpayām āsa tadā tīrthayātrām ariṃdama
16.3.22	aghoṣayanta puruṣās tatra keśavaśāsanāt tīrthayātrā samudre vaḥ kāryeti puruṣarṣabhāḥ
16.4.1	vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca kālī strī pāṇḍurair dantaiḥ praviśya hasatī niśi striyaḥ svapneṣu muṣṇantī dvārakāṃ paridhāvati
16.4.2	alaṃkārāś ca chatraṃ ca dhvajāś ca kavacāni ca hriyamāṇāny adṛśyanta rakṣobhiḥ subhayānakaiḥ
16.4.3	tac cāgnidattaṃ kṛṣṇasya vajranābham ayasmayam divam ācakrame cakraṃ vṛṣṇīnāṃ paśyatāṃ tadā
16.4.4	yuktaṃ rathaṃ divyam ādityavarṇaṃ; hayāharan paśyato dārukasya te sāgarasyopariṣṭhād avartan; manojavāś caturo vājimukhyāḥ
16.4.5	tālaḥ suparṇaś ca mahādhvajau tau; supūjitau rāmajanārdanābhyām uccair jahrur apsaraso divāniśaṃ; vācaś cocur gamyatāṃ tīrthayātrā
16.4.6	tato jigamiṣantas te vṛṣṇyandhakamahārathāḥ sāntaḥpurās tadā tīrthayātrām aicchan nararṣabhāḥ
16.4.7	tato bhojyaṃ ca bhakṣyaṃ ca peyaṃ cāndhakavṛṣṇayaḥ bahu nānāvidhaṃ cakrur madyaṃ māṃsam anekaśaḥ
16.4.8	tataḥ sīdhuṣu saktāś ca niryayur nagarād bahiḥ yānair aśvair gajaiś caiva śrīmantas tigmatejasaḥ
16.4.9	tataḥ prabhāse nyavasan yathoddeśaṃ yathāgṛham prabhūtabhakṣyapeyās te sadārā yādavās tadā
16.4.10	niviṣṭāṃs tān niśamyātha samudrānte sa yogavit jagāmāmantrya tān vīrān uddhavo 'rthaviśāradaḥ
16.4.11	taṃ prasthitaṃ mahātmānam abhivādya kṛtāñjalim jānan vināśaṃ vṛṣṇīnāṃ naicchad vārayituṃ hariḥ
16.4.12	tataḥ kālaparītās te vṛṣṇyandhakamahārathāḥ apaśyann uddhavaṃ yāntaṃ tejasāvṛtya rodasī
16.4.13	brāhmaṇārtheṣu yat siddham annaṃ teṣāṃ mahātmanām tad vānarebhyaḥ pradaduḥ surāgandhasamanvitam
16.4.14	tatas tūryaśatākīrṇaṃ naṭanartakasaṃkulam prāvartata mahāpānaṃ prabhāse tigmatejasām
16.4.15	kṛṣṇasya saṃnidhau rāmaḥ sahitaḥ kṛtavarmaṇā apibad yuyudhānaś ca gado babhrus tathaiva ca
16.4.16	tataḥ pariṣado madhye yuyudhāno madotkaṭaḥ abravīt kṛtavarmāṇam avahasyāvamanya ca
16.4.17	kaḥ kṣatriyo manyamānaḥ suptān hanyān mṛtān iva na tan mṛṣyanti hārdikya yādavā yat tvayā kṛtam
16.4.18	ity ukte yuyudhānena pūjayām āsa tad vacaḥ pradyumno rathināṃ śreṣṭho hārdikyam avamanya ca
16.4.19	tataḥ paramasaṃkruddhaḥ kṛtavarmā tam abravīt nirdiśann iva sāvajñaṃ tadā savyena pāṇinā
16.4.20	bhūriśravāś chinnabāhur yuddhe prāyagatas tvayā vadhena sunṛśaṃsena kathaṃ vīreṇa pātitaḥ
16.4.21	iti tasya vacaḥ śrutvā keśavaḥ paravīrahā tiryak saroṣayā dṛṣṭyā vīkṣāṃ cakre sa manyumān
16.4.22	maṇiḥ syamantakaś caiva yaḥ sa satrājito 'bhavat tāṃ kathāṃ smārayām āsa sātyakir madhusūdanam
16.4.23	tac chrutvā keśavasyāṅkam agamad rudatī tadā satyabhāmā prakupitā kopayantī janārdanam
16.4.24	tata utthāya sakrodhaḥ sātyakir vākyam abravīt pañcānāṃ draupadeyānāṃ dhṛṣṭadyumnaśikhaṇḍinoḥ
16.4.25	eṣa gacchāmi padavīṃ satyena ca tathā śape sauptike ye ca nihatāḥ suptānena durātmanā
16.4.26	droṇaputrasahāyena pāpena kṛtavarmaṇā samāptam āyur asyādya yaśaś cāpi sumadhyame
16.4.27	itīdam uktvā khaḍgena keśavasya samīpataḥ abhidrutya śiraḥ kruddhaś ciccheda kṛtavarmaṇaḥ
16.4.28	tathānyān api nighnantaṃ yuyudhānaṃ samantataḥ abhyadhāvad dhṛṣīkeśo vinivārayiṣus tadā
16.4.29	ekībhūtās tataḥ sarve kālaparyāyacoditāḥ bhojāndhakā mahārāja śaineyaṃ paryavārayan
16.4.30	tān dṛṣṭvā patatas tūrṇam abhikruddhāñ janārdanaḥ na cukrodha mahātejā jānan kālasya paryayam
16.4.31	te tu pānamadāviṣṭāś coditāś caiva manyunā yuyudhānam athābhyaghnann ucchiṣṭair bhājanais tadā
16.4.32	hanyamāne tu śaineye kruddho rukmiṇinandanaḥ tadantaram upādhāvan mokṣayiṣyañ śineḥ sutam
16.4.33	sa bhojaiḥ saha saṃyuktaḥ sātyakiś cāndhakaiḥ saha bahutvān nihatau tatra ubhau kṛṣṇasya paśyataḥ
16.4.34	hataṃ dṛṣṭvā tu śaineyaṃ putraṃ ca yadunandanaḥ erakāṇāṃ tadā muṣṭiṃ kopāj jagrāha keśavaḥ
16.4.35	tad abhūn musalaṃ ghoraṃ vajrakalpam ayomayam jaghāna tena kṛṣṇas tān ye 'sya pramukhato 'bhavan
16.4.36	tato 'ndhakāś ca bhojāś ca śaineyā vṛṣṇayas tathā jaghnur anyonyam ākrande musalaiḥ kālacoditāḥ
16.4.37	yas teṣām erakāṃ kaś cij jagrāha ruṣito nṛpa vajrabhūteva sā rājann adṛśyata tadā vibho
16.4.38	tṛṇaṃ ca musalībhūtam api tatra vyadṛśyata brahmadaṇḍakṛtaṃ sarvam iti tad viddhi pārthiva
16.4.39	āvidhyāvidhya te rājan prakṣipanti sma yat tṛṇam tad vajrabhūtaṃ musalaṃ vyadṛśyata tadā dṛḍham
16.4.40	avadhīt pitaraṃ putraḥ pitā putraṃ ca bhārata mattāḥ paripatanti sma pothayantaḥ parasparam
16.4.41	pataṃgā iva cāgnau te nyapatan kukurāndhakāḥ nāsīt palāyane buddhir vadhyamānasya kasya cit
16.4.42	taṃ tu paśyan mahābāhur jānan kālasya paryayam musalaṃ samavaṣṭabhya tasthau sa madhusūdanaḥ
16.4.43	sāmbaṃ ca nihataṃ dṛṣṭvā cārudeṣṇaṃ ca mādhavaḥ pradyumnaṃ cāniruddhaṃ ca tataś cukrodha bhārata
16.4.44	gadaṃ vīkṣya śayānaṃ ca bhṛśaṃ kopasamanvitaḥ sa niḥśeṣaṃ tadā cakre śārṅgacakragadādharaḥ
16.4.45	taṃ nighnantaṃ mahātejā babhruḥ parapuraṃjayaḥ dārukaś caiva dāśārham ūcatur yan nibodha tat

16.5.1	vaiśaṃpāyana uvāca tato yayur dārukaḥ keśavaś ca; babhruś ca rāmasya padaṃ patantaḥ athāpaśyan rāmam anantavīryaṃ; vṛkṣe sthitaṃ cintayānaṃ vivikte
16.5.2	tataḥ samāsādya mahānubhāvaḥ; kṛṣṇas tadā dārukam anvaśāsat gatvā kurūñ śīghram imaṃ mahāntaṃ; pārthāya śaṃsasva vadhaṃ yadūnām
16.5.3	tato 'rjunaḥ kṣipram ihopayātu; śrutvā mṛtān yādavān brahmaśāpāt ity evam uktaḥ sa yayau rathena; kurūṃs tadā dāruko naṣṭacetāḥ
16.5.4	tato gate dāruke keśavo 'tha; dṛṣṭvāntike babhrum uvāca vākyam striyo bhavān rakṣatu yātu śīghraṃ; naitā hiṃsyur dasyavo vittalobhāt
16.5.5	sa prasthitaḥ keśavenānuśiṣṭo; madāturo jñātivadhārditaś ca taṃ vai yāntaṃ saṃnidhau keśavasya; tvarantam ekaṃ sahasaiva babhrum brahmānuśaptam avadhīn mahad vai; kūṭonmuktaṃ musalaṃ lubdhakasya
16.5.6	tato dṛṣṭvā nihataṃ babhrum āha; kṛṣṇo vākyaṃ bhrātaram agrajaṃ tu ihaiva tvaṃ māṃ pratīkṣasva rāma; yāvat striyo jñātivaśāḥ karomi
16.5.7	tataḥ purīṃ dvāravatīṃ praviśya; janārdanaḥ pitaraṃ prāha vākyam striyo bhavān rakṣatu naḥ samagrā; dhanaṃjayasyāgamanaṃ pratīkṣan rāmo vanānte pratipālayan mām; āste 'dyāhaṃ tena samāgamiṣye
16.5.8	dṛṣṭaṃ mayedaṃ nidhanaṃ yadūnāṃ; rājñāṃ ca pūrvaṃ kurupuṃgavānām nāhaṃ vinā yadubhir yādavānāṃ; purīm imāṃ draṣṭum ihādya śaktaḥ
16.5.9	tapaś cariṣyāmi nibodha tan me; rāmeṇa sārdhaṃ vanam abhyupetya itīdam uktvā śirasāsya pādau; saṃspṛśya kṛṣṇas tvarito jagāma
16.5.10	tato mahān ninadaḥ prādurāsīt; sastrīkumārasya purasya tasya athābravīt keśavaḥ saṃnivartya; śabdaṃ śrutvā yoṣitāṃ krośatīnām
16.5.11	purīm imām eṣyati savyasācī; sa vo duḥkhān mocayitā narāgryaḥ tato gatvā keśavas taṃ dadarśa; rāmaṃ vane sthitam ekaṃ vivikte
16.5.12	athāpaśyad yogayuktasya tasya; nāgaṃ mukhān niḥsarantaṃ mahāntam śvetaṃ yayau sa tataḥ prekṣyamāṇo; mahārṇavo yena mahānubhāvaḥ
16.5.13	sahasraśīrṣaḥ parvatābhogavarṣmā; raktānanaḥ svāṃ tanuṃ tāṃ vimucya samyak ca taṃ sāgaraḥ pratyagṛhṇān; nāgā divyāḥ saritaś caiva puṇyāḥ
16.5.14	karkoṭako vāsukis takṣakaś ca; pṛthuśravā varuṇaḥ kuñjaraś ca miśrī śaṅkhaḥ kumudaḥ puṇḍarīkas; tathā nāgo dhṛtarāṣṭro mahātmā
16.5.15	hrādaḥ krāthaḥ śitikaṇṭho 'gratejās; tathā nāgau cakramandātiṣaṇḍau nāgaśreṣṭho durmukhaś cāmbarīṣaḥ; svayaṃ rājā varuṇaś cāpi rājan pratyudgamya svāgatenābhyanandaṃs; te 'pūjayaṃś cārghyapādyakriyābhiḥ
16.5.16	tato gate bhrātari vāsudevo; jānan sarvā gatayo divyadṛṣṭiḥ vane śūnye vicaraṃś cintayāno; bhūmau tataḥ saṃviveśāgryatejāḥ
16.5.17	sarvaṃ hi tena prāk tadā vittam āsīd; gāndhāryā yad vākyam uktaḥ sa pūrvam durvāsasā pāyasocchiṣṭalipte; yac cāpy uktaṃ tac ca sasmāra kṛṣṇaḥ
16.5.18	sa cintayāno 'ndhakavṛṣṇināśaṃ; kurukṣayaṃ caiva mahānubhāvaḥ mene tataḥ saṃkramaṇasya kālaṃ; tataś cakārendriyasaṃnirodham

16.9.8	mausale vṛṣṇivīrāṇāṃ vināśo brahmaśāpajaḥ babhūva vīrāntakaraḥ prabhāse romaharṣaṇaḥ
16.9.9	ye te śūrā mahātmānaḥ siṃhadarpā mahābalāḥ bhojavṛṣṇyandhakā brahmann anyonyaṃ tair hataṃ yudhi
16.9.10	gadāparighaśaktīnāṃ sahāḥ parighabāhavaḥ ta erakābhir nihatāḥ paśya kālasya paryayam
16.9.11	hataṃ pañcaśataṃ teṣāṃ sahasraṃ bāhuśālinām nidhanaṃ samanuprāptaṃ samāsādyetaretaram
16.9.12	punaḥ punar na mṛśyāmi vināśam amitaujasām cintayāno yadūnāṃ ca kṛṣṇasya ca yaśasvinaḥ
16.9.13	śoṣaṇaṃ sāgarasyeva parvatasyeva cālanam nabhasaḥ patanaṃ caiva śaityam agnes tathaiva ca
16.9.14	aśraddheyam ahaṃ manye vināśaṃ śārṅgadhanvanaḥ na ceha sthātum icchāmi loke kṛṣṇavinākṛtaḥ
16.9.15	itaḥ kaṣṭataraṃ cānyac chṛṇu tad vai tapodhana mano me dīryate yena cintayānasya vai muhuḥ
16.9.16	paśyato vṛṣṇidārāś ca mama brahman sahasraśaḥ ābhīrair anusṛtyājau hṛtāḥ pañcanadālayaiḥ
16.9.17	dhanur ādāya tatrāhaṃ nāśakaṃ tasya pūraṇe yathā purā ca me vīryaṃ bhujayor na tathābhavat
16.9.18	astrāṇi me pranaṣṭāni vividhāni mahāmune śarāś ca kṣayam āpannāḥ kṣaṇenaiva samantataḥ
16.9.19	puruṣaś cāprameyātmā śaṅkhacakragadādharaḥ caturbhujaḥ pītavāsā śyāmaḥ padmāyatekṣaṇaḥ
16.9.20	yaḥ sa yāti purastān me rathasya sumahādyutiḥ pradahan ripusainyāni na paśyāmy aham adya tam
16.9.21	yena pūrvaṃ pradagdhāni śatrusainyāni tejasā śarair gāṇḍīvanirmuktair ahaṃ paścād vyanāśayam
16.9.22	tam apaśyan viṣīdāmi ghūrṇāmīva ca sattama parinirviṇṇacetāś ca śāntiṃ nopalabhe 'pi ca
16.9.23	vinā janārdanaṃ vīraṃ nāhaṃ jīvitum utsahe śrutvaiva hi gataṃ viṣṇuṃ mamāpi mumuhur diśaḥ
16.9.24	pranaṣṭajñātivīryasya śūnyasya paridhāvataḥ upadeṣṭuṃ mama śreyo bhavān arhati sattama
16.9.25	vyāsa uvāca brahmaśāpavinirdagdhā vṛṣṇyandhakamahārathāḥ vinaṣṭāḥ kuruśārdūla na tāñ śocitum arhasi
16.9.26	bhavitavyaṃ tathā tad dhi diṣṭam etan mahātmanām upekṣitaṃ ca kṛṣṇena śaktenāpi vyapohitum
16.9.27	trailokyam api kṛṣṇo hi kṛtsnaṃ sthāvarajaṅgamam prasahed anyathā kartuṃ kim u śāpaṃ manīṣiṇām
16.9.28	rathasya purato yāti yaḥ sa cakragadādharaḥ tava snehāt purāṇarṣir vāsudevaś caturbhujaḥ
16.9.29	kṛtvā bhārāvataraṇaṃ pṛthivyāḥ pṛthulocanaḥ mokṣayitvā jagat sarvaṃ gataḥ svasthānam uttamam
16.9.30	tvayā tv iha mahat karma devānāṃ puruṣarṣabha kṛtaṃ bhīmasahāyena yamābhyāṃ ca mahābhuja
16.9.31	kṛtakṛtyāṃś ca vo manye saṃsiddhān kurupuṃgava gamanaṃ prāptakālaṃ ca tad dhi śreyo mataṃ mama
16.9.32	balaṃ buddhiś ca tejaś ca pratipattiś ca bhārata bhavanti bhavakāleṣu vipadyante viparyaye
16.9.33	kālamūlam idaṃ sarvaṃ jagadbījaṃ dhanaṃjaya kāla eva samādatte punar eva yadṛcchayā
16.9.34	sa eva balavān bhūtvā punar bhavati durbalaḥ sa eveśaś ca bhūtveha parair ājñāpyate punaḥ
16.9.35	kṛtakṛtyāni cāstrāṇi gatāny adya yathāgatam punar eṣyanti te hastaṃ yadā kālo bhaviṣyati
16.9.36	kālo gantuṃ gatiṃ mukhyāṃ bhavatām api bhārata etac chreyo hi vo manye paramaṃ bharatarṣabha
16.9.37	etad vacanam ājñāya vyāsasyāmitatejasaḥ anujñāto yayau pārtho nagaraṃ nāgasāhvayam
16.9.38	praviśya ca purīṃ vīraḥ samāsādya yudhiṣṭhiram ācaṣṭa tad yathāvṛttaṃ vṛṣṇyandhakajanaṃ prati
