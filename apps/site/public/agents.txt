# Falsafa — Agent Entry Point

Falsafa (https://falsafa.ai) is an open-access digital library of philosophy and the history of thought, currently indexing 2017 works across multiple languages and traditions. The site is fully static and open for reading and indexing.

## Corpus discovery

- **Curated summary** (LLM context-friendly, ~2 KB): https://falsafa.ai/llms.txt
- **Complete work index** (all 2017 works, grouped by era, ~200 KB): https://falsafa.ai/llms-full.txt

Each work is accessible at `https://falsafa.ai/works/<slug>/`. Chapters are served at `https://falsafa.ai/works/<slug>/<chapter-slug>/` with multiple text variants (original, translation, transliteration) selectable via query parameter `?v=<variant_id>`.

The corpus manifest (JSON, machine-readable) is available at: https://falsafa.ai/corpus/manifest.json
