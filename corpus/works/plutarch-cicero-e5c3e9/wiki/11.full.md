## Key passage (TextRank #1)
> [p-a04c1d] However, Catiline wished to obtain first a strong base of operations, and therefore sued for the consulship; and he had bright hopes that he would share the consulship with Caius Antonius, a man who, of himself, would probably not take the lead either for good or for bad, but would add strength to another who took the lead.

## Closes

And yet Cicero was the only one of the candidates who was the son, not of a senator, but of a knight.
