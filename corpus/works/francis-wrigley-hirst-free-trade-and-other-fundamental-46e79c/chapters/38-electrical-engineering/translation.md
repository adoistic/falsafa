A practical elementary text-book, written by an Engineer from an Engineering standpoint, and designed for the use of Students, for those engaged in the Mechanical and Electrical Engineering Trades, and for all who wish to acquire a knowledge of the chief principles and practice of the subject.

Translated by W. W. HALDANE QBE, B.Sc. (Lond.), A.M.I.E.E.,

Professor of Applied Physics at the Municipal School of Technology, Manchester, formerly lecturer of the Victoria University;

And CARL KINZBRUNNER,

Diplomate in Electrical Engineering of the Vienna Technical High School, Lecturer on Electrical Engineering at the Municipal School of Technology, Manchester.

Demy 8vo, with 263 Illustrations and Diagrams, Copious Index, etc.

Post Free, 6s.

The book covers a wide area. It comprises, besides the fundamental phenomena of the electric current, dynamos and motors for continuous, alternating, and three-phrase current, accumulators and their apparatus, measuring instruments and electric lighting.

In one way in which it differs from other books of an elementary kind is in the space that is given to alternating-current electrical engineering. This subject is usually practically ignored, or is given a treatment so mathematical that it is quite beyond the powers of most readers.

HARPER & BROTHERS, 45, ALBEMARLE ST., LONDON, W.
