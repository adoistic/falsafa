EPIGRAM.

Why will you, W**, seek with Paine to find
 Men like yourselves of discontented mind? 
Believe me, Sir, you may as well be quiet,
 Do what you will, you cannot hatch a riot;
 If Reformation's all you wish to get,
 Reform yourself, and leave the State to Pitt.
