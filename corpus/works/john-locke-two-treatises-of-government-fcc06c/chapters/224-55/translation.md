Children, I confess, are not born in this full state of equality, though they are born to it. Their parents have a sort of rule and jurisdiction over them, when they come into the world, and for some time after; but it is but a temporary one. The bonds of this subjection are like the swaddling clothes they art wrapt up in, and supported by, in the weakness of their infancy: age and Edition: current; Page: [241] reason as they grow up, loosen them, till at length they drop quite off, and leave a man at his own free disposal.
