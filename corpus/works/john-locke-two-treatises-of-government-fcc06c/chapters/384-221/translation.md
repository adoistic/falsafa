There is therefore, secondly, another way whereby governments are dissolved, and that is, when the legislative, or the prince, either of them, act contrary to their trust.

First, The legislative acts against the trust reposed in them, when they endeavour to invade the property of the subject, and to Edition: current; Page: [392] make themselves, or any part of the community, masters, or arbitrary disposers of the lives, liberties, or fortunes of the people.
