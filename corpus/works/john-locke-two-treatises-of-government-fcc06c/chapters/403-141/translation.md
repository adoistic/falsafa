But farther, this question, (Who shall be judge?) cannot mean, that there is no judge at all: for where there is no judicature on earth, to decide controversies amongst men, God in heaven is judge. He alone, it is true, is judge of the right. But every man is judge for himself, as in all other cases, so in this, whether another hath put Edition: current; Page: [415] himself into a state of war with him, and whether he should appeal to the Supreme Judge, as Jeptha did.
