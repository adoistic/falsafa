His arrival, therefore, in the town was not very agreeable to the people; and this appeared at the next public spectacle. For when the actors in a farce began a well-known song, Venit, io, Simus a villa

Lo! Clodpate from his village comes all the spectators, with one voice, went on with the rest, repeating and acting the first verse several times over.
