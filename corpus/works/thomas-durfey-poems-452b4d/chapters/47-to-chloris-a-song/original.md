To Chloris: A SONG.

IF my Addresses are grateful,
Shew it in granting my Suit,
Or if my Passion be hateful,
Leave me and end the dispute:
I hate your doubling and turning,
Like a cours'd Hare in a Morning,
Either comply as you should,
Or leave me to others that would.
