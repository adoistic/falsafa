**10**

From the unmanifest (avyakta) the [cosmic] egg arose; from the egg, Brahmā; thence the emission of creatures. This whole unfolding, made of {{māyā|the deluding creative power that produces the dual world}}, is in turn withdrawn again, step by step.

**11**

The aggregate of instruments [made] of the {{guṇa|the three qualities}}s, though composed of {{māyā|deluding creative power}} and insentient, performs actions; the embodied one (dehin) that presides over it, though sentient, does nothing whatever.

**12**

Just as iron, though it is insentient, moves when a magnet stands near, so the assemblage of instruments stirs into activity when the body is presided over by consciousness (cit).

**13**

Just as, when the sun has risen, this world of living beings performs its actions, yet the sun neither does those actions nor causes them to be done — so too the {{ātman / paramātman|the Self}}.

**14**

For the deluded mind, swooned [into identity] by the I-sense (ahaṃkāra) and [yet] awakened by consciousness, there arises here the conceit 'I am the person ({{puruṣa|the conscious self}})' and the experiencing of pleasure and pain.

**15**

"I am the doer, the enjoyer, the witness of actions high and low" — thus does the {{ātman / paramātman|Self}}, [though] by its own nature stainless and all-pervading, [falsely] presume.

**16**

As a flawless crystal takes on the color of variously colored [objects placed near it], so the all-pervading [Self] takes on the character of the {{upādhi|limiting adjunct that makes the Self appear conditioned}}, which is itself shaped by the {{guṇa|qualities}}s.

**17**

As the sun's reflected disc moves when the water moves and comes to rest when [the water] is still, so here too the {{ātman / paramātman|Self}} [seems to] move when the inner organ (antaḥkaraṇa) moves.

**18**

As Rāhu, though [himself] invisible, becomes manifest to the world when [he] stands upon the disc of the moon, so the {{ātman / paramātman|Self}}, though all-pervading, comes into view when [reflected as] resident in the intellect (buddhi).

**19**

All-pervading, beyond compare, non-dual ({{advaita|non-duality}}) — that is to be reached by the mind; the {{brahman|the absolute, the non-dual ground of being}} which, [reflected as] residing in the intellect, is apprehended — that, O disciple, is what is to be understood.
