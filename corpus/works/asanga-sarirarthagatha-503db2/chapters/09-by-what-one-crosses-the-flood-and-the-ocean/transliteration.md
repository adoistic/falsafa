**Śag_9.1**

kena svid oghaṃ tarati kenottarati cārṇavaṃ / duḥkhaṃ tyajati kena svit kena svit pariśudhyati //

**Śag_9.2**

śraddhayā tarati hy ogham apramādena cārṇavaṃ / vīryeṇa duḥkhaṃ tyajati prajñayā pariśudhyati //
