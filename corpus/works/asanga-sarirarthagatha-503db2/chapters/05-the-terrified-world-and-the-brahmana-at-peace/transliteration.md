**Śag_5.1**

nityotrasto hy ayaṃ loko nityodvignā iyaṃ prajā / anutpanneṣu duḥkheṣu samutpanneṣu vā punaḥ / yadi kiṃcid anutrastaṃ pṛṣṭa ācakṣva tan mama //

**Śag_5.2**

nānyatra jñānatapaso nānyatrendriyanigrahāt / nānyatra sarvasaṃtyāgān mokṣaṃ paśyāmi devate //

**Śag_5.3**

cirasya bata paśyāmi brāhmaṇaṃ parinirvṛtaṃ / sarvavairabhayātītaṃ tīrṇaṃ loke viṣaktikām //
