**Śag_7.1**

kathaṃ yaśasvī bhavati kathaṃ bhavati bhogavān / kathaṃ kīrtim avāpnoti kathaṃ mitrāṇi vindati //

**Śag_7.2**

śīlād yaśasvī bhavati dānād bhavati bhogavān / satyena kīrtim āpnoti dadan mitrāṇi vindati //
