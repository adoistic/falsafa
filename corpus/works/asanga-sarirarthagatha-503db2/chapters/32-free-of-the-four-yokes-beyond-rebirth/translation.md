**Śag_32**

He whose [discursive] musings are burnt away, inwardly free of all discrimination without remainder—he, having transcended attachment and the perception of form, freed from the {{caturyoga:four yokes|kāma, bhava, dṛṣṭi, avidyā}}, comes to no [further] birth.
