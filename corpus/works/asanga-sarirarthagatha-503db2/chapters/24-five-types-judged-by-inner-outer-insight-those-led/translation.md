**Śag_24.1**

Those who measure me by [outward] form and follow me by [mere] sound, fallen under the sway of desire and passion—those people do not know me.

**Śag_24.2**

One who understands the inward but does not see the outward, who beholds [only] the inward fruit—he indeed is led by mere sound.

**Śag_24.3**

One who does not know the inward but discerns the outward, who beholds [only] the outward fruit—he too is led by mere sound.

**Śag_24.4**

One who neither knows the inward nor sees the outward, a fool enveloped on every side—he too is led by mere sound.

**Śag_24.5**

One who understands the inward and also discerns the outward—the steadfast one, wise in the way of escape—he is not led by mere sound.
