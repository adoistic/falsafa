**Śag_2.1**

ākhyeyasaṃjñinaḥ satvā ākhyeye 'smin pratiṣṭhitāḥ / ākhyeyam aparijñāya yogam āyānti mṛtyunaḥ //

**Śag_2.2**

ākhyeyaṃ tu parijñāya ākhyātāraṃ na manyate / tad vai na vidyate tasya vadeyur yena taṃ pare //

**Śag_2.3**

samo viśeṣa uta vāpi hīno yo manyate sa vivadeta tena / vidhātraye 'smin na vikampate yaḥ samo viśiṣṭaś ca na tasya bhavati //

**Śag_2.4**

ācchidya tṛṣṇām iha nāmarūpe prahāya mānaṃ ca na saṃgam eti / taṃ śāntadhūmam anighaṃ nirāśaṃ nādrākṣus te devamanuṣyaloke / iha bāhirataś ca //
