**Śag_10.1**

Who crosses this flood, day and night untiring? Who, in the supportless, baseless deep, does not sink?

**Śag_10.2**

He who is wholly perfect in virtue, wise, well composed, inwardly reflective, and mindful—he crosses this [flood] so hard to cross.

**Śag_10.3**

Detached from the perceptions of sensual desire, gone beyond the fetter of form—he, in the supportless, baseless deep, does not sink.
