**Śag_8.1**

kutaḥ sarā nivartante kutra vartma na vartate / kutra duḥkhasukhaṃ loke niḥśeṣam uparudhyate //

**Śag_8.2**

cakṣuḥ śrotraṃ tathā ghrāṇaṃ jihvā kāyo manas tathā / yatra nāma ca rūpaṃ ca niḥśeṣam uparuhyate //

**Śag_8.3**

tataḥ sarā nivartante tatra vartma na vartate / tatra duḥkhasukhaṃ loke niḥśeṣam uparudhyate //
