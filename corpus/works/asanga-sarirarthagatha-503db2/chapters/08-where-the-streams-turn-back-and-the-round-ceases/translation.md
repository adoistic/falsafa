**Śag_8.1**

Whence do the streams turn back? Where does the track not run? Where, in the world, does pleasure-and-pain cease without remainder?

**Śag_8.2**

Eye, ear, and nose, tongue, body, and likewise mind—[there the streams turn back;] where {{nāmarūpa:name and form|the psycho-physical complex}} both cease without remainder.

**Śag_8.3**

Thence the streams turn back; there the track does not run; there, in the world, pleasure-and-pain ceases without remainder.
