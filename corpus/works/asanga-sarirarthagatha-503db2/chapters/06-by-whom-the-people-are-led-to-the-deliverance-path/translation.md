**Śag_6.1**

By whom is the populace extolled and led on, and the {{nairyāṇika:deliverance-leading|conducing to release}} path set forth? Established where, and training in what, does this mortal not dread the world beyond?

**Śag_6.2**

He who is virtuous, wise, of cultivated self, composed, mindful, and upright—all his fevers of grief are abandoned, who is rightly mindful and whose mind is liberated.

**Śag_6.3**

By him is the populace extolled and led on, and the deliverance-leading path set forth; established herein, and training herein, this mortal does not dread the world beyond.
