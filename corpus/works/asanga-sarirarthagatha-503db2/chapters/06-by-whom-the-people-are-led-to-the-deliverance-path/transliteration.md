**Śag_6.1**

kenābhivarṇā janatā praṇītā mārgaś ca nairyāṇikataḥ prayuktaḥ / kutra sthitaḥ kutra ca śikṣamāṇo nāyaṃ martyaḥ paralokād bibheti //

**Śag_6.2**

yaḥ śīlavāñ jñānavān bhāvitātmā samāhitaḥ smṛtimān ṛjugataś ca / sarve 'sya śokajvarathāḥ prahīṇāḥ samyaksmṛto yasya cittaṃ vimuktaṃ //

**Śag_6.3**

tenābhivarṇā janatā praṇītā mārgaś ca nairyāṇikataḥ prayuktaḥ / atra sthitaḥ atra ca śikṣamāṇo nāyaṃ martyaḥ paralokād bibheti //
