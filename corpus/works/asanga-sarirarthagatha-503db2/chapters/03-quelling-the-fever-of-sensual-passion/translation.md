**Śag_3.1**

Overpowered by sensual passion, my mind is wholly aflame; pray, Gautama, out of compassion, tell me of peace.

**Śag_3.2**

It is through the distortion of perceptions that your mind is aflame; therefore let the attractive sign that is conjoined with passion be shunned.

**Śag_3.3**

Cultivate ever the {{aśubhā(-bhāvanā):unattractive|the foulness meditation}}, one-pointed and well composed; swiftly quench the fire of passion, lest you be burned again and again.

**Śag_3.4**

See the conditioned {{saṃskāra:formations|conditioned, volitional constructs}} as wretched, as suffering, and likewise as not-self; setting up mindfulness directed to the body, abound in disgust [for them].

**Śag_3.5**

And let the {{animitta:signless|the objectless concentration that is also a gateway to liberation}} be cultivated, the destroyer of the latent tendency to conceit; then, through the full comprehension of conceit, you will make an end of suffering.
