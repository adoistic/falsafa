ANTI-SLAVERY TRACTS. No. 16.

the

UNANIMOUS REMONSTRANCE

of the

FOURTH CONGREGATIONAL CHURCH,

HARTFORD, Conn.,

against the

Policy of the American Tract Society

of

THE SUBJECT OF SLAVERY.

The following unanimous Remonstrance of the Fourth Congregational Church of Hartford, Conn., was originally published and circulated to the number of several thousands by private individuals. The stereotype plates having been generously presented to the American Anti-Slavery Society, it has been adopted as a tract in their series, and for obvious reasons is published without alteration. The committee by whom it was prepared consisted of the Pastor, (Rev. Wm. W. Patton,) John Hooker, Esq., and Mr. Milo Doty.
