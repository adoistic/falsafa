That the fugitive slave law of 1793, (Stat. 1793, c. 7,) entitled “An Act respecting fugitives from justice, and persons escaping from the service of their masters,” and the act of Sept. 18, 1850, “to amend, and supplementary to” this act, are both framed to carry out this clause of the Constitution, is too apparent to need comment.
