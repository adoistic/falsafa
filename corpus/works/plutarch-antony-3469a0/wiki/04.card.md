## Closes
> [p-9664ef] To one of his friends he ordered that two hundred and fifty thousand drachmas should be given (a sum which the Romans call decies). His steward was amazed, and in order to show Antony the magnitude of the sum, deposited the money in full view. Antony, passing by, asked what that was; and when his steward told him it was the gift which he had ordered, he divined the man’s malice and said: I thought the decies was more; this is a trifle; therefore add as much more to it.
