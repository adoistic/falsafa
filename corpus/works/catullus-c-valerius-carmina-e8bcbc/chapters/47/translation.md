Beautifully it fits the shameless sodomites, Mamurra and sexually submissive Caesar. It’s no wonder: they share like stains—the one from the City, the other, Formian— which stay deep-marked and they can not be washed off. Debauched twins each, both learned, both in one bed, one not more than the other the greater greedier adulterer, allied rivals of the girls.
