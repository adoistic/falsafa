Gellius is thin: why not? He who lives with so good a mother, so healthy and so beauteous a sister, and who has such a good uncle, and a world-full of girl cousins, why would he cease to be skinny?

who if he touched nothing but what is not lawful to touch—you will find ample reason why he is skinny.
