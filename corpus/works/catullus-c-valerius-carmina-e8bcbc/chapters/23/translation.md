Boy cupbearer of old Falernian, pour me more pungent cups as bids the laws of Postumia, mistress of the feast, drunker than a drunken grape.

But off with you, as far as you please, crystal waters, bane of wine, depart you to the sober: here the Thyonian juice is pure.
