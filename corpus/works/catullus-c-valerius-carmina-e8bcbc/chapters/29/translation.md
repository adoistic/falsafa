O best of the thieves of the baths, Vibennius the father, and his sexually submissive son (for the father is the filthier with the right hand, the son is the greedier with the backside), why don’t you go into exile and to hellish shores, seeing that the father’s plunder is known to the people, and that, son, you cannot sell your hairy butt for one cent?
