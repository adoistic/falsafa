Cleon, too, was already harassing him, taking advantage of the wrath with which the citizens regarded him to make his own way toward the leadership of the people, as these anapaestic verses of Hermippus show:— Thou king of the Satyrs, why pray wilt thou not Take the spear for thy weapon, and stop the dire talk With the which, until now, thou conductest the war.
