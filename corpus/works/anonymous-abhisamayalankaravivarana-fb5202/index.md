# Abhisamayālaṅkāravivaraṇa

Abhisamayālaṅkāravivaraṇa, a Sanskrit philosophical text by Anonymous, with transliteration and Thothica's English translation from the GRETIL source text. A Tibetan-tradition padārtha digest of Maitreya's Abhisamayālaṅkāra, quoting the root kārikās, Haribhadra's Ālokā, and the Mahāyānasūtrālaṅkāra; edited by Ram Shankar Tripathi in 1977.

Citation scheme: Native digest scheme: padārtha-class . subgroup . topic-number. Part I = the 8 abhisamayas (I.1–I.8). Part II = the 70 padārthas in three groups: A (30 dharmas of the three knowledges: 10/11/9), B (36 dharmas of the four applications: 11/8/13/4), C (4 dharmas of the dharmakāya). Part III = the closing pariṇāmanā. Each topic carries the fixed sub-slots lakṣaṇa / bheda / avadhi (and occasionally paryāya, nirvacana, liṅga, vaiśiṣṭya), here folded into a single translated unit per topic..
