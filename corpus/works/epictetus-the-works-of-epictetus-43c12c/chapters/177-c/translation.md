Kindly receive those who are willing to give good advice; but not those who upon every occasion are eager to flatter. For the former truly see what is Edition: current; Page: [423] advantageous; but the latter consider only the opinions of their superiors; and imitate the shadows of bodies, nodding assent to what they say.
