These reasonings have no logical connection: “I am richer than you; therefore I am your superior”: Edition: current; Page: [395] “I am more eloquent than you; therefore I am your superior.” The true logical connection is rather this: “I am richer than you; therefore my possessions must exceed yours”: “I am more eloquent than you; therefore my style must surpass yours.” But you, after all, consist neither in property nor in style.
