As, if you were to breed lions, you would not be solicitous about the magnificence of their dens, but about the qualities of the animals; so, if you undertake to preside over your fellow-citizens, be not so Edition: current; Page: [419] solicitous about the magnificence of the buildings, as careful of the nobleness of those who inhabit them.
