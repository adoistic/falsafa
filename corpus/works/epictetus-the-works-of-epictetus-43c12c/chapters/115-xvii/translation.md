Remember that you are an actor in a drama of such sort as the author chooses. If short, then in a short one; if long, then in a long one. If it be his pleasure that you should act a poor man, see that you act it well; or a cripple, or a ruler, or a private Edition: current; Page: [382] citizen. For this is your business, to act well the given part; but to choose it, belongs to another.
