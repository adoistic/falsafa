328. To Hyperechius
I both greet your Honour and pray that good things may be yours; and I disclose my own affairs to you, who are altogether eager to know how we are faring, namely that I am doing nothing better than my usual state. For I spare you the more dismal news, so as not to grieve too greatly one who joins us in praying for the best.
