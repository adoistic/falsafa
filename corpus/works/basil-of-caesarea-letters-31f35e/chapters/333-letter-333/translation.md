333. To a secretary
Words are winged by nature. For this reason they require signs, so that the writer may catch their speed as they take flight. You, then, my boy, make your strokes complete, and place the points correctly in their proper places. For by a small error much of the sense is corrupted, whereas by the writer's care what is spoken is set down aright.
