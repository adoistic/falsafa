According to Hultsch no fragments or extracts of book 17 are preserved. In it would have been contained the campaign of B. C. 199, in the war between Rome and Philip, for which see -43. And the operations of Flamininus in the season of B. C. 198, -18. The first seventeen chapters of this book are generally classed in book 17.
