**SK 43**

sāṃsiddhikāś ca bhāvāḥ prākṛtikā vaikṛtāś ca dharmādyāḥ
dṛṣṭāḥ karaṇāśrayiṇaḥ kāryāśrayiṇaś ca kalalādyāḥ.

**SK 44**

dharmeṇa gamanam ūrdhvaṃ gamanam adhastād bhavaty a-dharmeṇa
jñānena cā ’pavargo viparyayād iṣyate bandhaḥ.

**SK 45**

vai-rāgyāt prakṛti-layaḥ saṃsāro bhavati rājasād rāgāt
aiśvaryād a-vighāto viparyayāt tad-viparyāsaḥ.

**SK 46**

eṣa pratyaya-sargo viparyayāśakti-tuṣṭi-siddhy-ākhyaḥ
guṇa-vaiṣamya-vimardāt tasya ca bhedās tu pañcāśat.

**SK 47**

pañca viparyaya-bhedā bhavanty a-śakteś ca karaṇa-vaikalyāt
aṣṭāviṃśati-bhedās tuṣṭir navadhā ’ṣṭadhā siddhiḥ.

**SK 48**

bhedas tamaso ’ṣṭa-vidho mohasya ca daśa-vidho mahā-mohaḥ
tāmisro ’ṣṭā-daśadhā tathā bhavaty andha-tāmisraḥ.

**SK 49**

ekādaśendriya-vadhāḥ saha buddhi-vadhair a-śaktir uddiṣṭā
saptadaśa vadhā buddher viparyayāt tuṣṭi-siddhīnām.

**SK 50**

ādhy-ātmikāś catasraḥ prakṛty-upādāna-kāla-bhāgyākhyāḥ
bāhyā viṣayoparamāc ca pañca nava tuṣṭayo ’bhimatāḥ.

**SK 51**

ūhaḥ śabdo ’dhyayanaṃ duḥkha-vighātās trayaḥ su-hṛt-prāptiḥ
dānaṃ ca siddhayo ’ṣṭau siddheḥ pūrvo ’ṅkuśas tri-vidhaḥ.

**SK 52**

na vinā bhāvair liṅgaṃ na vinā liṅgena bhāva-nirvṛttiḥ
liṅgākhyo bhāvākhyas tasmād dvi-vidhaḥ pravartate sargaḥ.
