**SK 15**

bhedānāṃ parimāṇāt samanvayāc chaktitaḥ pravṛtteś ca
kāraṇa-kārya-vibhāgād a-vibhāgād vaiśva-rūpasya.

**SK 16**

kāraṇam asty avyaktaṃ pravartate tri-guṇataḥ samudayāc ca
pariṇāmataḥ salilavat prati-prati-guṇāśraya-viśeṣāt.

**SK 17**

saṃghāta-parārthatvāt tri-guṇādi-viparyayād adhiṣṭhānāt
puruṣo ’sti bhoktṛ-bhāvāt kaivalyārthaṃ pravṛtteś ca.

**SK 18**

janana-maraṇa-karaṇānāṃ prati-niyamād a-yugapat pravṛtteś ca
puruṣa-bahutvaṃ siddhaṃ trai-guṇya-viparyayāc caiva.

**SK 19**

tasmāc ca viparyāsāt siddhaṃ sākṣitvam asya puruṣasya
kaivalyam mādhya-sthyaṃ draṣṭṛtvam a-kartṛ-bhāvaś ca.

**SK 20**

tasmāt tat-saṃyogād a-cetanaṃ cetanāvad iva liṅgaṃ
guṇa-kartṛtve ca tathā kartā iva bhavaty udāsīnaḥ.

**SK 21**

puruṣasya darśanārthaṃ kaivalyārthaṃ tathā pradhānasya
paṅgv-andhavad ubhayor api saṃyogas tat-kṛtaḥ sargaḥ.
