**SK 56**

ity eṣa prakṛti-kṛto mahad-ādi-viśeṣa-bhūta-pary-antaḥ
prati-puruṣa-vimokṣārthaṃ svārtha iva parārtha ārambhaḥ.

**SK 57**

vatsa-vivṛddhi-nimittaṃ kṣīrasya yathā pravṛttir a-jñasya
puruṣa-vimokṣa-nimittaṃ tathā pravṛttiḥ pradhānasya.

**SK 58**

autsukya-nivṛtty-arthaṃ yathā kriyāsu pravartate lokaḥ
puruṣasya vimokṣārtham pravartate tadvad a-vyaktam.

**SK 59**

raṅgasya darśayitvā nivartate nartakī yathā nṛtyāt
puruṣasya tathā ’tmānam prakāśya vinivartate prakṛtiḥ.

**SK 60**

nānā-vidhair upāyair upakāriṇy an-upakāriṇaḥ puṃsaḥ
guṇavaty a-guṇasya satas tasyā ’rtham apārthakaṃ carati.

**SK 61**

prakṛteḥ su-kumārataraṃ na kiṃ-cid astī ’ti me matir bhavati
yā dṛṣṭā ’smī ’ti punar na darśanam upaiti puruṣasya.
