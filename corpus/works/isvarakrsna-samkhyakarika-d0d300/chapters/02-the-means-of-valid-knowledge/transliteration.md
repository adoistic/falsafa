**SK 4**

dṛṣṭam anumānam āpta-vacanaṃ ca sarva-pramāṇa-siddhatvāt
tri-vidhaṃ pramāṇam iṣṭaṃ prameya-siddhiḥ pramāṇād dhi.

**SK 5**

prati-viṣayādhyavasāyo dṛṣṭaṃ tri-vidham anumānam ākhyātam
tal liṅga-liṅgi-pūrvakam āpta-śrutir āpta-vacanaṃ tu.

**SK 6**

sāmānyatas tu dṛṣṭād atīndriyāṇām pratītir anumānāt
tasmād api cā’siddham parokṣam āptāgamāt siddham.

**SK 7**

ati-dūrāt sāmīpyād indriya-ghātān mano’navasthānāt
saukṣmyād vyavadhānād abhibhavāt samānābhihārāc ca.

**SK 8**

saukṣmyāt tad-an-upalabdhir nā’bhāvāt kāryatas tad-upalabdhiḥ
mahad-ādi tac ca kāryam prakṛti-vi-rūpaṃ sa-rūpaṃ ca.
