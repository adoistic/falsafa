TO TERENTIA (AT ROME) BRUNDISIUM, I SEPTEMBER

If you are well, I am glad. I am well. I am expecting my letter-carriers any time today. If they come, I shall perhaps learn what I shall have to do, and will at once let you know. Take good care of your health. Good-bye. 1 September.
