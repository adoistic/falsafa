EPITAPH.

TO darkest deeds the fairest tombs
 Erected are by pride;
 But modest virtue never tries,
 By art your steps to guide. 
Truth was her search while here below,
 She's found it now in heaven;
 The soul's set free, while to this earth
 The dust alone is given.
