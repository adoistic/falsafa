**2.1**  I, ever-giving, call upon you much, O Agni — truly yours. Like a refuge from the sharp thorn, you are a shelter of greatness.

**2.2**  Carry forth the ancient word, great and primal, to Agni the Hotṛ. Like a sage bearing the lights of the waters, he is the wise ordainer.

**2.3**  O Agni, lord of wealth rich in cattle, O son of strength — grant to us, O Jātavedas, great renown.

**2.4**  O Agni, most worthy of worship at the rite, sacrifice to the gods for the god-seeking worshipper. The Hotṛ, gladdening, shines forth, dispelling failures.

**2.5**  Born, he sought wisdom from the seven mothers for glory. This one, firm and steadfast among riches, became discerning.

**2.6**  And may this thought of ours, Aditi, come to us by day with her aid. May she, bringing peace, grant us delight and drive away failures.

**2.7**  Praise him indeed, worship Jātavedas who faces all directions — whose smoke moves freely, whose flame cannot be grasped.

**2.8**  No mortal foe can overcome him, whether by craft or cunning, who has given offering to Agni, the receiver of oblations.

**2.9**  Drive away that crooked foe, that thief, O Agni, that ill-minded one. Make the path easy, far away from him, O lord of the good.

**2.10**  Hear, O Agni, my new hymn of praise, O hero, lord of peoples. Burn down with your heat the deceitful demons.

**2.11**  Praises go up to Agni.

**2.12**  He, O Agni, crosses over with your aids, with good heroes, with prize-winning deeds — he whose friendship you have cherished.

**2.13**  Honour him, the swift golden one; the gods have sent the divine messenger onward. You carry the oblation to the gods.

**2.14**  Let not Agni, the wealth-giving guest, much-praised and ruling, be wrathful with us — he who is the good Hotṛ, the one of excellent rites.

**2.15**  Auspicious is Agni when invoked for us, auspicious is his gift, O blessed one; auspicious is the rite, and auspicious too are the praises.

**2.16**  We have chosen you, most worthy of worship, divine Hotṛ among the gods, immortal — of good purpose for this sacrifice.

**2.17**  O Agni, bring that splendour which, conquering all, sits in the abode, overcoming every devourer — the wrath of the ill-minded among people.

**2.18**  Or when the lord of the clan, sharp and well-pleased, guards the tribe of Manu — then Agni of all things repels the demons.

**2.19**  Sing that for him, when the Soma is pressed, together, to the much-invoked hero — as a blessing, like to the able bull for the cow.

**2.20**  That rapture of yours, O Indra of a hundred powers, which is most radiant — with that rapture exhilarate us now.

**2.21**  The cows low near the great trough, the prize-giver of the sacrifice, with both its golden ears.

**2.22**  Sing fittingly for the horse, for the famous one of the shoulder-strap, and for the cow — fittingly for the dwelling of Indra.

**2.23**  We speed on Indra, great for the slaying of Vṛtra. May he be the bull, the virile one.

**2.24**  You, O Indra, were born above strength, from might and vigour. You, winning as a bull, are the bull indeed.

**2.25**  The sacrifice strengthened Indra when he rolled back the earth and made an attachment in the sky.

**2.26**  O Indra, if I were as you, the sole lord of wealth — then my singer would be the companion of my cattle.

**2.27**  O pressers, hasten again and again, worthy of praise, for the exhilarating drink — Soma for the hero, for the champion.

**2.28**  O Vasu, drink this pressed Soma-juice with a well-filled belly. We have granted it to you without fear.

**2.29**  You travel, O Sun, as the unsetting one.

**2.30**  Whatever you rise over today, O Vṛtra-slayer, O Sun shining upon all — all that, O Indra, is under your will.

**2.31**  He who led Turvaśa and Yadu from the distant land with good guidance — that Indra is our ever-young companion.

**2.32**  O Indra, let not the sun restrain us from any direction in the night. With you as ally may we win that.

**2.33**  O Indra, bring the winning wealth, conquering and ever-victorious, the most abundant, for our aid.

**2.34**  We call upon Indra in the great battle for great wealth, and Indra in the small — the thunderbolt-wielder, the yoke-fellow in conflicts.

**2.35**  Indra drank the pressed Soma of the Kadrū plant at the thousand-armed one's feast. There he declared his heroic might.

**2.36**  We who desire you, O Indra, call out to you again and again, O bull. Know yourself as ours, O lord of wealth.

**2.37**  Those who kindle Agni and strew the sacred grass in order — Indra is the ever-young companion of such men.

**2.38**  Break all enmities, repel the pressures on every side, slay the scorners. Then bring the desirable wealth.

**2.39**  They adorn the wondrous path downward.

**2.40**  These Soma-pressing companions gaze upon you, O Indra — as those rich in cattle watch over the animal.

**2.41**  Before the wrath of that one, all peoples and all tribes bow down — as rivers bow to the ocean.

**2.42**  The great help of the gods — that we choose for ourselves; the aid of the bountiful bulls, for our protection.

**2.43**  Make the Soma-cups resound, O Lord of Prayer. May the Auśija, he of the armpit, Kakṣīvant, be your Soma-drinker.

**2.44**  Let the Vṛtra-slayer of many pressings be alert in mind for us. May the mighty Śakra hear the blessing.

**2.45**  O divine Savitṛ, today impel auspicious fortune for us together with progeny. Drive away ill dreams.

**2.46**  Where is this young bull, thick-necked and unbowing? What Brahmin priest serves him with devotion?

**2.47**  In the hidden place of the mountains and at the confluence of the rivers, the wise one was born through inspired thought.

**2.48**  Let the praiser sing forth, with fresh songs, Indra the sovereign of all peoples, the hero, the vanquisher of men, the most bountiful.

**2.49**  He drank with his jaw the Soma juice of him of beautiful jaw, the well-skilled one who pours offerings — Indra indeed is Indra, the barley-mixed.

**2.50**  These songs, O Wealthy One, resound toward you in praise — like milch-cows lowing toward their calf.

**2.51**  Here indeed they found the secret name of the cow belonging to Tvaṣṭṛ — thus in the house of the Moon.

**2.52**  When Indra, unhindered and most showering, led forth the great waters, there Pūṣan was present at his side.

**2.53**  The shining cow gives suck — she who is glory-seeking, the mother of the Maruts, the generous ones, harnessed as the bearer of the chariots.

**2.54**  Come to us with your tawny steeds to the pressed soma, O Lord of raptures — come to us with your tawny steeds to the pressed soma.

**2.55**  The desired priestly libations have been poured forth, magnifying Indra at the rite — toward the concluding bath with vigor.

**2.56**  I alone have grasped from the Father the wisdom of immortality — I was born like the sun.

**2.57**  Let our joint festival-joys in Indra be rich, abundantly strong — rich in wealth, by which we may rejoice.

**2.58**  Soma and Pūṣan, perceivers of all good dwellings, are yoked as charioteers in the divine realm.

**2.59**  The all-conquering, hundred-powered, most bountiful one among men.

**2.60**  Sing forth for Indra the exhilarating hymn, O friends — for the tawny-steeded, soma-drinking one.

**2.61**  We indeed, O Indra, for that very purpose — friends devoted to you — the Kaṇvas grow old praising you with recitations.

**2.62**  For Indra, for his exhilaration, let our songs resound around the pressed soma — let the singers worship with the hymn.

**2.63**  This soma of yours, O Indra, is purified upon the sacred grass — come, hasten here, drink of it.

**2.64**  For aid we invoke the beautiful craftsman, as a milker milks a good-milking cow — day by day we call.

**2.65**  O Bull, toward you I pour the pressed soma upon the pressing-stones for your drinking — be satisfied, spread your exhilaration wide.

**2.66**  The soma that is poured for you, O Indra, in the cups and in the vessels — you are the lord over drinking of it.

**2.67**  At each yoking we call upon the most powerful, at each prize-contest — friends calling Indra for his aid.

**2.68**  Come here, sit down, and sing forth to Indra — O friends who bring the hymns of praise.

**2.69**  Drink, O you who are delighted by songs.

**2.70**  Great is Indra, and before us too let greatness belong to the thunderbolt-bearer — his might is broad like the sky.

**2.71**  Come now, O Indra, seize and gather for us the splendid, wealth-rich prize — with your great right hand.

**2.72**  Praise and sing forth to Indra the lord of the mountain-cows as you know him — the son of Truth, the true lord.

**2.73**  By what means shall the wondrous one, the ever-growing friend, come to us in aid — accompanied by what most powerful deed?

**2.74**  That altogether-conquering one, stretched forth in all your songs — set him in motion for your aid.

**2.75**  For the wonderful lord of the assembly hall, the dear and beloved one of Indra, I have sought the winning of wisdom.

**2.76**  Those paths of yours that are beneath the sky, by which you sent forth Vyaśva — may the worlds hear us too.

**2.77**  Bring to us blessings upon blessings, food and strength, O Hundred-powered — when you, O Indra, show us grace.

**2.78**  This soma has been pressed — the Maruts drink of it, and likewise the self-ruling Aśvins.

**2.79**  Winning for themselves great heroic strength.

**2.80**  We do not diminish the gods, we do not obstruct anything — we walk in the hearing of sacred utterance.

**2.81**  Evening has come — sing the great song, the bright song, O descendant of Atharvan — praise the god Savitṛ.

**2.82**  This Dawn, unprecedented, the dear one of the sky, shines forth — I praise the two lovely Aśvins greatly.

**2.83**  Indra, unapproachable, with the bones of Dadhīca smote the obstacles — ninety-nine he slew.

**2.84**  Come, O Indra, exhilarate yourself with all the soma-pressings — the great one, powerful in conquest.

**2.85**  Come, O Indra, slayer of Vṛtra, come to our side — the great one with great aids.

**2.86**  His might gleams in that — when he rolled both together, Indra spread the two worlds apart like a hide.

**2.87**  This one is like a pigeon carrying an embryo toward you, O equal one — do you not carry that speech for us?

**2.88**  May the Wind blow its medicine, bringing peace and joy to our heart — may it extend our life-spans for us.

**2.89**  That person is not deceived by anyone.

**2.90**  As before with cattle, with horses, and with chariots — honor us with the bounty of the great ones.

**2.91**  These dappled cows of yours, O Indra, milk clarified butter mixed with milk — swollen full with the immortal.

**2.92**  By this thought and by the prayer for cattle, O much-named, much-praised one — when you, O Soma, arose as Soma.

**2.93**  May Sarasvatī, pure, rich in nourishment, possessing strength — she who is wealthy in prayer — desire our sacrifice.

**2.94**  Who among the Nahuṣa-folk would satisfy Indra here with soma? Let him bring us riches.

**2.95**  Come here — for the way is good for you, O Indra — drink this soma; this sacred grass is my seat.

**2.96**  Great and lowest among the three, gleaming in the sky — the might of Mitra and Aryaman, and the unconquerable power of Varuṇa.

**2.97**  We, O Indra, O much-wealthy one, O guide, are yours — we stand as holders of your tawny steeds.
