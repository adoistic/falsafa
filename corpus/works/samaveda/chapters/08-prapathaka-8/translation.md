**8.1**  Know, O Maghavan, know the path in the directions that have been proclaimed. Teach us, O lord of mighty deeds, O Purūvasu, of many ancient gifts.

**8.2**  With aids of conquest thou art the conqueror; the soma ray is thine own. O all-knowing one, enlighten us, O Indra, for glory and for nourishment.

**8.3**  For so does Śakra, the wielder of the thunderbolt, come for wealth and sustenance. O mightiest thunderbolt-bearer, thou dost adorn thyself; O most munificent thunderbolt-bearer, thou dost adorn thyself. Come, drink, and be exhilarated.

**8.4**  Know wealth and heroic strength; become lord of victories, following according to will. O most munificent thunderbolt-bearer, thou dost adorn thyself, thou who art the mightiest among heroes.

**8.5**  He who is most bountiful among the givers of gifts, his soma ray blazes like fire. The all-wise one, may Indra lead us onward; he is known — praise him.

**8.6**  For Śakra is the master; we call upon him for protection, the conqueror who is never vanquished. May he set us beyond our foes in the heavenly realm; great is his will, his hymn, and his cosmic order.

**8.7**  We call upon Indra for the winning of wealth, the conqueror who is never vanquished. May he place us in the solar realm beyond our foes; may he place us in the solar realm beyond our foes.

**8.8**  Of thine that is ancient, O crusher of stones, the soma draught is for exhilaration. Bestow well-being upon us, O Vasu; O mightiest one, fulfillment is proclaimed. For Śakra, the sovereign, now lays down the new body.

**8.9**  O lord of the people, O slayer of Vṛtra, let us declare thee in battles. The hero who goes forth among the cattle, the friend who is gracious and undeceitful.

**8.10**  Even so, O serpent — so indeed. Even so, O Agni. Even so, O Indra. Even so, O Pūṣan. Even so, O gods. Om — even so, O gods.
