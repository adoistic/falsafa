**4.1**

ima indrāya sunvire somāso dadhyāśiraḥ . tāṃ ā madāya vajrahasta pītaye haribhyāṃ yāhyoka ā

**4.2**

ima indra madāya te somāścikitra ukithanaḥ . madhoḥ papāna upa no giraḥ śṛṇu rāsva stotrāya girvaṇaḥ

**4.3**

ā tvā3dya sabardughāṃ huve gāyatravepasam . indraṃ dhenuṃ sudughāmanyāmiṣamurudhārāmaraṅkṛtam

**4.4**

na tvā bṛhanto adrayo varanta indra vīḍavaḥ . yacchikṣasi stuvate māvate vasu na kiṣṭadā mināti te

**4.5**

ka īṃ veda sute sacā pibantaṃ kadvayo dadhe . ayaṃ yaḥ puro vibhinatyojasā mandānaḥ śipryandhasaḥ

**4.6**

yadindra śāso avrataṃ cyāvayā sadasaspari . asmākamaṃśuṃ maghavanpuruspṛhaṃ vasavye adhi barhaya

**4.7**

tvaṣṭā no daivyaṃ vacaḥ parjanyo brahmaṇaspatiḥ . putrairbhrātṛbhiraditirnu pātu no duṣṭaraṃ trāmaṇaṃ vacaḥ

**4.8**

kadā ca na starīrasi nendra saścasi dāśuṣe . upopennu maghavanbhūya innu te dānaṃ devasya pṛcyate

**4.9**

yuṅkṣvā hi vṛtrahantama harī indra parāvataḥ . arvācīno maghavantsomapītaya ugra ṛṣvebhirā gahi

**4.10**

tvāmidā hyo naro 'pīpyanvajrinbhūrṇayaḥ . sa indra stomavāhasa iha śrudhyupa svasaramā gahi

**4.11**

apo mahī vṛṇute cakṣuṣā tamo jyotiṣkṛṇoti sūnari

**4.12**

imā u vāṃ diviṣṭaya usrā havante aśvinā . ayaṃ vāmahve 'vase śacīvasū viśaṃviśaṃ hi gacchathaḥ

**4.13**

ku ṣṭhaḥ ko vāmaśvinā tapāno devā martyaḥ . ghnatā vāmaśmayā kṣapāmāṇoṃśunetthamu ādunyathā

**4.14**

ayaṃ vāṃ madhumattamaḥ sutaḥ somo diviṣṭiṣu . tamaśvinā pibataṃ tiro ahnyaṃ dhattaṃ ratnāni dāśuṣe

**4.15**

ā tvā somasya galdayā sadā yācannahaṃ jyā . bhūrṇiṃ mṛgaṃ na savaneṣu cukrudhaṃ ka īśānaṃ na yāciṣat

**4.16**

adhvaryo drāvayā tvaṃ somamindraḥ pipāsati . upo nūnaṃ yuyuje vṛṣṇā harī ā ca jagāma vṛtrahā

**4.17**

abhī ṣatastadā bharendra jyāyaḥ kanīyasaḥ . purūvasurhi maghavanbabhūvitha bharebhare ca havyaḥ

**4.18**

yadindra yāvatastvametāvadahamīśīya . stotāramiddadhiṣe radāvaso na pāpatvāya raṃsiṣam

**4.19**

tvamindra pratūrtiṣvabhi viśvā asi spṛdhaḥ . aśastihā janitā vṛtratūrasi tvaṃ tūrya taruṣyataḥ

**4.20**

pra yo ririkṣa ojasā divaḥ sadobhyaspari . na tvā vivyāca raja indra pārthivamati viśvaṃ vavakṣitha

**4.21**

bodhāmasi tvā haryaśva yajñairbodhā na stomamandhaso madeṣu

**4.22**

yoniṣṭa indra sadane akāri tamā nṛbhiḥ purūhūta pra yāhi . aso yathā no 'vitā vṛdhaściddado vasūni mamadaśca somaiḥ

**4.23**

adardarutsamasṛjo vi khāni tvamarṇavānbadbadhānāṃ aramṇāḥ . mahāntamindra parvataṃ vi yadvaḥ sṛjaddhārā ava yaddānavānhan

**4.24**

suṣvāṇāsa indra stumasi tvā saniṣyantaścittuvinṛmṇa vājam . ā no bhara suvitaṃ yasya konā tanā tmanā sahyāmā tvotāḥ

**4.25**

jagṛhmā te dakṣiṇamindra hastaṃ vasūyavo vasupate vasūnām . vidmā hi tvā gopatiṃ śūra gonāmasmabhyaṃ citraṃ vṛṣaṇaṃ rayindāḥ

**4.26**

indraṃ naro nemadhitā havanti yatpāryā yunajate dhiyastāḥ . śūro nṛṣātā śravasaśca kāma ā gomati vraje bhajā tvaṃ naḥ

**4.27**

vayaḥ suparṇā upa sedurindraṃ priyamedhā ṛṣayo nādhamānāḥ . apa dhvāntamūrṇuhi pūrdhi cakṣurmumugdhyā3smānnidhayeva baddhān

**4.28**

nāke suparṇamupa yatpatantaṃ hṛdā venanto abhyacakṣata tvā . hiraṇyapakṣaṃ varuṇasya dūtaṃ yamasya yonau śakunaṃ bhuraṇyum

**4.29**

brahma jajñānaṃ prathamaṃ purastādvi sīmataḥ suruco vena āvaḥ . sa budhnyā upamā asya viṣṭhāḥ sataśca yonimasataśca vivaḥ

**4.30**

apūrvyā purutamānyasmai mahe vīrāya tavase turāya . virapśine vajriṇe śantamāni vacāṃsyāsmai sthavirāya takṣuḥ

**4.31**

āvattamindraḥ śacyā dhamantamapa snīhitiṃ nṛmaṇā adhadrāḥ

**4.32**

vṛtrasya tvā śvasathādīṣamāṇā viśve devā ajahurye sakhāyaḥ . marudbhirindra sakhyaṃ te astvathemā viśvāḥ pṛtanā jayāsi

**4.33**

vidhuṃ dadrāṇaṃ samane bahūnāṃ yuvānaṃ santaṃ palito jagāra . devasya paśya kāvyaṃ mahitvādyā mamāra sa hyaḥ samāna

**4.34**

tvaṃ ha tyatsaptabhyo jāyamāno 'śatrubhyo abhavaḥ śatrurindra . gūḍhe dyāvāpṛthivī anvavindo vibhumadbhyo bhuvanebhyo raṇaṃ dhāḥ

**4.35**

meḍiṃ na tvā vajriṇaṃ bhṛṣṭimantaṃ purudhasmānaṃ vṛṣabhaṃ sthirapsnum . karoṣyaryastaruṣīrduvasyurindra dyukṣaṃ vṛtrahaṇaṃ gṛṇīṣe

**4.36**

pra vo mahe mahevṛdhe bharadhvaṃ pracetase pra sumatiṃ kṛṇudhvam . viśaḥ pūrvīḥ pra cara carṣaṇiprāḥ

**4.37**

śunaṃ huvema maghavānamindramasminbhare nṛtamaṃ vājasātau . śṛṇvantamugramūtaye samatsu dhnantaṃ vṛtrāṇi sañjitaṃ dhanāni

**4.38**

udu brahmāṇyairata śravasyendraṃ samarye mahayā vasiṣṭha . ā yo viśvāni śravasā tatānopaśrotā ma īvato vacāṃsi

**4.39**

cakraṃ yadasyāpsvā niṣattamuto tadasmai madhviccacchadyāt . pṛthivyāmatiṣitaṃ yadūdhaḥ payo goṣvadadhā oṣadhīṣu

**4.40**

ariṣṭanemiṃ pṛtanājamāśuṃ svastaye tārkṣyamihā huvema

**4.41**

trātāramindramavitāramindraṃ havehave suhavaṃ śūramindram . huve nu śakraṃ puruhūtamindramidaṃ havirmaghavā vetvindraḥ

**4.42**

yajāmaha indraṃ vajradakṣiṇaṃ harīṇāṃ rathya3ṃ vivratānām . pra śmaśrubhirdodhuvadūrdhvadhā bhuvadvi senābhirbhayamāno vi rādhasā

**4.43**

satrāhaṇaṃ dādhṛṣiṃ tumramindraṃ mahāmapāraṃ vṛśabhaṃ suvajram . hantā yo vṛtraṃ sanitota vājaṃ dātā maghāni maghavā surādhāḥ

**4.44**

yo no vanuṣyannabhidāti marta ugaṇā vā manyamānasturo vā . kṣidhī yudhā śavasā vā tamindrābhī ṣyāma vṛṣamaṇastvotāḥ

**4.45**

yaṃ vṛtreṣu kṣitaya spardhamānā yaṃ yukteṣu turayanto havante . yaṃ śūrasātau yamapāmupajmanyaṃ viprāso vājayante sa indraḥ

**4.46**

indrāparvatā bṛhatā rathena vāmīriṣa ā vahataṃ suvīrāḥ . vītaṃ havyānyadhvareṣu devā vardhethāṃ gīrbhīriḍayā madantā

**4.47**

indrāya giro aniśitasargā apaḥ prairayatsagarasya budhnāt . yo akṣeṇeva cakriyau śacībhirviṣvaktastambha pṛthivīmuta dyām

**4.48**

ā tvā sakhāyaḥ sakhyā vavṛtyustiraḥ purū cidarṇavāṃ jagamyāḥ . piturnapātamā dadhita vedhā asminkṣaye pratarāṃ dīdyānaḥ

**4.49**

ko adya yuṅkte dhuri gā ṛtasya śimīvato bhāmino durhṛṇāyūn . āsanneṣāmapsuvāho mayobhūnya eṣāṃ bhṛtyāmṛṇadhatsa jīvāt

**4.50**

gāyanti tvā gāyatriṇo 'rcantyarkamarkiṇaḥ . brahmāṇastvā śatakrata udvaṃśamiva yemire

**4.51**

indraṃ viśvā avīvṛdhantsamudravyacasaṃ giraḥ . rathītamaṃ rathīnāṃ vājānāṃ satpatiṃ patim

**4.52**

imamindra sutaṃ piba jyeṣṭhamamartyaṃ madam . śukrasya tvābhyakṣarandhārā ṛtasya sādane

**4.53**

yadindra citra ma iha nāsti tvādātamadrivaḥ . rādhastanno vidadvasa ubhayāhastyā bhara

**4.54**

śrudī havaṃ tiraścyā indra yastvā saparyati . suvīryasya gomato rāyaspūrdhi mahāṃ asi

**4.55**

asāvi soma indra te śaviṣṭha dhṛṣṇavā gahi . ā tvā pṛṇaktvindriyaṃ rajaḥ sūryo na raśmibhiḥ

**4.56**

endra yāhi haribhirupa kaṇvasya suṣṭutim . divo amuṣya śāsato divaṃ yaya divāvaso

**4.57**

ā tvā giro rathīrivāsthuḥ suteṣu girvaṇaḥ . abhi tvā samanūṣata gāvo vatsaṃ na dhenavaḥ

**4.58**

eto nvindraṃ stavāma śuddhaṃ śuddhena sāmnā . śuddhairukthairvāvṛdhvāṃsaṃ śuddhairāśīrvānmamattu

**4.59**

yo rayiṃ vo rayintamo yo dyumnairdyumnavattamaḥ . somaḥ sutaḥ sa indra te 'sti svadhāpate madaḥ

**4.60**

araṅgamāya jagmaye 'paścādadhvane naraḥ

**4.61**

ā no vayovayaḥśayaṃ mahāntaṃ gahvareṣṭhāṃ mahāntaṃ pūrvineṣṭhām . ugraṃ vaco apāvadhīḥ

**4.62**

ā tvā rathaṃ yathotaye sumnāya vartayāmasi . tuvikūrmimṛtīṣahamindraṃ śaviṣṭha satpatim

**4.63**

sa pūrvyo mahonāṃ venaḥ kratubhirānaje . yasya dvārā manuḥ pitā deveṣu dhiya ānaje

**4.64**

yadī vahantyāśavo bhrājamānā ratheṣvā . pibanto madiraṃ madhu tatra śravāṃsi kṛṇvate

**4.65**

tyamu vo aprahaṇaṃ gṛṇīṣe śavasaspatim . indraṃ viśvāsāhaṃ naraṃ śaciṣṭhaṃ viśvavedasam

**4.66**

dadhikrāvṇo akāriṣaṃ jiṣṇoraśvasya vājinaḥ . surabhi no mukhā karatpra na āyūṃṣi tāriṣat

**4.67**

purāṃ bhinduryuvā kaviramitaujā ajāyata . indro viśvasya karmaṇo dharttā vajrī puruṣṭutaḥ

**4.68**

dhiyā vo medhasātaye purandhyā vivāsati

**4.69**

kaśyapasya svarvido yāvāhuḥ sayujāviti . yayorviśvamapi vrataṃ yajñaṃ dhīrā nicāyya

**4.70**

arcata prārcatā naraḥ priyamedhāso arcata . arcantu putrakā uta puramiddhṛṣṇvarcata

**4.71**

ukthamindrāya śaṃsyaṃ vardhanaṃ puruniḥṣidhe . śakro yathā suteṣu no rāraṇatsakhyeṣu ca

**4.72**

viśvānarasya vaspatimanānatasya śavasaḥ . evaiśca carṣaṇīnāmūtī huve rathānām

**4.73**

sa ghā yaste divo naro dhiyā martasya śamataḥ . ūtī sa bṛhato divo dviṣo aṃho na tarati

**4.74**

vibhoṣṭa indra rādhaso vibhvī rātiḥ śatakrato . athā no viśvacarṣaṇe dyumnaṃ sudatra maṃhaya

**4.75**

vayaścitte patatriṇo dvipāccatuṣpādarjuni . uṣaḥ prārannṛtūṃranu divo antebhyaspari

**4.76**

amī ye devā sthana madhya ā rocane divaḥ . kadva ṛtaṃ kadamṛtaṃ kā pratnā va āhutiḥ

**4.77**

ṛcaṃ sāma yajāmahe yābhyāṃ karmāṇi kṛṇvate . vi te sadasi rājato yajñaṃ deveṣu vakṣataḥ

**4.78**

kratve vare sthemanyāmurīmutogramojiṣṭhaṃ tarasaṃ tarasvinam

**4.79**

śratte dadhāmi prathamāya manyave 'hanyaddasyuṃ naryaṃ viverapaḥ . ubhe yatvā rodasī dhāvatāmanu bhyasātte śuṣmātpṛthivī cidadrivaḥ

**4.80**

sameta viśvā ojasā patiṃ divo ya eka idbhūratithirjanānām sa pūrvyo nūtanamājigīṣaṃ taṃ varttanīranu vāvṛta eka it

**4.81**

ime ta indra te vayaṃ puruṣṭuta ye tvārabhya carāmasi prabhūvaso . na hi tvadanyo girvaṇo giraḥ saghatkṣoṇīriva prati taddharya no vacaḥ

**4.82**

carṣaṇīdhṛtaṃ maghavānamukthyā3mindraṃ giro bṛhatīrabhyanūṣata . vāvṛdhānaṃ puruhūtaṃ suvṛktibhiramartyaṃ jaramāṇaṃ divedive

**4.83**

acchā va indraṃ matayaḥ svaryuvaḥ sadhrīcīrviśvā uśatīranūṣata . pari ṣvajanta janayo yathā patiṃ maryaṃ na śundhyuṃ maghavānamūtaye

**4.84**

abhi tyaṃ meṣaṃ puruhūtamṛgmiyamindraṃ gīrbhirmadatā vasvo arṇavam . yasya dyāvo na vicaranti mānuṣaṃ bhuje maṃhiṣṭhamabhi vipramarcata

**4.85**

tyaṃ su meṣaṃ mahayā svarvidaṃ śataṃ yasya subhuvaḥ sākamīrate . atyaṃ na vājaṃ havanasyadaṃ rathamendraṃ vavṛtyāmavase suvṛktibhiḥ

**4.86**

ghṛtavatī bhuvanānāmabhiśriyorvī pṛthvī madhudughe supeśasā . dyāvāpṛthivī varuṇasya dharmaṇā viṣkabhite ajare bhūriretasā

**4.87**

ubhe yadindra rodasī āpaprāthoṣā iva . mahāntaṃ tvā mahīnāṃ samrājaṃ carṣaṇīnām . devī janitryajījanadbhadrā janitryajījanat

**4.88**

pra mandine pitumadarcatā vaco yaḥ kṛṣṇagarbhā nirahannṛjiśvanā . avasyavo vṛṣaṇaṃ vajradakṣiṇaṃ marutvantaṃ sakhyāya huvemahi

**4.89**

vide vṛdhasya dakṣasya mahāṃ hi ṣaḥ

**4.90**

tamu abhi pra gāyata puruhūtaṃ puruṣṭutam . indraṃ gīrbhistaviṣamā vivāsata

**4.91**

taṃ te madaṃ gṛṇīmasi vṛṣaṇaṃ pṛkṣu sāsahim . u lokakṛtnumadrivo hariśriyam

**4.92**

yatsomamindra viṣṇavi yadvā gha trita āptye . yadvā marutsu mandase samindubhiḥ

**4.93**

edu madhormadintaraṃ siñcādhvaryo andhasaḥ . evā hi vīrastavate sadāvṛdhaḥ

**4.94**

endumindrāya siñcata pibāti somyaṃ madhu . pra rādhāṃsi codayate mahitvanā

**4.95**

eto nvindraṃ stavāma sakhāyaḥ stomyaṃ naram . kṛṣṭīryo viśvā abhyastyeka it

**4.96**

indrāya sāma gāyata viprāya bṛhate bṛhat . brahmakṛte vipaścite panasyave

**4.97**

ya eka idvidayate vasu martāya dāśuṣe . īśāno apratiṣkuta indro aṅga

**4.98**

sakhāya ā śiṣāmahe brahmendrāya vajriṇe . stuṣa ū ṣu vo nṛtamāya dhṛṣṇave
