**1.1**

agna ā yāhi vītaye gṛṇāno havyadātaye . ni hotā satsi barhiṣi tvamagne yajñānāṃ hotā viśveṣāṃ hitaḥ .

**1.2**

devebhirmānuṣe jane

**1.3**

agniṃ dūtaṃ vṛṇīmahe hotāraṃ viśvavedasam . asya yajñasya sukratum

**1.4**

agnirvṛtrāṇi jaṅghanaddraviṇasyurvipanyayā . samiddhaḥ śukra āhutaḥ

**1.5**

preṣṭhaṃ vo atithiṃ stuṣe mitramiva priyam . agne rathaṃ na vedyam

**1.6**

tvaṃ no agne mahobhiḥ pāhi viśvasyā arāteḥ . uta dviṣo martyasya

**1.7**

ehyū ṣu bravāṇi te 'gna itthetarā giraḥ . ebhirvardhāsa indubhiḥ

**1.8**

ā te vatso mano yamatparamāccitsadhasthāt . agne tvāṃ kāmaye girā

**1.9**

tvāmagne puṣkarādadhyatharvā niramanthata . mūrdhno viśvasya vāghataḥ

**1.10**

agne vivasvadā bharāsmabhyamūtaye mahe . devo hyasi no dṛśe

**1.11**

amairamitramardaya

**1.12**

dūtaṃ vo viśvavedasaṃ havyavāhamamartyam . yajiṣṭhamṛñjase girā

**1.13**

upa tvā jāmayo giro dediśatīrhaviṣkṛtaḥ . vāyoranīke asthiran

**1.14**

upa tvāgne divedive doṣāvastardhiyā vayam . namo bharanta emasi

**1.15**

jarābodha tadviviḍḍhi viśeviśe yajñiyāya . stomaṃ rudrāya dṛśīkam

**1.16**

prati tyaṃ cārumadhvaraṃ gopīthāya pra hūyase . marudbhiragna ā gahi

**1.17**

aśvaṃ na tvā vāravantaṃ vandadhyā agniṃ namobhiḥ . samrājantamadhvarāṇām

**1.18**

aurvabhṛguvacchucimapnavānavadā huve . agniṃ samudravāsasam

**1.19**

agnimindhāno manasā dhiyaṃ saceta martyaḥ . agnimindhe vivasvabhiḥ

**1.20**

āditpratnasya retaso jyotiḥ paśyanti vāsaram . paro yadidhyate divi

**1.21**

acchā naptre sahasvate

**1.22**

agnistigmena śociṣā yaṃsadviśvaṃ nyā3triṇam . agnirno vaṃsate rayim

**1.23**

agne mṛḍa mahāṃ asyaya ā devayuṃ janam . iyetha barhirāsadam

**1.24**

agne rakṣā ṇo aṃhasaḥ prati sma deva rīṣataḥ . tapiṣṭhairajaro daha

**1.25**

agne yuṅkṣvā hi ye tavāśvāso deva sādhavaḥ . araṃ vahantyāśavaḥ

**1.26**

ni tvā nakṣya viśpate dyumantaṃ dhīmahe vayam . suvīramagna āhuta

**1.27**

agnirmūrdhā divaḥ kakutpatiḥ pṛthivyā ayam . apāṃ retāṃsi jinvati

**1.28**

imamū ṣu tvamasmākaṃ saniṃ gāyatraṃ navyāṃsam . agne deveṣu pra vocaḥ

**1.29**

taṃ tvā gopavano girā janiṣṭhadagne aṅgaraḥ . sa pāvaka śrudhī havam

**1.30**

pari vājapatiḥ kaviragnirhavyānyakramīt . dadhadratnāni dāśuṣe

**1.31**

udu tyaṃ jātavedasaṃ devaṃ vahanti ketavaḥ . dṛśe viśvāya sūryam

**1.32**

kavimagnimupa stuhi satyadharmāṇamadhvare . devamamīvacātanam

**1.33**

śaṃ no devīrabhiṣṭaye śaṃ no bhavantu pītaye . śaṃ yorabhi sravantu naḥ

**1.34**

kasya nūnaṃ parīṇasi dhiyo jinvasi satpate . joṣātā yasya te giraḥ

**1.35**

prapra vayamamṛtaṃ jātavedasaṃ priyaṃ mitraṃ na śaṃsiṣam

**1.36**

pāhi no agna ekayā pāhyū3ta dvitīyayā . pāhi gīrbhistisṛbhirūrjāṃ pate pāhi catasṛbhirvaso

**1.37**

bṛhadbhiragne arcibhiḥ śukreṇa deva śociṣā . bharadvāje samidhāno yaviṣṭhya revatpāvaka dīdihi

**1.38**

tve agne svāhuta priyāsaḥ santu sūrayaḥ . yantāro ye maghavāno janānāmūrvaṃ dayanta gonām

**1.39**

agne jaritarviśpatistapāno deva rakṣasaḥ . aproṣivāngṛhapate mahāṃ asi divaspāyurduroṇayuḥ

**1.40**

agne vivasvaduṣasaścitraṃ rādho amartya . ā dāśuṣe jātavedo vahā tvamadyā devāṃ uṣarbudhaḥ

**1.41**

tvaṃ naścitra ūtyā vaso rādhāṃsi codaya . asya rāyastvamagne rathīrasi vidā gādhaṃ tuce tu naḥ

**1.42**

tvamitsaprathā asyagne trātarṛtaḥ kaviḥ . tvāṃ viprāsaḥ samidhāna dīdiva ā vivāsanti vedhasaḥ

**1.43**

ā no agne vayovṛdhaṃ rayiṃ pāvaka śaṃsyam . rāsvā ca na upamāte puruspṛhaṃ sunītī suyaśastaram

**1.44**

yo viśvā dayate vasu hotā mandro janānām . madhorna pātrā prathamānyasmai pra stomā yantvagnaye

**1.45**

priyaṃ cetiṣṭhamaratiṃ svādhvaraṃ viśvasya dūtamamṛtam

**1.46**

śeṣe vaneṣu mātṛṣu saṃ tvā martāsa indhate . atandro havyaṃ vahasi haviṣkṛta ādiddeveṣu rājasi

**1.47**

adarśi gātuvittamo yasminvratāya ādadhuḥ . upo ṣu jātamāryasya vardhanamagniṃ nakṣantu no giraḥ

**1.48**

agnirukthe purohito grāvāṇo barhiradhvare . ṛcā yāmi maruto brahmaṇaspate devā avo vareṇyam

**1.49**

agnimīḍiṣvāvase gāthābhiḥ śīraśociṣam . agniṃ rāye purumīḍha śrutaṃ naro 'gniḥ sudītaye chardiḥ

**1.50**

śrudhi śrutkarṇa vahnibhirdevairagne sayāvabhiḥ . ā sīdatu barhiṣi mitro aryamā prātaryāvabhiradhvare

**1.51**

pra daivodāso agnirdeva indro na majmanā . anu mātaraṃ pṛthivīṃ vi vāvṛte tasthau nākasya śarmaṇi

**1.52**

adha jmo adha vā divo bṛhato rocanādadhi . ayā vardhasva tanvā girā mamā jātā sukrato pṛṇa

**1.53**

kāyamāno vanā tvaṃ yanmātR^īrajagannapaḥ . na tatte agne pramṛṣe nivartanaṃ yaddūre sannihābhuvaḥ

**1.54**

ni tvāmagne manurdadhe jyotirjanāya śaśvate . dīdetha kaṇva ṛtajāta ukṣito yaṃ namasyanti kṛṣṭayaḥ

**1.55**

devo vo draviṇodāḥ pūrṇāṃ vivaṣṭvāsicam . udvā siñjadhvamupa vā pṛṇadhvamādidvo deva ohate

**1.56**

praitu brahmaṇaspatiḥ pra devyetu sūnṛtā . acchā vīraṃ naryaṃ paṅktirādhasaṃ devā yajñaṃ nayantu naḥ

**1.57**

ūrdhva ū ṣu ṇa ūtaye tiṣṭhā devo na savitā . ūrdhvo vājasya sanitā yadañjibhirvāghadbhirvihvayāmahe

**1.58**

pra yo rāye ninīṣati marto yaste vaso dāśat . sa vīraṃ dhatte agna ukthaśaṃsinaṃ tmanā sahasrapoṣiṇam

**1.59**

pra vo yahvaṃ purūṇāṃ viśāṃ devayatīnām . agniṃ sūktebhirvacobhirvṛṇīmahe yaṃsamidanya indhate

**1.60**

ayamagniḥ suvīryasyeśe hi saubhagasya . rāya īśe svapatyasya gomata īśe vṛtrahathānām

**1.61**

tvamagne gṛhapatistvaṃ hotā no adhvare . tvaṃ potā viśvavāra pracetā tākṣi yāsi ca vāryam

**1.62**

sakhāyastvā vavṛmahe devaṃ martāsa ūtaye . apāṃ napātaṃ subhagaṃ sudaṃsasaṃ supratūrtimanehasam

**1.63**

iḍaspade namasā rātahavyaṃ saparyatā yajataṃ pastyānām

**1.64**

citra icchiśostaruṇasya vakṣatho na yo mātarāvanveti dhātave . anūdhā yadajījanadadhā cidā vavakṣatsadyo mahi dūtyā3ṃ caran

**1.65**

idaṃ ta ekaṃ para ū ta ekaṃ tṛtīyena jyotiṣā saṃ viśasva . saṃveśanastanve3 cāruredhi priyo devānāṃ parame janitre

**1.66**

imaṃ stomamarhate jātavedase rathamiva saṃ mahemā manīṣayā . bhadrā hi naḥ pramatirasya saṃsadyagne sakhye mā riṣāmā vayaṃ tava

**1.67**

mūrdhānaṃ divo aratiṃ pṛthivyā vaiśvānaramṛta ā jātamagnim . kaviṃ samrājamatithiṃ janānāmāsannāḥ pātraṃ janayanta devāḥ

**1.68**

vi tvadāpo na parvatasya pṛṣṭhādukthebhiragne janayanta devāḥ . taṃ tvā giraḥ suṣṭutayo vājayantyājiṃ na girvavāho jigyuraśvāḥ

**1.69**

ā vo rājānamadhvarasya rudraṃ hotāraṃ satyayajaṃ rodasyoḥ . agniṃ purā tanayitnoracittāddhiraṇyarūpamavase kṛṇudhvam

**1.70**

indhe rājā samaryo namobhiryasya pratīkamāhutaṃ ghṛtena . naro havyebhirīḍate sabādha āgniragramuṣasāmaśoci

**1.71**

pra ketunā bṛhatā yātyagnirā rodasī vṛṣabho roravīti . divaścidantādupamāmudānaḍapāmupasthe mahiṣo vavardha

**1.72**

agniṃ naro dīdhitibhiraṇyornihito janayanta praśastam . dūredṛśaṃ gṛhapatimathavyum

**1.73**

**1.74**

**1.75**

śukraṃ te anyadyajataṃ te anyadviṣurūpe ahanī dyaurivāsi . viśvā hi māyā avasi svadhāvanbhadrā te pūṣanniha rātirastu

**1.76**

iḍāmagne purudaṃsaṃ saniṃ goḥ śaśvattamaṃ havamānāya sādha . syānnaḥ sūnustanayo vijāvāgne sā te sumatirbhūtvasme

**1.77**

pra hotā jāto mahānnabhovinnṛṣadmā sīdadapāṃ vivarte . dadhadyo dhāyī sute vayāṃsi yantā vasūni vidhate tanūpāḥ

**1.78**

pra samrājamasurasya praśstaṃ puṃsaḥ kṛṣṭīnāmanumādyasya . indrasyeva pra tavasaskṛtāni vandadvārā vandamānā vivaṣṭu

**1.79**

araṇyornihito jātavedā garbha ivetsubhṛto garbhiṇībhiḥ . divediva īḍyo jāgṛvadbhirhaviṣmadbhirmanuṣyebhiragniḥ

**1.80**

sanādagne mṛṇasi yātudhānānna tvā rakṣāṃsi pṛtanāsu jigyuḥ . anu daha sahamūrānkayādo mā te hetyā mukṣata daivyāyāḥ
