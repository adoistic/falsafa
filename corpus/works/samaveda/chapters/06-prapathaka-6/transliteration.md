**6.1**

upo ṣu jātamapturaṃ gobhirbhaṅgaṃ pariṣkṛtam . induṃ devā ayāsiṣuḥ

**6.2**

punāno akramīdabhi viśvā mṛdho vicarṣaṇiḥ . śumbhanti vipraṃ dhītibhiḥ

**6.3**

āviśankalaśaṃ suto viśvā arṣannabhi śriyaḥ . indurindrāya dhīyate

**6.4**

asarji rathyo yathā pavitre camvoḥ sutaḥ . kārṣmanvājī nyakramīt

**6.5**

pra yadgāvo na bhūrṇayastveṣā ayāso akramuḥ . ghnantaḥ kṛṣṇāmapa tvacam

**6.6**

apaghnanpavase mṛdhaḥ kratuvitsoma matsaraḥ . nudasvādevayuṃ janam

**6.7**

ayā pavasva dhārayā yayā sūryamarocayaḥ . hinvāno mānuṣīrapaḥ

**6.8**

sa pavasva ya āvithendraṃ vṛtrāya hantave . vavrivāṃsaṃ mahīrapaḥ

**6.9**

ayā vītī pari srava yasta indo madeṣvā . avāhannavatīrnava

**6.10**

pari dyukṣaṃ sanadrāyiṃ bharadvājaṃ no andhasā . svāno arṣa pavitra ā

**6.11**

saṃ sūryeṇa didyute

**6.12**

ā te dakṣaṃ mayobhuvaṃ vahnimadyā vṛṇīmahe . pāntamā puruspṛham

**6.13**

adhvaryo adribhiḥ sutaṃ somaṃ pavitra ā naya . punīhīndrāya pātave

**6.14**

taratsa mandī dhāvati dhārā sutasyāndhasaḥ . taratsa mandī dhāvati

**6.15**

ā pavasva sahasriṇaṃ rayiṃ soma suvīryam . asme śravāṃsi dhāraya

**6.16**

anu pratnāsa āyavaḥ padaṃ navīyo akramuḥ . ruce jananta sūryam

**6.17**

arṣā soma dyumattamo 'bhi droṇāni roruvat . sīdanyonau yoneṣvā

**6.18**

vṛṣā soma dyumāṃ asi vṛṣā deva vṛṣavrataḥ . vṛṣā dharmāṇi dadhriṣe

**6.19**

iṣe pavasva dhārayā mṛjyamāno manīṣibhiḥ . indo rucābhi gā ihi

**6.20**

mandrayā soma dhārayā vṛṣā pavasva devayuḥ . avyo vārebhirasmayuḥ

**6.21**

ayā soma sukṛtyapā mahāntsannabhyavardhathāḥ . hindāna idvṛṣāyase

**6.22**

ayaṃ vicarṣaṇirhitaḥ pavamānaḥ sa cetati . hinvāna āpyaṃ bṛhat

**6.23**

pra ṇa indo mahe tu na ūrmiṃ na bibhradarṣasi . abhi devāṃ ayāsyaḥ

**6.24**

apaghnanpavate mṛdho 'pa somo arāvṇaḥ . gacchannindrasya niṣkṛtam

**6.25**

ā ratnadhā yonimṛtasya sīdasyutso devo hiraṇyayaḥ

**6.26**

parīto ṣiñcatā sutaṃ somo ya uttamaṃ haviḥ . dadhanvāṃ yo naryo apsvā3ntarā suṣāva somamadribhiḥ

**6.27**

ā soma svāno adribhistiro vārāṇyavyayā . jano na puri camvorviśaddhariḥ sado vaneṣu dadhriṣe

**6.28**

pra soma devavītaye sindhurna pipye arṇasā . aṃśoḥ payasā madiro na jāgṛviracchā kośaṃ madhuścutam

**6.29**

soma u ṣvāṇaḥ sotṛbhiradhi ṣṇubhiravīnām . aśvayeva haritā yāti dhārayā mandrayā yāti dhārayā

**6.30**

tavāhaṃ soma rāraṇa sakhya indo divedive . purūṇi babhro ni caranti māmava paridhīṃrati tāṃ ihi

**6.31**

mṛjyamānaḥ suhastyā samudre vācaminvasi . rayiṃ piśaṅgaṃ bahulaṃ puruspṛhaṃ pavamānābhyarṣasi

**6.32**

abhi somāsa āyavaḥ pavante madyaṃ madam . samudrasyādhi viṣṭape manīṣiṇo matsarāso madacyutaḥ

**6.33**

punānaḥ soma jāgṛviravyā vāraiḥ pari priyaḥ . tvaṃ vipro abhavo 'ṅgirastama madhvā yajñaṃ mimikṣa ṇaḥ

**6.34**

indrāya pavate madaḥ somo marutvate sutaḥ . sahasradhāro atyavyamarṣati tamī mṛjantyāyavaḥ

**6.35**

pavasva vājasātamo 'bhi viśvāni vāryā . tvaṃ samudraḥ prathame vidharmaṃ devebhyaṃ soma matsaraḥ

**6.36**

pavamānā asṛkṣata pavitramati dhārayā . marutvanto matsarā indriyā hayā medhāmabhi prayāṃsi ca

**6.37**

aśvaṃ na tvā vājinaṃ marjayanto 'cchā barhī raśanābhirnayanti

**6.38**

pra kāvyamuśaneva bruvāṇo devo devānāṃ janimā vivakti . mahivrataḥ śucibandhuḥ pāvakaḥ padā varāho abhyeti rebhan

**6.39**

tisro vāca īrayati pra vahnirṛtasya dhītiṃ brahmaṇo manīṣām . gāvo yanti gopatiṃ pṛcchamānāḥ somaṃ yanti matayo vāvaśānāḥ

**6.40**

asya preṣā hemanā pūyamāno devo devebhiḥ samapṛkta rasam . sutaḥ pavitraṃ paryeti rebhanmiteva sadma paśumanti hotā

**6.41**

somaḥ pavate janitā matīnāṃ janitā divo janitā pṛthivyāḥ . janitāgnerjanitā sūryasya janitendrasya janitota viṣṇoḥ

**6.42**

abhi tripṛṣṭhaṃ vṛṣaṇaṃ vayodhāmāṅgoṣiṇamavāvaśanta vāṇīḥ . vanā vasāno varuṇo na sindhūrvi ratnadhā dayate vāryāṇi

**6.43**

akrāntsamudraḥ prathame vidharmaṃ janayanprajā bhuvanasya gopāḥ . vṛṣā pavitre adhi sāno avye bṛhatsomo vāvṛdhe svāno adriḥ

**6.44**

kanikranti harirā sṛjyamānaḥ sīdanvanasya jaṭhare punānaḥ . nṛbhiryataḥ kṛṇute nirṇijaṃ gāmato matiṃ janayata svadhābhiḥ

**6.45**

eṣa sya te madhumāṃ indra somo vṛṣā vṛṣṇaḥ pari pavitre akṣāḥ

**6.46**

pavasva soma madhumāṃ ṛtāvāpo vasāno adhi sāno avye . ava droṇāni ghṛtavanti roha madintamo matsara indrapānaḥ

**6.47**

bhadrānkṛṇvannindrahavāntsakhibhya ā somo vastrā rabhasāni datte

**6.48**

pra te dhārā madhumatīrasṛgranvāraṃ yatpūto atyeṣyavyam . pavamāna pavase dhāma gonāṃ janayantsūryamapinvo arkaiḥ

**6.49**

pra gāyatābhyarcāma devāntsomaṃ hinota mahate dhanāya . svāduḥ pavatāmati vāramavyamā sīdatu kalaśaṃ deva induḥ

**6.50**

pra hinvāno janitā rodasyo ratho na vājaṃ saniṣannayāsīt . indraṃ gacchannāyudhā saṃśiśāno viśvā vasu hastayorādadhānaḥ

**6.51**

takṣadyadī manaso venato vāgjyeṣṭhasya dharmaṃ dyukṣoranīke . ādīmāyanvaramā vāvaśānā juṣṭaṃ patiṃ kalaśe gāva indum

**6.52**

sākamukṣo marjayanta svasāro daśa dhīrasya dhītayo dhanutrīḥ . hariḥ paryadravajjāḥ sūryasya droṇaṃ nanakṣe atyo na vājī

**6.53**

adhi yadasminvājinīva śubhaḥ spardhante dhiyaḥ sūre na viśaḥ . apo vṛṇānaḥ pavate kavīyanvrajaṃ na paśuvardhanāya manma

**6.54**

indurvājī pavate gonyoghā indre somaḥ saha invanmadāya . hanti rakṣo bādhate paryarātiṃ varivaskṛṇvanvṛjanasya rājā

**6.55**

ayā pavā pavasvainā vasūni māṃścatva indro sarasi pra dhanva . braghnaścidyasya vāto na jūtiṃ purumedhāścittakave naraṃ dhāt

**6.56**

mahattatsomo mahiṣaścakārāpāṃ yadgarbho 'vṛṇīta devān . adadhādindre pavamāna ojo 'janayatsūrye jyotirinduḥ

**6.57**

asarji vakvā rathyo yathājau dhiyā manotā prathamā manīṣa . daśa svasāro adhi sāno avye mṛjanti vahniṃ sadaneṣvaccha

**6.58**

apāmivedūrmayastartturāṇāḥ pra manīṣā īrate somamaccha . namasyantīrupa ca yanti saṃ cāca viśantyuśatīruśantam

**6.59**

purojitī vo andhasaḥ sutāya mādayitnave . apa śvānaṃ śnathiṣṭana sakhāyo dīrghajihvyam

**6.60**

ayaṃ pūṣā rayirbhagaḥ somaḥ punāno arṣati . patirviśvasya bhūmano vyakhyadrodasī ubhe

**6.61**

sutāso madhumattamāḥ somā indrāya mandinaḥ . pavitravanto akṣarandevāngacchantu vo madāḥ

**6.62**

somāḥ pavanta indavo 'smabhyaṃ gātuvittamāḥ . mitrāḥ svānā arepasaḥ svādhyaḥ svarvidaḥ

**6.63**

abhī no vājasātamaṃ rayimarṣa śataspṛham . indo sahasrabharṇasaṃ tuvidyumnaṃ vibhāsaham

**6.64**

abhī navante adruhaḥ priyamindrasya kāmyam . vatsaṃ na pūrva āyuni jātaṃ rihanti mātaraḥ

**6.65**

ā haryatāya dhṛṣṇave dhanuṣṭanvanti pauṃsyam . śukrā vi yantyasurāya nirṇije vipāmagre mahīyuvaḥ

**6.66**

pari tyaṃ haryataṃ hariṃ babhruṃ punanti vāreṇa . yo devānviśvāṃ itpari madena saha gacchati

**6.67**

pra sunvānāsyāndhaso marto na vaṣṭa tadvacaḥ . apa śvānamarādhasaṃ hatā makhaṃ na bhṛgavaḥ

**6.68**

ā sūryasya bṛhato bṛhannadhi rathaṃ viṣvañcamaruhadvicakṣaṇaḥ

**6.69**

acodaso no dhanvantvindavaḥ pra svānāso bṛhaddeveṣu harayaḥ . vi cidaśnānā iṣayo arātayo 'ryo naḥ santu saniṣantu no dhiyaḥ

**6.70**

eṣa pra kośe madhumāṃ acikradadindrasya vajro vapuṣo vapuṣṭamaḥ . abhyR^ī3tasya sudughā ghṛtaścuto vāśrā arṣanti payasā ca ghenavaḥ

**6.71**

pro ayāsīdindurindrasya niṣkṛtaṃ sakhā sakhyurna pra mināti saṅgiram . marya iva yuvatibhiḥ samarṣati somaḥ kalaśe śatayāmanā pathā

**6.72**

dhartā divaḥ pavate kṛtvyo raso dakṣo devānāmanumādyo nṛbhiḥ . hariḥ sṛjāno atyo na satvabhirvṛthā pājāṃsi kṛṇuṣe nadīṣvā

**6.73**

vṛṣā matīnāṃ pavate vicakṣaṇaḥ somo ahnāṃ pratarītoṣasāṃ divaḥ . prāṇā sindhūnāṃ kalaśāṃ acikradadindrasya hārdyāviśanmanīṣibhiḥ

**6.74**

trirasmai sapta dhenavo duduhrire satyāmāśiraṃ parame vyomani . catvāryanyā bhuvanāni nirṇije cārūṇi cakre yadṛtairavardhata

**6.75**

indrāya soma suṣutaḥ pari sravāpāmīvā bhavatu rakṣasā saha . mā te rasasya matsata dvayāvino draviṇasvanta iha santvindavaḥ

**6.76**

asāvi somo aruṣo vṛṣā harī rājeva dasmo abhi gā acikradat . punāno vāramatyeṣyavyayaṃ śyeno na yoniṃ ghṛtavantamāsadat

**6.77**

pra devamacchā madhumanta indavo 'siṣyadanta gāva ā na dhenavaḥ . barhiṣado vacanāvanta ūdhabhiḥ parisrutamusriyā nirṇijaṃ dhire

**6.78**

añjate vyañjate samañjate kratuṃ rihanti maghvābhyañjate . sindhoru 'cchvāse patayantamukṣaṇaṃ hiraṇyapāvāḥ paśumapsu gṛbhṇate

**6.79**

pavitraṃ te vitataṃ brahmaṇaspate prabhurgātrāṇi paryeṣi viśvataḥ . ataptatanūrna tadāmo aśnute śṛtāsa idvahantaḥ saṃ tadāśata

**6.80**

śruṣṭe jātāsa indavaḥ svarvidaḥ

**6.81**

pra dhanvā soma jāgṛvirindrāyendo pari srava . dyumantaṃ śuṣmamā bhara svarvidam

**6.82**
