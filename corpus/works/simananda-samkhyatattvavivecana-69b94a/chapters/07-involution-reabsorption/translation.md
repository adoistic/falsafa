**Tats_6**

[Base sūtra:] {{saṃcara / pratisaṃcara|Involution}} (pratisaṃcara).

**Tats_6.c1**

[Verse:] In just the reverse order they dissolve: the pentad of [gross] elements [dissolves] into the subtle elements; the subtle elements and the organs dissolve into the I-maker;

**Tats_6.c2**

[Verse:] then the I-maker [dissolves] into the intellect, and the intellect into that named the unmanifest; [but] the unmanifest is dissolved into nothing. This is remembered as "involution" (pratisaṃcara).
