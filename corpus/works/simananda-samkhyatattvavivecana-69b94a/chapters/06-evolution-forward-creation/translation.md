**Tats_5**

[Base sūtra:] {{saṃcara / pratisaṃcara|Evolution}} (saṃcara).

**Tats_5.c1**

[Verse:] That state of equilibrium of the constituents which is prakṛti—from it, by its own nature, through agitation by Time, comes disequilibrium; of old, in the field joined to the Supreme,

**Tats_5.c2**

[Verse:] [first] intellect [arose], and then the I-maker, threefold, was born; [then] the subtle elements, the organs, and the great elements—in [due] sequence.

**Tats_5.c3**

[Verse:] Origination just in this sequence is proclaimed [to be] "evolution" (saṃcara).
