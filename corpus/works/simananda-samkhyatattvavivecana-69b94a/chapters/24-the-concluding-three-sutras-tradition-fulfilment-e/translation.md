**Tats_23**

[Base sūtra:] This [is] the truth [received] through [unbroken] tradition (paramparā).

**Tats_24**

[Base sūtra:] Having known all this, one becomes one whose task is accomplished (kṛtakṛtya).

**Tats_25**

[Base sūtra:] [And] one is no longer overcome by the threefold suffering. [These three sūtras carry no separate gloss; their substance is unfolded in the recapitulation that follows.]
