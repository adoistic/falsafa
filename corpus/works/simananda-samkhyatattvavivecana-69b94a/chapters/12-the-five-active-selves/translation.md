**Tats_11**

[Base sūtra:] The five "action-selves" (karmātmans).

**Tats_11.c1**

[Verse:] The [five], beginning with the modificatory (vaikārika), have been set forth before in due order. [The permutations are:] the auspicious, the inauspicious-and-deluded, the auspicious-deluded, and the inauspicious-deluded—[these] in due order are the agents, [each] correlated with its respective deed.
