**Tats_17**

[Base sūtra:] The gracious creation (anugraha-sarga).

**Tats_17.c1**

[Verse:] Brahman created the gracious [creation] apart from the form-subtle-element, having produced [it] through the contemplation of grace, by abandoning the prior substrate.
