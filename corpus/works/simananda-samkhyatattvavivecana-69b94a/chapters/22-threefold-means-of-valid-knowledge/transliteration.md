**Tats_21**

trividhaṃ pramāṇam ||

**Tats_21.c1**

dṛṣṭamanumānamāptavacanaṃ caitat pramāṇakam / pañcendriyāṇi pratyakṣaṃ vṛṣṭirmeghodayena tu //

**Tats_21.c2**

anumānamindro devānāṃ rājāśabdaḥ pramāpakaḥ //

**Tats_21.c3**

āgame 'pi, svakarmabhirvinirmukto rāgadveṣavivarjitaḥ // jñānavān śīlasampannaḥ so 'pi jñeyaśca tādṛśaḥ //
