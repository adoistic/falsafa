SONG.

SPRING returns, the flowrets blow;
 Will hope return? ah, no! ah, no!
 With the dreams of youth she flies,
 And like the rose, her emblem, dies. 
Fancy droops beneath the shade,
 And all the gay delights are fled. 
Spring returns, the flowrets blow;
 Will hope return? ah, no! ah, no!
