When the king asked him if he would become his friend provided he spared his life, Pomponius answered: Yes, indeed, if you come to terms with the Romans; otherwise I must remain your enemy. Mithridates was struck with admiration for him, and did him no harm.
> [p-12bb07] Lucullus was now afraid of the plains, since the enemy was superior in cavalry, and yet hesitated to go forward into the hill country, which was remote, woody, and impassable. But it chanced that certain Greeks, who had taken refuge in a sort of cave, were captured, and the elder of them, Artemidorus, promised to serve Lucullus as a guide, and set him in a place which was safe for his camp, and which had a fortress overlooking Cabira.
