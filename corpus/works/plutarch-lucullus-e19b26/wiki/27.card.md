## Closes
> [p-a653d9] As Lucullus was about to cross the river, some of his officers advised him to beware of the day, which was one of the unlucky days—the Romans call them black days. For on that day Caepio and his army perished in a battle with the Cimbri. But Lucullus answered with the memorable words: Verily, I will make this day, too, a lucky one for the Romans. Now the day was the sixth of October.
