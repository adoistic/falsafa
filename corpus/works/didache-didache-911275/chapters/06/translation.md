See that no one make thee to err from this Way of the teaching, for he teaches thee without God.

For if thou canst bear the whole yoke of the Lord, thou wilt be perfect, but if thou canst not, do what thou canst.

And concerning food, bear what thou canst, but keep strictly from that which is offered to idols, for it is the worship of dead gods.
