## Closes
> [p-6ec724] For it would be outrageous if those covenants should be held valid for the exculpation of the evil-doers, but should be made invalid for us, your benefactors! And it is prudent for you to guard well your present fortune, remembering that while in the past such agreements have increased civic discord in other cities, yet to ours they have brought a greater degree of concord. So you, keeping these considerations in mind, should cast your votes for that which is at the same time just and also expedient.
