To Novella, on her saying deridingly, that a Lady of great Merit, and fine Address, was bred in the Old Way.

An EPIGRAM.

You cry, She's bred in the Old Way;
 Then into Laughter fall:
 Were she as just to you, she'd say,
 You are not bred at all.
