# Caryāmelāpakapradīpa

Caryāmelāpakapradīpa, a Sanskrit philosophical text by Āryadeva, with transliteration and Thothica's English translation from the GRETIL source text. The source identifies this as the tantric Āryadeva of the Ārya school of the Guhyasamāja, not the early Madhyamaka Āryadeva of the Catuḥśataka.

Citation scheme: pariccheda (chapter) as primary canonical unit: CMP <pariccheda>; positional verse/passage index within a chapter as CMP <pariccheda>.<seq> (sequence assigned by reading order, since the source provides no verse numbers).
