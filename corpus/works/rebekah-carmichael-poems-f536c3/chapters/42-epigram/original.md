EPIGRAM

A Friend of mine the other day,
 Who has more grace than pelf,
 When speaking of a sot, did pray,
 Lord take him to thyself. 

When turning quick, Eliza cries,
 Indeed 'tis mighty odd;
 A man you do yourself despise,
 You think is fit for God.
