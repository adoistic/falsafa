He therefore made an expedition into their territories and compelled Hicetas to forsake the cause of Carthage, and to agree to demolish his citadels and live as a private person in Leontini.

> [p-cbbe7e] Moreover, he wished that his mercenaries might get booty from the enemy’s country and not remain idle.
