**12**

avikalpasphuṭābhāsā sā sṛṣṭiḥ śāmbhavī matā | abhedenātmanirmāṇe svātmaiva karaṇīkṛtaḥ || 12 ||

**13**

kriyājñānātmacicchaktir bhedāsūtraṇakāraṇāt | kalāvidyāpadaṃ prāpya bhedodbhede punar bhavet || 13 ||

**14**

pramāṇabuddhiprakarā yatkriyāyā vikalpanāḥ | saiva vicchinnavicchinnakriyā jñānodyamā yadā || 14 ||

**15**

karmabuddhyakṣatāṃ prāptā tadāsau paśur ucyate | itīcchayā sa bhagavān bhairavaḥ parimīyate || 15 ||

**16**

mitavṛttis satī sā ca śaktis tam anuvartate | tasya vidyeśasādākhyaśivatās saṃkucatsthiteḥ | dehaprāṇabuddhiśūnyapramātṛtvam upāgaman || 16 ||

**17**

tasyāsādhāraṇī sṛṣṭis tattannānāvikalpanā | śaivasargotthasaṃskārasaṃskṛtasya tadāśrayā || 17 ||

**18**

pramātaivāyam etāvatprakāro 'vagrahotsave | avyagro vartate svātmany avikalpe cidātmake || 18 ||
