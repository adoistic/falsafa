**29**

antaḥkārabahiṣkārāv akālakramam ātmanaḥ | aparādhīnam ādhatse yat tvaṃ prābhavanirbharaḥ || 29 ||

**30**

anuttaravisargātmaviśramodayayāmalam | saṃghaṭṭaś śivaśaktyos tadantare cānyaśaktayaḥ || 30 ||

**31**

ānandeccheśanonmeṣā ūnatā ceśanecchayoḥ | asphuṭasphuṭabhedena vedyollāsas tayor vidaḥ || 31 ||

**32**

ānandānuttarābhyāṃ ca sandhībhāvo 'tha vedanā | vedyānāṃ prāṇabhūteti pūrṇaṃ tad bhairavaṃ vapuḥ || 32 ||

**33**

vedyollāso 'yam icchāyām īśane caikataḥ sthitaḥ | sthūlatvena pṛthivyādibhūtapañcakatām iyāt || 33 ||

**34**

sūkṣmatvena ca gandhādipañcatanmātratāṃ tataḥ | karaṇatvena saṃsparśād indriyāṇāṃ dvipañcatām || 34 ||

**35**

vedyatāyāḥ chādanena vettṛtodbhāsanakrame | mano 'haṃbuddhitāṃ eṣām avibhāge pradhānatām | puruṣatvaṃ ca saṃkocavikāsau yadapekṣayā || 35 ||

**36**

abhūvann iṣyasaṃyogād dvirūpād ubhayātmanaḥ | icchāsvarūpād unmeṣād anyathānuttarāyutāt || 36 ||

**37**

catasraś śaktyo 'ntaḥsthāḥ puṃso 'syāntaś ca dhārikāḥ | cidvyomno jaḍabhūmeś ca māyāmayyas triśaṅkuvat || 37 ||

**38**

icchāśaktes trirūpāyās tisro 'nyāś śaktyo 'bhavan | visargo 'yam athātyarthaṃ sphuṭo 'bhūt sṛjyavistarāt || 38 ||

**39**

etat sṛṣṭvātmaśaktīnām itaretaramiśrāt | bhavet kṣobhāntaraṃ nāsmād visargād bāhyato bhavet || 39 ||

**40**

iyatsarvaṃ sad icchādidvārānuttarabhairave | viśramya viśramya muhus tata eva visṛjyate || 40 ||
