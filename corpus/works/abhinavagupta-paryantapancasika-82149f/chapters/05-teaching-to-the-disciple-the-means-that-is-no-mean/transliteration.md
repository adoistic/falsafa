**19**

vatsa paśya tvam evaitat advayatvena saṃvidaḥ | viditaṃ tanmayaṃ viśvam anyat tuccham asad yataḥ || 19 ||

**20**

kim upāyam idaṃ tattvaṃ yato 'nyan naiva sambhavi | ananyopāyam etat tadātmopāyaṃ pracakṣate || 20 ||

**21**

saṃhṛtya sarvataś cintām aham ity anuśīlayan | avikalpāṃ svasaṃvittiṃ pratyakṣāṃ pratipadyate || 21 ||

**22**

anantaitāvadākārasvīkāre 'py ekalakṣaṇām | tāṃ svasaṃvidam āviśya vikalpān na vikalpayet || 22 ||

**23**

khecaryādicatussrotovāhapūrṇamahāhradām | vyomeśvarīm ātmacitiṃ vigāhya broḍayej jaḍam || 23 ||

**24**

sargasthāpanasaṃhāranigrahānugrahakramāt | pañcakṛtyāni kurvāṇaḥ śivo 'ham iti viśvaset || 24 ||

**25**

kṣepajñānaprasaṃkhyānagatinādakrameṇa vā | guruvaktreṇa tāny ātmany ākalayya kṛtī bhavet || 25 ||

**26**

vikalpayitvā viśvātmā bhairavo 'haṃ mamaiva tāḥ | viśvabhaṅgyo yathā vahner jvālābhaṅgyaḥ prabhā raveḥ || 26 ||

**27**

eko 'haṃ mātṛvijñānamānameyaprakāravān | śuddhabodhādirūpeṇa khecaryādicaturdaśaḥ || 27 ||

**28**

medāmedaprakāreṇa bandhamokṣau vibhāvayan | bhairavo 'smīti visrambhāt svācchandyaṃ paramaṃ vrajet || 28 ||
