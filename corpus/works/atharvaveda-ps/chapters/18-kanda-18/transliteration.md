**18.1.1**

satyenottabhitā bhūmiḥ sūryeṇottabhitā dyauḥ | ṛtenādityās tiṣṭhanti divi somo adhi śritaḥ ||

**18.1.2**

somenādityā balinaḥ somena pṛthivī mahī | ato nakṣatraṇām eṣām upasthe soma āhitaḥ ||

**18.1.3**

somaṃ manyate papivān yat saṃpiṃṣanty oṣadhim | somaṃ yaṃ braḥmāno vidur na tasyāśnāti pārthivaḥ ||

**18.1.4**

ācchadvidhānair gupito bārhataiḥ soma rakṣitaḥ | grāvṇām ic chṛṇvan tiṣṭhasi na te aśnāti pārthivaḥ ||

**18.1.5**

cittir vāvopabarhaṇaṃ cakṣur āvad abhyañjanam | dyaur bhūmiḥ kośa āsīd yad ayāt sūryā patim ||

**18.1.6**

raibhy āsīd anudeyī nārāśaṃsī nyocanam | sūryāyā bhadram id vāso gāthayaiti pariṣkṛtā ||

**18.1.7**

stomā āsan pratidhayaḥ kurīraṃ chanda opaśaḥ | sūryāyā aśvinā varā- -agnir āsīt purogavaḥ ||

**18.1.8**

somo vadhūyur abhavad aśvināstām ubhā varā | sūryāṃ yat patye śaṃsantīṃ manasā savitādadāt ||

**18.1.9**

mano asyā ana āsīd dyaur āsīd uta chadiḥ | śukrāv anaḍvāhāv āstāṃ yad ayāt sūryā patim ||

**18.1.10**

ṛksāmābhyām abhihitau gāvau te sāmanāv aitām | śrotre te cakre āstāṃ divi panthāś carācaraḥ ||

**18.2.1**

śucī te cakre yātyā vyāno akṣa āhataḥ | ano manasmayaṃ sūryā- -ārohat prayatī patim ||

**18.2.2**

sūryāyā vahatuḥ prāgāt savitā yam avāsṛjat | aghāsu hanyante gāvaḥ phalgunīṣu vy uhyate ||

**18.2.3**

yad aśvinā pṛcchamānāv ayātaṃ tricakreṇa vahatuṃ sūryāyāḥ | kvaikaṃ cakraṃ vām āsīt kva deṣṭrāya tasthathuḥ ||

**18.2.4**

yad ayātaṃ śubhaspatī vareyaṃ sūryām upa | viśve devā anu tad vām ajānan putraḥ pitarāv avṛnīta pūṣā ||

**18.2.5**

dve te cakre sūrye brahmāṇa ṛtuthā viduḥ | athaikaṃ cakraṃ yad guhā tad addhātaya id viduḥ ||

**18.2.6**

pra tvā muñcāmi varuṇasya pāśād yena tvābadhnāt savitā suśevāḥ | ṛtasya yonau sukṛtasya loke syonaṃ te astu sahapatnyai vadhu ||

**18.2.7**

aryamaṇaṃ yajāmahe subandhuṃ pativedanam | urvārukam iva bandhanād ito muñcantu māmutaḥ ||

**18.2.8**

preto muñcantu māmutaḥ subaddhām amutas karat | yatheyam indra mīḍhvaḥ suputrā subhagāsati ||

**18.2.9**

bhagas tveto nayatu hastagṛhya- -aśvinā tvā pra vahatāṃ rathena | gṛhān gaccha gṛhapatnī yathāso vaśinī tvaṃ vidatham ā vadāsi ||

**18.2.10**

iha priyaṃ prajāyai te sam ṛdhyatām asmin gṛhe gārhapatyāya jāgṛhi | enā patyā tanvaṃ saṃ spṛśasva- -atha jirvir vidatham ā vadāsi ||

**18.3.1**

ihaiva staṃ mā vi yauṣṭaṃ dīrgham āyur vy aśnutam | krīḍantau putrair naptṛbhir modamānau sve gṛhe ||

**18.3.2**

pūrvāparaṃ carato māyayaitau śiśū krīḍantau pari yāto adhvaram | viśvāny anyo bhuvanā vicaṣṭa ṛtūṃr anyo vidadhaj jāyate navaḥ ||

**18.3.3**

navonavo bhavati jāyamāno 'hnāṃ ketur uṣasām ety agram | bhāgaṃ devebhyo vi dadhāty āyan pra candramās tirate dīrgham āyuḥ ||

**18.3.4**

parā dehi śāmūlyaṃ brahmabhyo vi bhajā vasu | kṛtyaiṣā padvatī bhūtvā- -ā jāyā viśate patim ||

**18.3.5**

nīlalohitaṃ bhavati kṛtyāsaktir vy ajyate | edhante asyā jñātayaḥ patir bandheṣu badhyate ||

**18.3.6**

**18.3.7**

**18.3.8**

tṛṣṭam etat kaṭukam apāṣṭhavad viṣavan naitad attave | sūryāṃ yo brahmā veda sa id vādhūyam arhati ||

**18.3.9**

sa vai tat syonaṃ harati brahmā vāsaḥ sumaṅgalam | prāyaścittiṃ yo adhyeti yena jāyā na riṣyati ||

**18.3.10**

yuvaṃ bhagaṃ saṃ bharathaḥ samṛddham ṛtaṃ vadantām ṛtodyena | brahmaṇaspate patim asyai rocayāmuṃ cāru sambhalo vadatu vācam etām ||

**18.4.1**

ihed asātha na paro gamātha- -imaṃ gāvaḥ prajayā vardhayātha | śubhaṃ yatīr usriyāḥ somavarcaso viśve devāḥ krann iha vo manāṃsi ||

**18.4.2**

imaṃ gāvaḥ prajayā saṃ viśadhvam ayaṃ devānāṃ na mināti bhāgam | asmai vaḥ pūṣā marutaś ca sarve asmai vo dhātā savitā suvāti ||

**18.4.3**

anṛkṣarā ṛjavaḥ santu panthāno yebhiḥ sakhāyo yanti no vareyam | saṃ bhagena sam aryamṇā saṃ dhātā sṛjatu varcasā naḥ ||

**18.4.4**

yena mahānagnyā jaghanam aśvinā yena vā sūrā | yenākṣā abhyaṣicyanta tenemāṃ varcasāvatam ||

**18.4.5**

yad varco akṣeṣu sūrāyāṃ ca yad āhitam | yad goṣv aśvinā varcas tenemāṃ varcasāvatam ||

**18.4.6**

yo anidhmo dīdāyāpsv antar yaṃ viprāsa īḍate adhvareṣu | apān napān madhumatīr apo dā yābhir indro vāvṛdhe vīryāvān ||

**18.4.7**

idam ahaṃ ruśantaṃ grābhaṃ tānūdūṣim apa nudāmi | yaḥ śico bhadro rocānas tam ud acāmi ||

**18.4.8**

asyai harantu stapanaṃ brāhmaṇā avīraghnīr ud acantv āpaḥ | aryamṇo agniṃ pary etu kṣipraṃ pratīkṣante śvaśuro devaraś ca ||

**18.4.9**

śaṃ te hiraṇyaṃ śam u santv āpaḥ śaṃ methir bhavatu śaṃ yugasya tardma | śaṃ ta āpaḥ śatapavitrā bhavantu śaṃ patyā tanvaṃ saṃ spṛśasva ||

**18.4.10**

yathā sindhur nadīnāṃ sāmrājyaṃ suṣuve vṛṣā | evā tvaṃ samrājñy edhi patyur astaṃ paretya ||

**18.5.1**

samrājñy edhi śvaśureṣu samrājñy uta śvaśruvām | nanānduḥ samrājñy edhi samrājñy uta devṛṣu ||

**18.5.2**

yā akṛntann avayan yāś ca tatnire yā devīr antām̐ abhito 'dadanta | tās tvā jarase saṃ vyayantv āyuṣmatīdaṃ pari dhatsva vāsaḥ ||

**18.5.3**

jīvaṃ rudanti vi nayanty adhvaraṃ dīrghām anu prasitiṃ dīdhiyur naraḥ | vāmaṃ pitṛbhyo ya idaṃ samīrire mayaḥ patibhyo janaye pariṣvaje ||

**18.5.4**

dhruvaṃ syonaṃ prajāyai te dhārayāmy aśmānaṃ devyāḥ pṛthivyā upasthe | tam ā rohānumādyā suvīrā dīrghaṃ ta āyuḥ savitā kṛṇotu ||

**18.5.5**

devas te savitā hastaṃ gṛhṇātu somo rājā suprajasaṃ kṛṇotu | agniḥ subhagāṃ jātavedāḥ patyā patnīṃ jaradaṣṭiṃ kṛṇotu ||

**18.5.6**

gṛhṇāmi te saubhagatvāya hastaṃ mayā patyā jaradaṣṭir yathāsaḥ | bhago aryamā savitā purandhir mahyaṃ tvādur gārhapatyāya devāḥ ||

**18.5.7**

yenāgnir asyā bhūmyā hastaṃ jagrāha dakṣiṇam | tena gṛhṇāmi te hastaṃ mā vyathiṣṭhā mayā saha ||

**18.5.8**

dhātā te hastam agrahīt savitā hastam agrahīt | bhagas te hastam agrahīd aryamā hastam agrahīt | patnī tvam asi dharmaṇā- -ahaṃ gṛhapatis tava ||

**18.5.9**

mameyam astu poṣyā mahyaṃ tvādād bṛhaspatiḥ | mayā patyā prajāvatī saṃ jīva śaradaḥ śatam ||

**18.5.10**

tvaṣṭā vāso vy adadhāc chubhe kaṃ bṛhaspateḥ praśiṣā kavīnām | tenemāṃ nārīṃ savitā bhagaś ca sūryām iva pari dhattāṃ prajāyai ||

**18.6.1**

gṛhās tvā prorṇuvantu varcasā bhagena jyotiṣmad idaṃ prati muñcasva vapuḥ | uṣā iva sūryasya saṃdṛśi ||

**18.6.2**

indrāgnī dyāvāpṛthivī ha pūṣā mitrāvaruṇā bhago aśvinobhā | bṛhaspatir maruto brahma soma imāṃ nārīṃ prajayā vardhayantu ||

**18.6.3**

bṛhaspatir prathamaḥ sūryāyāḥ śīrṣe keṣām̐ akalpayat | tenemām aśvinā nārīṃ patyā saṃ śobhayāmasi ||

**18.6.4**

idaṃ tad rūpaṃ yad avasta yoṣā jāyāṃ jijñāse manasā carantīm | tām anv artiṣye sakhibhir navagvaiḥ ka imaṃ vidvān vi cacarta pāśam ||

**18.6.5**

ahaṃ vi ṣyāmi mayi rūpam asyā vedad it paśyan manasaḥ kulāyam | na steyam admi manasod amucye svayaṃ śrathnāno varuṇasya pāśam ||

**18.6.6**

imaṃ vi ṣyāmi varuṇasya pāśaṃ yena tvābadhnāt savitā suśevaḥ | uruṃ lokaṃ sugam atra panthāṃ kṛṇomi tubhyaṃ sahapatnyai vadhu ||

**18.6.7**

ud yacchadhvam apa rakṣo hanātha- -imāṃ nārīṃ sukṛte dadhāta | dhātā vipaścit patim asyai viveda bhago rājā pura etu prajānan ||

**18.6.8**

bhagas tatakṣa caturaḥ pado bhagas tatakṣa catvāry uṣpalāni | tvaṣṭā pipeśa madhyato vardhrān sā no astu sumaṅgalī ||

**18.6.9**

enājyena haviṣā prajāyai ca vareṇyam | paśubhyaś cakṣuṣe ca kaṃ sam agniṃ sam idhīmahi ||

**18.6.10**

sukiṃśukaṃ vahatuṃ viśvarūpaṃ hiraṇyavarṇaṃ suvṛtaṃ sucakram | ā roha sūrye amṛtasya lokaṃ syonaṃ patibhyo vahatuṃ kṛṇu tvam ||

**18.6.11**

mā hiṃsiṣṭāṃ kumāryaṃ sthūne devakṛte pathi | śālāyā devyā dvāraṃ syonaṃ kṛṇmo vadhūpatham ||

**18.6.12**

brahmāparaṃ yujyatāṃ brahma pūrvaṃ brahmāntato madhyato brahma sarvataḥ | anāvyādhāṃ devapurāṃ prapadya śivā syonā patiloke vi rāja ||

**18.7.1**

tubhyam agre pary avahan sūryāṃ vahatunā saha | punaḥ patibhyo jāyāṃ dā agne prajayā saha ||

**18.7.2**

punaḥ patnīm agnir adād āyuṣā saha varcasā | dīrghāyur asyā yaḥ patir jīvāti śaradaḥ śatam ||

**18.7.3**

somasya jāyā prathamaṃ gandharvas te 'paraḥ patiḥ | tṛtīyo agniṣ ṭe patis turīyas te manuṣyajāḥ ||

**18.7.4**

somo dadad gandharvāya gandharvo dadad agnaye | rayiṃ ca putrāṃś cādād agnir mahyam atho imām ||

**18.7.5**

ā vām agan sumatir vājinīvasū ny aśvinā hṛtsu kāmā araṃsata | abhūtaṃ gopā mithunā śubhaspatī priyā aryamṇo duryām̐ aśīmahi ||

**18.7.6**

sā mandasānā manasā śivena rayiṃ dhehi sarvavīraṃ vacasyam | sugaṃ tīrthaṃ suprapāṇaṃ śubhaspatī sthāṇuṃ pathiṣṭhām apa durmatiṃ hatam ||

**18.7.7**

apa rakṣāṃsy apa durmatiṃ hataṃ śubhaspatī vahator yāto asmāt | purogavo jayan rakṣāṃsy agne kṣetravit pūrvo vi mṛdho nudasva ||

**18.7.8**

yā oṣadhayo yā nadyo yāni kṣetrāṇi yā vanā | te tvā vadhu prajāvatīṃ patye rakṣantu rakṣasaḥ ||

**18.7.9**

emaṃ panthām aganma sugaṃ svastivāhanam | yatra vīro na riṣyaty anyeṣāṃ vindate vasu ||

**18.7.10**

idaṃ su me naraḥ śṛṇuta yayāśiṣā dampatī vāmam aśnutaḥ | ye gandharvā apsarasaś ca devīr eṣu vānaspatyeṣv adyādhi tasthuḥ | syonās te asyai vadhvai bhavantu mā hiṃsiṣur vahatum uhyamānam ||

**18.8.1**

ye vadhvaś candraṃ vahatuṃ yakṣmā yanti janām̐ anu | punas tān yajñiyā devā nayantu yata āgatāḥ ||

**18.8.2**

mā vidan paryāyiṇo ya āsīdanti dampatī | sugena durgam atītām apa drāntv arātayaḥ ||

**18.8.3**

saṃ kāśayāmi vahatuṃ brahmaṇā gṛhair aghoreṇa cakṣuṣā mitryeṇa | paryāṇaddhaṃ viśvarūpaṃ yad asmin syonaṃ patibhyaḥ savitā kṛṇotu tat ||

**18.8.4**

śivā nārīyam astam āgann imaṃ dhātā lokam asyai viveda | tām aryamā bhago aśvinobhā prajāpatiḥ prajayā vardhayantu ||

**18.8.5**

ātmanvaty urvarā narīyam āgan tasyāṃ naro vapata bījam asyām | sā vaḥ prajāṃ janayād vakṣaṇābhyo bibhratī dudhram ṛṣabhasya retaḥ ||

**18.8.6**

prati tiṣṭha virāḍ iva viṣṇur iveha sarasvati | dūrvevendra pra jāyatāṃ bhagasya sumatāv asat ||

**18.8.7**

ud vām ūrmiḥ śamye hantv āpo yoktrāṇi muñcantu | māduṣkṛtau vyenasā- -aghnyau śūnam āratām ||

**18.8.8**

aghoracakṣur apatighnī syonā śagmā suśevā suyamā gṛheṣu | prajāvatī vīrasūr devṛkāmā- -imam agniṃ gārhapatyaṃ sarpaya ||

**18.8.9**

adevṛghny apatighnīhaidhi syonā paśubhyaḥ sumanāḥ suvīrā | vīrasūr devṛkāmā syonā saṃ tvaidhiṣīmahi sumanasyamānā ||

**18.8.10**

ut tiṣṭhātaḥ kim icchantīdam āgā ahaṃ tveḍe abhibhūḥ svād gṛhāt | śūnyaiśī nirṛte yājagantha- -uttiṣṭhārāte pra pata māti raṃsthāḥ ||

**18.9.1**

**18.9.2**

**18.9.5**

ā roha carmopa sīdāgnim eṣa devo hanti rakṣāṃsi sarvā | sumaṅgaly upa sīdemam agniṃ saṃpatnī prati bhūṣeha devān ||

**18.9.6**

pra jāyantāṃ mātur asyā upasthān nānārūpāḥ paśavo jāyamānāḥ | iha prajāṃ janaya patye asmai sujyaiṣṭhyo bhavatu te putra eṣaḥ ||

**18.9.7**

sumaṅgalī prataraṇī gṛhāṇāṃ suśeva patye śvaśurāya śambhūḥ | syonā śvaśruvai pra gṛhān viśemān prajāvatī jaradaṣṭir yathāsaḥ ||

**18.9.8**

sumaṅgalīr iyaṃ vadhūr imāṃ sameta paśyata | saubhāgyam asyai dattvā daurbhāgyena paretana ||

**18.9.9**

**18.9.10**

**18.10.1**

ā roha talpaṃ sumanasyamānā- -iha prajāṃ janaya patye asmai | indrāṇīva subudhā budhyamānā jyotirugrā uṣasaḥ prati jāgaraḥ ||

**18.10.2**

devā agre ny apadyanta patnīṃ sam aspṛśanta tanvas tanūbhiḥ | sūryeva nāri viśvarūpā mahitvā prajāvatī patye śaṃ bhavāsi ||

**18.10.3**

ud īrṣvātaḥ pativatnī hy eṣā viśvāvasuṃ namasā gīrbhir īḍe | jāmim iccha pitṛṣadaṃ nyaktāṃ sa te bhāgo januṣā tasya viddhi ||

**18.10.4**

yā apsarasaḥ sadhamādaṃ madanty antarā havirdhānaṃ sūryaṃ ca | tās te janitram abhi tāḥ parehi namas te gandharva rtunā kṛṇomi ||

**18.10.5**

namo ghandharvasya manase namo bhāmāya cakṣuṣe ca kṛṇmaḥ | viśvāvaso namo brahmaṇā te kṛṇomy abhi jāyā apsarasaḥ parehi ||

**18.10.6**

rāyā vayaṃ sumanasaḥ syāma- -ud ito gandharvam āvīvṛtāma | agan sa devaḥ paramaṃ sadhastham aganma vayaṃ pratiranta āyuḥ ||

**18.10.7**

saṃ pitarāv ṛtviye sṛjethāṃ pitā mātā ca retaso bhavāthaḥ | maryo yoṣām adhi rohayaitāṃ prajāṃ kṛṇvāthām iha modamānau ||

**18.10.8**

ā vāṃ prajāṃ janayatu prajāpatir ahorātrābhyāṃ sam anaktv aryamā | adurmaṅgalī patilokam ā viśemaṃ śaṃ no bhava dvipade śaṃ catuṣpade ||

**18.10.9**

tāṃ pūṣañ chivatamām erayasva yasyāṃ bījaṃ manuṣyā vapanti | yā na ūrū uśatī viśrayāti yasyām uśantaḥ praharema śepaḥ ||

**18.10.10**

ā rohorum upa dhatsva hastaṃ pari ṣvajasva jāyāṃ sumanasyamānaḥ | prajāṃ kṛṇvāthām iha puṣyataṃ rayiṃ dīrghaṃ vām āyuḥ savitā kṛṇotu ||

**18.11.1**

yaṃ no dadur brahmabhāgaṃ vadhūyor vāso vadhvaś ca vastram | yuvaṃ brahmaṇe 'numanyamānau bṛhaspate sākam indraś ca dattam ||

**18.11.2**

devair dattaṃ vanunā sākam etad vādhūyaṃ vadhvo vāso asyāḥ | yo brahmaṇe cikituṣe dadāti sa id rakṣāṃsi talpyāni hanti ||

**18.11.3**

syonād yoner adhi budhyamānau hasāmudau mahasā modamānau | sugū suputrau sugṛhau tarātho jīvā uṣaso vibhātīḥ ||

**18.11.4**

navaṃ vasānaḥ surabhiḥ suvāsā udāgāṃ jīva uṣaso vibhātīḥ | āṇḍāt patatrīvāmukṣi viśvasmād enasas pari ||

**18.11.5**
