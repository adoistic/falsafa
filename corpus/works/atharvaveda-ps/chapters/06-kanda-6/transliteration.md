**6.1.1**

tad id āsa bhuvaneṣu jyeṣṭhaṃ yato jajña ugras tveṣanṛmṇaḥ | sadyo jajñāno ni riṇāti śatrūn anu yaṃ viśve madanty ūmāḥ ||

**6.1.2**

vāvṛdhānaḥ śavasā bhūryojāḥ śatrur dāsāya bhiyasaṃ dadhāti | avyanac ca vyanac ca sasni saṃ te navanta prabhṛtā madeṣu ||

**6.1.3**

tve kratum api pṛñcanti viśve dvir yad ete trir bhavanty ūmāḥ | svādoḥ svādīyaḥ svādunā sṛjā sam adaḥ su madhu madhunābhi yodhīḥ ||

**6.1.4**

iti cid dhi tvā dhanā jayantaṃ raṇeraṇe anumadanti viprāḥ | ojīyo dhṛṣṇo sthiram ā tanuṣva mā tvā dabhan durevā yātudhānāḥ ||

**6.1.5**

tvayā vayaṃ śāśadmahe raṇeṣu prapaśyanto yudhenyāni bhūri | codayāmi ta āyudhā vacobhiḥ saṃ te śyāmi brahmaṇā vayāṃsi ||

**6.1.6**

stuṣeyyaṃ puruvarpasam ṛbhvam inatamam āptyam āptyānām | ā darśate śavasā sapta dānūn pra sākṣate pratimānāni bhūri ||

**6.1.7**

ni tad dadhiṣe 'varaṃ paraṃ ca yasminn āvithāvasā duroṇe | ā mātarā sthāpayase jigatnū ata inoṣi karvarā purūṇi ||

**6.1.8**

imā brahma bṛhaddivo vivakti- -indrāya śūṣam agriyaḥ svarṣāḥ | maho gotrasya kṣayati svarājo duraś ca viśvā avṛṇod apa svāḥ ||

**6.1.9**

evā mahān bṛhaddivo atharvā- -avocat svāṃ tanvam indram eva | svasāro mātaribhvarīr ariprā hinvanti ca śavasā vardhayanti ca ||

**6.2.1**

ṛdhaṅmandrayoninovibhāvā amṛtāsuḥ sujanmā vardhamānaḥ | adabdhāsur bhrājamāno 'heva trito dādhāra trīṇi ||

**6.2.2**

ni yo dharmaṇi prathamaḥ sasāda- -ato vapūṃṣi kṛṇute purūṇi | dhāsyur yoniṃ prathama ā viveśa- -ā yo vācam anuditāṃ cikāya ||

**6.2.3**

yas te śokas tanva ārireca kṣarad dhiraṇyaṃ śucayo 'nu svāḥ | atrā dadhiṣe amṛtāni nāma- -asme vastrāṇīṣa erayanta ||

**6.2.4**

pra yad ete prataraṃ pūrvyaṃ guḥ sadaḥsada ātiṣṭhanto ajuryam | kaviḥ śuṣasya mātarā rihāṇe jāmirvadhuryuḥpratimānimīta ||

**6.2.5**

tad ū ṣu te mahā pṛthuryaman namaḥ kaviḥ kāvyenākṛṇot | yat samyañco 'bhiyanto *'dhi kṣām adhā mahī rodhacakrā vavardha ||

**6.2.6**

**6.2.7**

**6.2.8**

darśan nu tān varuṇa ye ta iṣṭāv āvarvṛtataḥ kṛṇavo vapūṃṣi | ardham ardhena śavasā pṛṇakṣy ardhena śuṣmaṃ vardhase *'mūra ||

**6.2.9**

avīvṛdhāma śagmyaṃ sakhāyaṃ varuṇaṃ putram aditer iṣiram | kaviśastāny asmai vacāṃsy avocāma rodasī satyavācau ||

**6.3.1**

**6.3.2**

**6.3.3**

punānā āpo bahudhā sravanti- -imāṃś ca lokān pradiśaś ca sarvāḥ | punantv asmān duritād avadyān muñcantu mṛtyor nirṛter upasthāt ||

**6.3.4**

āpo asmān mātaraḥ sūdayantu ghṛtena no ghṛtapuvaḥ punantu | viśvaṃ hi ripraṃ pravahanti devīr ud id ābhyaḥ śucir ā pūta emi ||

**6.3.5**

**6.3.6**

**6.3.7**

**6.3.8**

**6.3.9**

**6.3.10**

yad dhāvanti punate tad āpo yat tiṣṭhanti śuddhā it tad bhavanti | nāsām avadyam avidaṃ na ripraṃ sanād eva madhunā saṃ papṛcre ||

**6.3.11**

hiraṇyavarṇāḥ śucayaḥ pāvakāḥ pra cakramur hitvāvadyam āpaḥ | śataṃ ca vaḥ prasravaṇeṣu devīḥ sahasraṃ ca pavitāraḥ punanti ||

**6.3.12**

tās tādṛśīr brahmāṇaṃ sūdayanty aṅgoṣṭhyā stotryā jīvadhanyāḥ | yā viśvasya śucikṛto ayātor gāva iva payasā sthā sujātāḥ ||

**6.3.13**

viśvād riprān muñcata sindhavo no yāny enāṃsi cakṛmā tanūbhiḥ | indrapraśiṣṭā varuṇaprasūtā ā siñcatāpo madhv ā samudre ||

**6.4.2**

**6.4.3**

**6.4.4**

**6.4.5**

**6.4.6**

**6.4.8**

**6.4.9**

**6.4.10**
