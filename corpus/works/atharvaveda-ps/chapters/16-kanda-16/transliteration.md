**16.1.1**

antakāya mṛtyave nama iha- -ayam astu puruṣaḥ sahāsunā | sūryasya bhāge amṛtasya loke prāṇā apānā iha te ramantām ||

**16.1.2**

ud enaṃ bhago agrabhīd ud enaṃ somo aṃśumān | ud enaṃ maruto devā ud indrāgnī svastaye ||

**16.1.3**

iha te 'sur iha prāṇa ihāyur iha te manaḥ | ut tvā nirṛtyāḥ pāśebhyo daivyā vācā bharāmasi ||

**16.1.4**

ut krāmāto māva patthā mṛtyoḥ paḍvīśam avamuñcamānaḥ | mā cchitthā asmāl lokād agneḥ sūryasya saṃdṛśaḥ ||

**16.1.5**

tubhyaṃ vātaḥ pavatāṃ mātariśvā tubhyaṃ varṣantv amṛtāny āpaḥ | sūryas te tanve śaṃ tapāt tvāṃ mṛtyur dayatāṃ mā pra meṣṭhāḥ ||

**16.1.6**

udyānaṃ te puruṣa nāvayānaṃ jīvātaṃ te dakṣatātiṃ kṛṇotu | ā hi rohemam amṛtaṃ sukhaṃ ratham atha jirvir vidatham ā vadāsi ||

**16.1.7**

mā te manas tatra gān mā tiro bhūn mā jīvebhyaḥ pra mado mānu gāḥ pitṝn | viśve devā abhi rakṣantu tveha ||

**16.1.8**

mā gatānām ā dīdhīthā ye nayanti parāvatam | ud ā roha tamaso jyotir ehy ā te hastaṃ rabhāmahe

**16.1.9**

śyāmaś ca tvā śabalaś ca preṣitau yamasya yau pathirakṣī śvānau | arvāṅ ehi mā vi dīdhyo māti tiṣṭhaḥ parāṅmanāḥ ||

**16.1.10**

maitaṃ panthām anu gā bhīma eṣa yena pūrvaṃ nayatha taṃ bravīmi | tama etat puruṣa mā pra patthā bhayaṃ parastād abhayaṃ te arvāk ||

**16.2.1**

rakṣantu tvāgnayo ye apsv antā rakṣatu tvā manuṣyā yam indhate | vaiśvānaro rakṣatu jātavedā divyas tvā mā pra dhāg vidyutā saha ||

**16.2.2**

rakṣatu tvā pṛthivī rakṣatu dyaus sūryaś ca tvā rakṣatāṃ candramāś ca | mā tvā kravyād abhi maṃsta- -ārāt saṃkasukāc cara ||

**16.2.3**

antarikṣaṃ rakṣatu devahetyā bodhaś ca tvā pratībodhaś ca rakṣatām | asvapnaś ca tvānavadrāṇaś ca rakṣatāṃ gopāyaṃś ca tvā rakṣatāṃ jāgṛviś ca ||

**16.2.4**

te tvā rakṣantu te tvā gopāyantu te tvāṃhasas pāntu | tebhyo namas tebhyaḥ svāhā ||

**16.2.5**

jīvebhyas tvā samude vāyur indro dhātā dadhātu savitā trāyamāṇaḥ | mā tvā prāṇo balaṃ hāsīd asuṃ te 'nu hvayāmasi ||

**16.2.6**

mā tvā jambhaḥ saṃhanur mā tamo vidan mā jihvā barhiḥ pramayuḥ kathā syāḥ | ut tvādityā vasavo bharantu- -ud indrāgnī svastaye ||

**16.2.7**

ayaṃ devā ihaivāstv ayaṃ māmutra gād itaḥ | imaṃ sahasravīryeṇa mṛtyor ut pārayāmasi ||

**16.2.8**

ut tvā mṛtyor apīparaṃ saṃ dhamantu vayodhasaḥ | mā tvā vyastakeśyo mā tvāgharudo rudan ||

**16.2.9**

āhārṣaṃ tvāvidaṃ tvā punar āgāḥ punarṇavaḥ | sarvāṅga sarvaṃ te cakṣuḥ sarvam āyuś ca te 'vidam ||

**16.2.10**

ut tvā dyaur ut pṛthivy ut prajāpatir agrabhīt | ut tvā mṛtyor oṣadhayaḥ | somarājñīr apīparan ||

**16.2.11**

yāvat te jyotir abhūd apa tvat tamo akramīt | apa tvan mṛtyuṃ nirṛtim apa yakṣmaṃ ni dadhmasi ||

**16.3.1**

ā rabhasvemām amṛtasya śnuṣṭim acchidyamānā jaradaṣṭir astu te | asuṃ ta āyuḥ punar ā bharāmi rajas tamo mopa gā mā pra meṣṭhāḥ ||

**16.3.2**

jīvatāṃ jyotir abhy ehi lokam ā tvā harāmi śataśāradāya | drāghīya āyuḥ prataraṃ te kṛṇomy ava muñcantu mṛtyupāśā aśastīḥ ||

**16.3.3**

vātāt te prāṇam avidaṃ sūryāc cakṣur ahaṃ pari | yat te manas tvayi tad dhārayāmi saṃ vitsvāṅgair vada jihvayālapan ||

**16.3.4**

prāṇena tvāṃ dvipadāṃ catuṣpadām agnim iva jātam abhi saṃ dhamāmi | namas te mṛtyo cakṣuṣe namaḥ prāṇāya te 'karam ||

**16.3.5**

ayaṃ jīvatu mā mṛta- -imaṃ sam īrayāmasi | kṛṇomy asmai bheṣajaṃ mṛtyo mā puruṣaṃ vadhīḥ ||

**16.3.6**

jīvalāṃ naghāriṣāṃ jīvantīm oṣadhīm aham | trāyamāṇāṃ sahamānāṃ sahasvatīm arundhatīm iha huve 'smā ariṣṭatātaye ||

**16.3.7**

adhi brūhi mā rabhathāḥ sṛjemaṃ tavaiva sant sarvahāyā ihāstu | bhavāśarvau mṛḍataṃ śarma yacchatam apasidhya duritaṃ dhattam āyuḥ ||

**16.3.8**

devānāṃ hetiḥ pari tvā vṛṇaktu pārayāmi rajasa ut tvā mṛtyor apīparam | ārād agniṃ kravyādaṃ nirūhan jivātave te paridhiṃ dadhāmi ||

**16.3.9**

asmai mṛtyo adhi brūhi- -imaṃ dayasvod ito 'yam etu | ariṣṭaḥ sarvāṅgaḥ suśruj jarasā śatahāyanātmanā bhujam aśnavat ||

**16.3.10**

yat te niyānaṃ rajasaṃ mṛtyo anavadhariṣyam | patha imaṃ tasmād rakṣanto brahmāsmai varma kṛṇmasi ||

**16.4.1**

kṛṇomi te prāṇāpānau jarāṃ mṛtyuṃ kṛṇutāṃ dīrgham āyuḥ svasti | vaivasvatena prahitān yamadūtāṃś carata ārād apa sedhāmi sarvān ||

**16.4.2**

ārād arātiṃ nirṛtiṃ paro grāhiṃ kravyādaḥ piśacān | rakṣo yat sarvaṃ durbhūtaṃ tat tama ivāpa hanmasi ||

**16.4.3**

agneṣ ṭe prāṇam amṛtād āyuṣmato manave jātavedasaḥ | yathā na riṣyā amṛtaḥ sajūr asas tat te kṛṇomi tad u te sam ṛdhyatām ||

**16.4.4**

śive te stāṃ dyāvāpṛthivī asantāpe adhiśriyau | śaṃ te sūrya ā tapati śaṃ vāto vātu te hṛde | śivā abhi kṣarantu tvā- -āpo divyāḥ payasvatīḥ śivās te santv oṣadhīḥ ||

**16.4.5**

ut tvāhārṣam adharasyād uttarāṃ pṛthivīm abhi | tatra tvādityau rakṣatāṃ sūryāś candramasā ubhā ||

**16.4.6**

yat te vāsaḥ paridhānaṃ yāṃ nīviṃ kṛṇuṣe tvam | śivaṃ te tanve tat kṛṇmaḥ saṃsparśe 'dhrūkṣṇam astu te ||

**16.4.7**

yat kṣureṇa marcayatā sutejasā vaptā vapasi keśaśmasru | śumbhaṃ mukhaṃ mainam āyuḥ pra moṣīḥ ||

**16.4.8**

śivau te stāṃ vrīhiyavāv abalāsāv adomadhau | etau yakṣmaṃ vi bādhete etau muñcato aṃhasaḥ ||

**16.4.9**

yad aśnāsi yat pibasi dhānyaṃ kṛṣyāḥ payaḥ | ādyaṃ yad anādyaṃ sarvaṃ te annam aviṣaṃ kṛṇomi ||

**16.4.10**

ahne ca tvā rātraye ca- -ubhābhyāṃ pari dadhmasi | arāyebhyo jighatsubhyaḥ imaṃ naḥ pari rakṣata ||

**16.5.1**

śataṃ te 'yutaṃ hāyanā dve yuge trīṇi catvāri santu | indrāgnī viśve devā anu manyantām ahṛṇīyamānāḥ ||

**16.5.2**

śarade tvā hemantāya vasantāya griṣmāya pari dadhmasi | varṣāṇi tubhyaṃ syonāni yeṣu vardhanta oṣadhīḥ ||

**16.5.3**

mṛtyur īśe dvipadāṃ mṛtyur īśe catuṣpadām | tasmāt tvā mṛtyor gopater ud dharāmi sa mā mṛthāḥ ||

**16.5.4**

so 'riṣṭa na mariṣyasi na mariṣyasi mā bibheḥ | na vai tatra pra mīyante no yanty adharaṃ rajaḥ ||

**16.5.5**

sarvo vai tatra jīvati gaur aśvaḥ puruṣaḥ paśuḥ | yatredaṃ brahma kriyate paridhir jīvanāya kam ||

**16.5.6**

pari tvā pātu samānebhyo 'bhicārāt sabandhubhyaḥ | amamrir bhavāmṛto 'tijīvo mā te hāsiṣur asavaḥ śarīram ||

**16.5.7**

viśve tvā devā amṛtena bibhratv adhivaktā paśupatiṣ ṭe astu | anāmayat savitā te kṛṇotv ā tvā prāṇo viśatu jīvanāya ||

**16.5.8**

ye mṛtyava ekaśataṃ yā nāṣṭrātijīvyā | muñcantu tasmāt tvā devā agner vaiśvānarād adhi ||

**16.6.1**

rakṣohaṇaṃ vājinam ā jigharmi mitraṃ prathiṣṭham upa yāmi śarma | śiśāno agniḥ kratubhiḥ samiddhaḥ sa no divā sa riṣaḥ pātu naktam ||

**16.6.2**

ayodaṃṣṭrān arciṣā vāvṛdhānān upa spṛśa jātavedaḥ samiddhaḥ | ā jihvayā mūradevān rabhasva kravyādo vṛṣṭvāpi dhatsvāsan ||

**16.6.3**

ubhobhayāvinn upa dehi daṃṣṭrā hiṃsraḥ śiśāno 'varaṃ paraṃ ca | utāntarikṣe pari yāhy agne jambhaiḥ saṃ dhehy abhi yātudhānān ||

**16.6.4**

agne tvacaṃ yātudhānasya bhindhi hiṃsrāśanir harasā hantv enam | pra parvāṇi jātavedaḥ śṛṇīhi kravyāt kraviṣṇur vi cinoty enam ||

**16.6.5**

yajñair iṣūḥ saṃnamamāno agne vācā śalyām̐ aśanibhir dihānaḥ | tābhir vidhya hṛdaye yātudhānān pratīco bāhūn prati bhaṅdhy eṣām ||

**16.6.6**

yatredānīṃ paśyasi jātavedas tiṣṭhantam agna uta vā carantam | utāntarikṣe patantaṃ yātudhānaṃ tamasā vidhya sarvā śiśānaḥ ||

**16.6.7**

utārabdhāc chṛṇīhi jātaveda utārebhāṇād ṛṣṭibhir yātudhānāt | agne pūrvo ni jahi śośucāna āmādaḥ kṣviṅkās tam adantv enīḥ ||

**16.6.8**

iha pra brūhi yatamaḥ so agne yo yātudhāno ya idaṃ śṛṇoti | tam ā rabhasva samidhā yaviṣṭha nṛcakṣasaś cakṣuṣe randhayaitam ||

**16.6.9**

tīkṣṇenāgne cakṣuṣā rakṣa yajñaṃ prāñcaṃ vasubhyaḥ pra ṇaya pracetaḥ | hiṃsraṃ rakṣāṃsy abhi śośucānaḥ mā tvā dabhan yātudhānā nṛcakṣaḥ ||

**16.6.10**

nṛcakṣā rakṣaḥ prati paśya vikṣu tasya trīṇi prati śṛṇīhy agrā | tasyāgne pṛṣṭīr harasā śṛṇīhi tredhā mūlaṃ yātudhānasya vṛśca ||

**16.7.1**

trir yātudhānaḥ prasitiṃ ta etv ṛtaṃ yo agne anṛtena hanti | tam arciṣā sphūrjayañ jātavedaḥ samakṣam enaṃ gṛṇate ni vṛṅdhi ||

**16.7.2**

yad agne adya mithunā śapāto yad vācas tṛṣṭaṃ janayanta rebhāḥ | manyor manasaḥ śaravyā jāyate yā tayā vidhya hṛdaye yātudhānān ||

**16.7.3**

parā śṛṇīhi tapasā yātudhānān parāgne rakṣo harasā śṛṇīhi | parārciṣā mūradevāñ chṛṇīhi parāsutṛpaḥ śośucataḥ śṛṇīhi ||

**16.7.4**

parādya devā vṛjinaṃ śṛṇantu pratyag enaṃ śapathā yantu sṛṣṭāḥ | vācāstenaṃ śarava ṛcchantu marman viśvayaitu prasitiṃ yātudhānaḥ ||

**16.7.5**

sanād agne mṛṇasi yātudhānān na tvā rakṣāṃsi pṛtanāsu jigyuḥ | anu daha sahamūrān kravyādo mā te hetyā mukṣata daivyāyāḥ ||

**16.7.6**

yaḥ pauruṣeyeṇa kraviṣā samaṅkte yo aśvyena paśunā yātudhānaḥ | yo aghnyāyā bharati kṣīram agne teṣāṃ śīrṣāṇi harasāpi vṛśca ||

**16.7.7**

saṃvatsarīṇaṃ paya usriyāyās tasya māsīd yātudhāno nṛcakṣaḥ | pīyūṣam agne yatamas titṛpsāt taṃ pratyañcam arciṣā vidhya marman ||

**16.7.8**

viṣaṃ gavāṃ yātudhānāḥ pibantv ā vṛścantām aditaye durevāḥ | paraiṇān devaḥ savitā dadātu parā bhāgam oṣadhīnāṃ jayantām ||

**16.7.9**

tvaṃ no agne adharād udaktas tvaṃ paścād uta rakṣā purastāt | prati tye te ajarāsas tapiṣṭhā aghaśaṃsaṃ śośucato dahantu ||

**16.7.10**

paścāt purastād adharād udaktaḥ kaviḥ kāvyena pari pāhy agne | sakhā sakhāyam ajaro jarimṇe agne martyām̐ amartyas tvaṃ naḥ ||

**16.8.1**

tad agne cakṣuḥ prati dhehi rebhe śaphārujaṃ yena paśyasi yātudhānam | atharvavaj jyotiṣā daivyena [Bhatt. atharvava(j) satyaṃ dhūrvantam acitaṃ ny oṣa ||

**16.8.2**

pari tvāgne puraṃ vayaṃ vipraṃ sahasya dhīmahi | dhṛṣadvarṇaṃ divedive hantāraṃ bhaṅgurāvatām ||

**16.8.3**

vi jyotiṣā bṛhatā bhāty agnir āvir viśvāni kṛṇute mahitvā | prādevīr māyāḥ sahate durevāḥ śiśīte śṛṅge rakṣase vinikṣe ||

**16.8.4**

agnī rakṣāṃsi sedhati śukraśocir amartyaḥ | śuciḥ pāvaka īḍyaḥ ||

**16.8.5**

agne rakṣā ṇo aṃhasaḥ prati ṣma deva rīṣataḥ | tapiṣṭhair ajaro daha ||

**16.8.6**

ye te śṛṅge ajare jātavedas tigmaśocī brahmasaṃśite | tābhyāṃ durhārdam abhidāsantaṃ kimīdinaṃ pratyañcaṃ yātudhānaṃ jātavedo vi nikṣva ||

**16.8.7**

viṣeṇa bhaṅgurāvataḥ sam indra rakṣaso daha | agne śukreṇa śociṣā tapuragrebhir arcibhiḥ ||

**16.8.8**

praty agne mithunā daha yātudhānā kimīdinā | saṃ tvā śiśāmi jāgṛhy adabdhaṃ vipra manmabhiḥ ||

**16.8.9**

praty agne harasā haraḥ śṛṇīhi viśvataḥ prati | yātudhānasya rakṣaso balaṃ vi ruja vīryam ||

**16.8.10**

sādānveyaṃ pra mṛṇa rakṣa indra yātudhānakṣayaṇair mūraiḥ | etāv agne mithunā yātudhānā viṣvañcau mruktau harasā śayātām ||

**16.8.11**

bṛhaspatir naḥ pari pātu paścād utottarasmād adharād aghāyoḥ | indraḥ purastād uta madhyato naḥ sakhā sakhibhyo varīyaḥ kṛṇotu ||

**16.9.1**

indrāsomā tapataṃ rakṣa ubjataṃ ny arpayataṃ vṛṣaṇā tamovṛdhaḥ | parā śṛṇīhi tam acito ny oṣataṃ hataṃ nudethāṃ ni śiśītam atriṇaḥ ||

**16.9.2**

indrāsomā sam aghaśaṃsam abhy aghaṃ tapur yayastu carur agnivām̐ iva | brahmadviṣe kravyāde ghoracakṣase dveṣo dhattam anavāyaṃ kimīdine ||

**16.9.3**

indrāsomā duṣkṛto vavre antar anārambhaṇe tamasi pra vidhyatam | yathaiṣāṃ nātaḥ punar ekaś canodayat tad vām astu sahase manyumac chavaḥ ||

**16.9.4**

indrāsomā vartayataṃ divas pary agnitaptebhir yuvam aśmahanmabhiḥ | tapurvadhebhir ajarebhir atriṇo ni parśāne vidhyataṃ yantu nisvaram ||

**16.9.5**

indrāsomā pra harataṃ divo vadhaṃ saṃ pṛthivyā aghaśaṃsāya tarhaṇam | ut takṣataṃ svaryaṃ parvatebhyo yena rakṣo vavṛdhānaṃ nijūrvathaḥ ||

**16.9.6**

indrāsomā pari vāṃ bhūtu viśvata iyaṃ matiḥ kakṣyāśveva vājinā | yāṃ vāṃ hotrāṃ pari hiṇomi medhayā- -imā brahmāṇi nṛpatīva jinvatam ||

**16.9.7**

prati smarethāṃ tujayadbhir evair hataṃ druho rakṣaso bhaṅgurāvataḥ | indrāsomā duṣkṛte mā sugaṃ bhūd yo mā kadā cid abhidāsati druhuḥ ||

**16.9.8**

yo mā pākena manasā carantam abhicaṣṭe anṛtebhir vacobhiḥ | āpa iva kāśinā saṃgṛbhītā asann astv asata indra vaktā ||

**16.9.9**

ye pākaśaṃsaṃ viharanta evair ye vā bhadraṃ dūṣayanti svadhābhiḥ | ahaye vā tān pradadātu soma ā vā dadhātu nirṛter upasthe ||

**16.9.10**

yo no rasaṃ dipsati pitvo agne yo aśvānāṃ yo gavāṃ yas tanūnām | ripu stena steyakṛd dabhram etu ni ṣa hīyatāṃ tanvā tanā ca ||

**16.10.1**

paraḥ so astu tanvā tanā ca tisraḥ pṛthivīr adho astu viśvāḥ | prati śuṣyati yaśo asya devā yo mā divā dipsati yaś ca naktam ||

**16.10.2**

suvijñānaṃ cikituṣe janāya sac cāsac ca vacasī paspṛśāte | tayor yat satyaṃ yatarad ṛjīyas tad it somo 'vati hanty āsat ||

**16.10.3**

na vā u somo vṛjinaṃ hinoti na kṣatriyaṃ mithuyā dhārayantam | hanti rakṣo hanty āsad vadantam ubhāv indrasya prasitau śayāte ||

**16.10.4**

yadi vāham anṛtadevo asmi moghaṃ vā devām̐ apyūhe agne | kim asmabhyaṃ jātavedo hṛṇīṣe droghavācas te nirṛthaṃ sacantām ||

**16.10.5**

adyā murīya yadi yātudhāno asmi yadi vāyus tatapa pūruṣasya | adhā sa vīrair daśabhir vi yūyā yo mā moghaṃ yātudhānety aha ||

**16.10.6**

yo māyātuṃ yātudhānety āha yo mā rakṣāḥ śucir asmīty āha | indras taṃ hantu mahatā vadhena viśvasya jantor adhamas padīṣṭa ||

**16.10.7**

pra yā jigāti khargaleva naktam apa druhus tanvaṃ gūhamānā | vavrām̐ anantām̐ ava sā padīṣṭa grāvāṇo ghnantu rakṣasa upabdaiḥ ||

**16.10.8**

vi tiṣṭhadhvaṃ maruto vikṣv icchata gṛbhāyata rakṣasaḥ saṃ pinaṣṭana | vayo ye bhūtvā patayanti naktabhir ye vā ripo dadhire deve adhvare ||

**16.10.9**

eta u tye patayanti śvayātava indraṃ dipsanti dipsavo 'dābhyam | śiśīte śakraḥ piśunebhyo vadhaṃ nūnaṃ sṛjad aśaniṃ yātumadbhyaḥ ||

**16.149.8**

**16.149.9**

kaṇṭhadaghnām ahimārttinṛṇāṃ devebhyaḥ kilbiṣaṃ yad babhūva | imās tad āpaḥ pra vahantu ripraṃ punātu mā śatadhāraṃ pavitram ||

**16.149.10**

snāhi ripraṃ śamalaṃ ca sarvaṃ kṛṣṇe cele sādayitvā pāpam | hitvāvartiṃ nirṛtiṃ mṛtyupāśān sūryaṃ jyotir abhy ehy agnim ||

**16.149.11**

udyanto asmān mahataḥ samudrān mucyamāno aṃhasaḥ pāpmanaś ca | punar manaḥ punar āyur na āgan mā mā dabhan paṇayo yātudhānāḥ ||

**16.149.12**

ava ripram anikṣmahy aśastim adhy ātmanaḥ | varca ā dhīyatāṃ mayi teja ā dhīyatāṃ mayi | prāṇāpānau mā mā hāsiṣṭaṃ sarvam āyur aśīya ||

**16.150.2**

brahma bhrājad ud agād antarikṣaṃ divaṃ ca brahmāvādhūṣṭāmṛtena mṛtyum | brahmopadraṣṭā sukṛtasya sākṣād brahmāsmad apa hantu śamalaṃ tamaś ca ||

**16.150.3**

pratīcīr āyatā stara vitatāḥ paśyāmahai | samudre apsu yā hitā- -asurāṇām ayasmayī ||

**16.150.4**

hariḥ suparṇo divam āruho 'rciṣā ye tvā dipsanti divam utpatantam | ava tāṃ jahi harasā jātavedo 'bibhyad ugro hy arciṣā divam ā roha sūrya ||

**16.150.5**

ayojālā asurā māyino 'yasmayaiḥ pāśair aṅkino ye caranti | tāṃs te randhayāmi harase jātavedaḥ sahasrabhṛṣṭiḥ sapatnān pramṛṇan yāhi vajraḥ ||

**16.150.6**

devānām adhipā eti gharma ṛtena bhrājann amṛtaṃ vicaṣṭe | hiraṇyavarṇo nabhaso deva sūrya gharmo bhrājan divo antān pary eṣi vidyutā ||

**16.150.7**

vidyotante vidyuto agnijihvā hiraṇyavarṇā amṛtā apsv antaḥ samudre | rudrasya kṣipno stanayitnor vidyut tasya vaiśvānārasya hetiḥ pari ṇo vṛṇaktu ||

**16.150.8**

vidyutā bhrājan hṛdayaṃ yāty agnir vyāghrā apsuṣado yatra bhīmāḥ | viṣṇoḥ kramai stanayann eti rudro nudaṃ chatrūn vi mṛdho bādhamāno 'sapatnāḥ pradiśo me kṛṇotu ||

**16.150.9**

apo vasānaḥ samaity antarikṣaṃ divaṃ ca samiddho agnir divyas tapodhāḥ | vaiśvānaraḥ śamayā śītarūre apāṃ suparṇo diva eti pṛṣṭhe ||

**16.150.10**

vaiśvānaraḥ samudraṃ pary eti śukro gharmo bhrājan tejasā rocamāṇaḥ | nudaṃ chatrūn pradahan me sapatnān ādityo dyām adhyarukṣad vipaścit ||

**16.151.1**

vaiśvānaraḥ saṃ tapaty antarikṣaṃ divaṃ ca samiddho agnir divyas tapojāḥ | śikṣantv asmā abhi sunvanti soma ṛtena bhrājann amṛtaṃ vaste atkī ||

**16.151.2**

rārahyete nelayataḥ śītarūre tanvāv asya bhīme | rūpāṇy eti bahudhā vasāno guhā kṛṇvānas tanvaḥ parācīḥ ||

**16.151.3**

pañcāre cakre parivartamāne samārohanti bhuvanāni viśvā | tasya nākṣas tapyate bhūribhāraḥ sanād eva na chidyate sanābhiḥ ||

**16.151.4**

pañcabhis taptas tapaty eṣa etat sahasradhāmānam anu tiṣṭhanty enam | sapta tvā sūrya harito vahanti brahmaṇādityas trivṛtā mukhena ||

**16.151.5**

vi dyotate dyotata ā ca dyotate apsv antar amṛto gharma udyan | hantā vṛtrasya haritām anīkam anādhṛṣṭās tanvaḥ sūryasya ||

**16.151.6**

gharmaḥ paścād uta gharmaḥ purastād ayodaṃṣṭrāya dviṣato 'pi dadhmaḥ | vaiśvānaraḥ śītarūre vasānaḥ sapatnān me dviṣato hantu sarvān ||

**16.151.7**

ṛtūn ṛtubhiḥ śrapayati brahmaṇaikavīro gharmaḥ śucānaḥ samidhā samiddhaḥ | brahmā tvā tapati brahmaṇā tejasā ca ||

**16.151.8**

brahma jajñānaṃ prathamaṃ samā dade taj jāyamānaṃ na bibhide na bhidyate | tad aṅgamānaṃ pitaraṃ vyāhati tasya nāḍya ātatā vitatās tatā u tāḥ ||

**16.151.9**

asayajjāna sata ā jabhāra ya īṃ jajāna sa id asya bhartā | prajāpatiḥ prajābhiḥ saṃvidānas trīṇi jyotīṃṣi dadhate na pākaḥ ||

**16.151.10**

prajāpatiś carati garbhe antaḥ sa jāyamāno bahudhā pra jāyate | tasya padam abhi paśyanti vedhasas tasminn ārpitā bhuvanāni viśvā ||

**16.152.1**

yasmin devāḥ pitaro manuṣyā arā nābhāv iva śritāḥ | apāṃ tvā puṣpaṃ pṛcchāmi yatra tan māyayāhitam ||

**16.152.2**

yatrāpāṃ puṣpaṃ nihitaṃ māyayātihitaṃ guhā | yo vai tad vidyāt pratyakṣaṃ sa vidyād brāhmaṇaṃ mahat ||

**16.152.3**

maṇis triṣūtro nihitaḥ svarvid ūrdhvas tiryaṅ viśa etu prajānan | sa pumān puṃso janayann ṛtena sarvān antān gacchatu sadya eva ||

**16.152.4**

sarvāṃ rātrīṃ sam oṣatv ādityo jātavedasā | agner adhi divam ā rohann āyuṣā sam anaktu mā varcasā saṃ sṛjāti mā ||

**16.152.5**

gharmaḥ sāhasraḥ samidhā samiddho 'sapatnāḥ pradiśo me kṛṇotu | sapatnān sarvān me sūryo hantu vaiśvānaro hariḥ ||

**16.152.6**

gharmas taptaḥ pra dahatu bhrātṛvyān dviṣato mama | ā dattāṃ chatrūn āditya udyan sūryaḥ pṛtanyataḥ ||

**16.152.7**

vaiśvānaraḥ pra dahatu bhrātṛvyān dviṣato vṛṣā | udyan me śukra ādityo vi mṛdho hantu sūryaḥ ||

**16.152.8**

śukraṃ suparṇaṃ hari brahma bhrājad ajasraṃ jyotir divam ā tatāna | hariḥ suparṇaḥ pramathiṣṇur āśuḥ sapatnān hantu mahātā vadhena ||

**16.152.9**

hariḥ suparṇaḥ sudino 'bhayaṃkaro hiraṇyavarṇo durudāpa āśuḥ | sapatnān sarvān me sūrya ādityo hantu raśmibhiḥ ||

**16.152.10**

mahāntam arthaṃ pari sadya etv ahorātre vidadhac chukra udyan | sapatnān sarvān me sūrya etu vaiśvānaro dahan ||

**16.152.11**

**16.152.12**

tejas tapāṃsi mukhato bibharṣy ānandaṃ bhūtiṃ mahasaḥ pratiṣṭhām | paryūhamāṇaḥ śriyam eṣi sarvato amoghaṃ satyaṃ yaśa udyataṃ te ||

**16.153.1**

brahmacārīṣṇaṃś carati rodasī ubhe yasmin devāḥ saṃmanaso bhavanti | sa dādhāra pṛthivīṃ dyām utāmūṃ sa ācāryaṃ tapasā pipartu ||

**16.153.2**

ācārya upanayamāno brahmacāriṇaṃ kṛṇute garbham antaḥ | taṃ rātrīs tisra udare bibharti taṃ jātaṃ draṣṭum abhisaṃyanti devāḥ | brahmacāriṇaṃ pitaro manuṣyā devajanā gandharvā enam anu yanti sarve ||

**16.153.3**

trayastriṃśataṃ triśatān ṣaṭ sahasrān sarvān sa devāṃs tapasā piparti | iyaṃ samit pṛthivī dyaur dvitīyā- -utāntarikṣaṃ samidhā pṛṇāti | brahmacārī samidhā mekhalāvī śrameṇa lokāṃs tapasā piparti ||

**16.153.4**

pūrvo jāto brahmaṇo brahmacārī gharmaṃ vasānas tapaso 'dhi tiṣṭhat | tasmāj jātaṃ brāhmaṇaṃ brahma jyeṣṭhaṃ devāś ca sarve amṛtena sākam ||

**16.153.5**

brahmacārī samidhā samiddhaḥ kārṣṇaṃ vasāno dīkṣito dīrghaśmaśruḥ | sa sadya eti pūrvād aparaṃ samudraṃ lokān saṃrabhya muhur ācarikrat ||

**16.153.6**

brahmacārī janayan brahma- -apo lokaṃ prajāpatiṃ parameṣṭhiṇaṃ virājam | garbho bhūtvāmṛtasya yonāv indro bhūtvāsurāṃs tatarha ||

**16.153.7**

imāṃ bhūmīṃ pṛthivīṃ brahmacārī bhikṣāṃ jabhāra prathamo divaṃ ca | te brahma kṛtvā samidhā upāsate tayor ārpitā bhuvanāni viśvā ||

**16.153.8**

ācāryas tatakṣa nabhasī ubhe urvī gambhīre pṛthivīṃ divaṃ ca | te brahmacārī tapasābhi rakṣati tayor devāḥ sadhamādaṃ madanti ||

**16.153.9**

arvāg anyaḥ paro anyo guhā nidhī nihitau brāhmaṇasya | tau brahmacārī tapasābhi rakṣati tat kevalaṃ kṛṇute brahma vidvān ||

**16.154.1**

arvāg anyo divas pṛṣṭhād ito 'nyaḥ pṛthivyā agnī sameto nabhasī antareme | tayoḥ śrayante raśmayo 'dhi dṛḍhās tān ā tiṣṭhati tapasā brahmacārī ||

**16.154.2**

abhikrandann aruṇaḥ śitiṅgo bṛhacchepo 'nu bhūmau jabhāra | brahmacārī siñcati sānau retaḥ pṛthivyāṃ tena jīvanti pradiśaś catasraḥ ||

**16.154.3**

**16.154.4**

agnau sūrye candramasi mātariśvani brahmacāry apsu samidham ā dadhāti | teṣām arcīṃṣi pṛthag abhre caranti teṣām ājyaṃ puruṣo varṣam aśvaḥ ||

**16.154.5**

**16.154.6**

**16.154.9**

**16.154.10**
