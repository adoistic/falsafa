**19.1.1**  Sing at evening, sing the great hymn, sing it brilliantly, O Atharvan. Praise the god Savitṛ.

**19.1.2**  Praise him, the one within the river, the son of truth, the youthful one — he of guileless speech, of goodly grace.

**19.1.3**  May that god Savitṛ, lord of good things, press out for us good things — through this good praise may he grant us easy passage.

**19.1.4**  O priests, press the soma for Indra and run the rite — he who hears the praiser's call, and hears our invocation.

**19.1.5**  Press the soma for Indra the soma-drinker, the wielder of the vajra — he is the young conqueror, the ruler, much praised by men.

**19.1.6**  Let the soma drops enter you, O full one, as birds enter a tree — crush and strike down the hostile demons.

**19.1.7**  Far from us be that bolt, O gods — may it be elsewhere. Far from mortals be the curse.

**19.1.8**  May our bounty be as a friend; may Indra be our friend, and Savitṛ our friend. May Bhaga, of true law, be our friend.

**19.1.9**  May the divine goddesses, mistresses of men, attend us with great shelter and aid — they whose wings are uncut.

**19.1.10**  I implore Agni and Bhava — all protect us, press down the serpent, the disease, and fierce plague. O Aditi, keep divine fear far from us; O Maruts, grant us shelter and heroic strength.

**19.1.11**  I implore heaven and earth, the mountains, the waters, the sky, the sun, the broad atmosphere, the forest trees, the plants, the cows, and the sacred verses — may the lords of cosmic order be gracious to us.

**19.1.12**  I call Viṣṇu, Pūṣan, Brahmaṇaspati, Bhaga, the worthy Savitṛ for our aid. May Soma, Varuṇa, Vāyu, Agni, and mighty Bhaga come here to protect us.

**19.1.13**  May Indra and Pūṣan protect us; may Varuṇa and the Maruts protect us. O Child of the Waters, O seven rivers, protect us — may Viṣṇu protect us, and heaven too.

**19.1.14**  May heaven and earth protect us for our victory; may the pressing stone protect us, may Soma protect us from distress. May the goddess Sarasvatī, of sweet invocation, protect us; may Agni protect us with his benevolent guardians.

**19.1.15**  May the two divine Aśvins, of great wonders, protect us; may Dawn and Night keep us safe. O Child of the Waters, of unwavering protection — O god at the summit of all — O Aditi, grant us shelter.

**19.2.1**  Tvaṣṭṛ, my divine speech, Parjanya, Brahmaṇaspati — may Aditi now protect us with sons and brothers; may she grant strength hard to cross for our rescue.

**19.2.2**  May Aṃśa, Bhaga, Varuṇa, Mitra, Aryaman, and Aditi protect us from distress. May the unharmed one drive away his enmity, and ward off the enemy near him.

**19.2.3**  O divine Tvaṣṭṛ, increase us for all wholeness; advance our resolve and our glory together. Protect us, O wide one, ever-vigilant; O Father Heaven, drive away misfortune from here.

**19.2.4**  Those who afflict our body with words and sorcerous acts, O Soma — cut them down low so that they may wither; may they not see the sun rising.

**19.2.5**  With your arrows, O Soma, with your bow, with your fist-smiting power do we prevail. Cut down those who have done this wrong — we strike them with staffs and with mighty acts.

**19.2.6**  May the treader lead them by the footstep; may the ancestors teach them their doom with blows — so that not one of them may live.

**19.2.7**  By the path by which Soma, Aditi, and the Mitras travel, the guileless ones — by that path be our protector.

**19.2.8**  He who harms us the well-praised, the ill-praiser, O Soma — strike him in the face with the vajra; crushed, he shall go away.

**19.2.9**  He who attacks us, O Soma — both the kinsman and the outsider — cut off his strength; like great heaven, smite him with your own killing blow.

**19.2.10**  O gods, may evil return upon the evil-doer; may guilt recognize him who made the guilt. May the knowing son return to strike the father — and whatever is his own is struck down too.

**19.2.11**  With what curse he curses, and with what curse we curse him in return — grasping both together, from that near place let them hurl the bolt at him.

**19.2.12**  The curses that dwell in the earth, those in the broad atmosphere, those in the wind's current — with all of these we roll him over: him who hates us, and him whom we hate.

**19.2.13**  Those who have crept around us bearing harmful charms — may the god pluck out their root as one pulls up a cucumber vine.

**19.2.14**  When the evil-placing gods seek to fasten evil upon another — the burden of that evil, hard to bear, shall come back upon its maker.

**19.2.15**  Let the evil of the evil-placer flow back again, as glory flows; like the great flood of the river Sindhu, let it roll back upon them.

**19.3.1**  The lightning bolt ever hurled, flying westward, has indeed fallen — may it come back and strike the one who sent it.

**19.3.2**  The one who ever presses forward against another, smearing arrows with poison — let the arrow strike him back in return.

**19.3.3**  Whatever you hurl abundantly against the one facing you to drive him away — eat that as poison eats poison.

**19.3.4**  Homage to you, O Son of the slope, from where you gather heat — be gracious to our bodies; bring well-being to our offspring.

**19.3.5**  Homage to you, O Son of the slope; homage to your burning bolt. We know your highest abode in the hidden place — you are set within the ocean as the navel.

**19.3.6**  You whom all the gods begot, fashioning you as an arrow, swift and fierce for casting — praised in our assembly, be gracious to us under the direction of Mitra and Varuṇa.

**19.3.7**  O Maruts with sun-like skin, O Sons of the slope, grant us shelter, grant us wide protection.

**19.3.8**  Be gracious, be completely gracious — be gracious to us for the sake of our cows, our offspring, and our bodies.

**19.3.9**  Homage to you, O lightning; homage to you, O thunder. Homage to you, O hailstone, with which you hurl against the steadfast one.

**19.3.10**  He who, being no god, attacks us — O Brahmaṇaspati, deliver all of him up to us.

**19.3.11**  With those powers by which you, O Soma, overcome and subdue the asuras — with those same powers be our protector.

**19.3.12**  The strengths of the asuras that you, O gods, have chosen for yourselves — with those same strengths speak on our behalf.

**19.3.13**  O Agni, fed with ghee, lead him upward, to the highest place; unite him with radiance — may he become a sharer in the gods' portion.

**19.3.14**  O Indra, make him foremost among his kinsmen; may he be the master of his peers. Unite him with the abundance of wealth; make him great through offspring.

**19.3.15**  He in whose house we make the oblation — him, O Agni, do you increase. May Soma speak on his behalf, and this Brahmaṇaspati too.

**19.4.1**  We implore Vaiśvānara, the possessor of cosmic order, the lord of the light of truth — the unceasing, blazing heat.

**19.4.2**  He spread this forth, lifting up the heavenly light of sacrifice; the self-willed one releases the seasons.

**19.4.3**  Agni in his beloved abodes, the desire of all that has been and all that shall be — the sole sovereign shines resplendent.

**19.4.4**  As the sun circles the sky, I have come to the birth-place of the serpents; as the night sweeps aside the other world, so ward off this poison.

**19.4.5**  By what was uttered in ancient times by the brahmin-priests, by the seers, by the gods — by whatever existed and shall come to be — with that I ward off your poison.

**19.4.6**  The rivers are mingled with honey; the mountains and hills are honey; sweet is the Paruṣṇī, sweet the water-plants — may there be ease for the face, ease for the heart.

**19.4.7**  As this broad earth has borne the established world, so may your embryo be held firm, to be brought forth for the Vivifier.

**19.4.8**  [Lacuna] ... may these forest-lords be borne ... [Lacuna].

**19.4.9**  As this broad earth has borne the mountains and the waters, so may your embryo be held firm, to be brought forth for well-being.

**19.4.10**  Flowing by day and flowing by night — I, of excellent resolve, call upon you, divine waters, you who flow on.

**19.4.11**  May the waters, invoked, the active ones, release from here for guidance — may they immediately come to pass.

**19.4.12**  At the impulsion of the divine Savitṛ, human beings perform their work — may these waters and herbs be propitious to us.

**19.4.13**  Of your hundred channels and of your thousand veins, those that stand in the middle — together they have come to rest at the extremities.

**19.4.14**  Around you the sand-made bows stood firm even crosswise; stand still, trembling lightly, be at peace.

**19.4.15**  Those kinswomen who go yonder, all clad in red — like women without brothers they stand, their splendor struck down.

**19.4.16**  Stand still, O lower one; stand still, O upper one; and you too stand still, O middle one; may the little finger also stand still; may the great vein stand still.

**19.5.1**  Homage to the deaths inflicted by gods, homage to the deaths inflicted by kings; and those deaths that belong to the common folk — to them, O Death, I offer you reverence.

**19.5.2**  Homage to you in your aspect of pronouncing over, homage to you in your aspect of pronouncing away; O Death, homage to your benevolent thought, and to your malevolent thought this homage.

**19.5.3**  Homage to your sorcerers, homage to your remedies; O Death, homage to your roots, and to the brahmin-priests this homage.

**19.5.4**  The nine and the ninety that come together towards the neck — may all of them vanish from here, like the growths of the apacit disease.

**19.5.5**  The seven and the seventy that come together towards the shoulders — may all of them vanish from here, like the growths of the apacit disease.

**19.5.6**  The five and the fifty that come together towards the throat — may all of them vanish from here, like the growths of the apacit disease.

**19.5.7**  The devouring one, the non-devouring one — your essence, O fierce devourer; we have eaten your gruel — for you are indeed that one by which you have devoured yourself.

**19.5.8**  The brown one and the brown-eared one, the blue-eared pot-lady — O Corpse-gazer.

**19.5.9**  O Tauvilikā, with Avelā — one has devoured, Ailava has devoured; here, accepting your own oblation with mind — svāhā.

**19.5.10**  Svāhā — with mind I make this offering; into her whose they were I pour it, O terrible one, for the releasing of these bound ones.

**19.5.11**  People think of you as Earth; I know you all around as Nirṛti, from every side; in truth you are rich in oblations — this is your portion, and through it service is to be rendered to you — svāhā.

**19.5.12**  So thus, O Nirṛti of all desirable things, release from us your iron bonds; you who are allied with Yama as his twin — the one who hates us and the one we hate, upon him cast all those bonds back.

**19.5.13**  You are the highest among plants; the trees are your support. May there be support for us against the one who seeks to harm us.

**19.5.14**  Whether he who seeks to harm us has kinsmen or has no kinsmen — crossing beyond all those with kinsmen, may I become the highest.

**19.5.15**  Just as Soma is called the highest oblation among plants, so — like you among trees — may I become the highest.

**19.6.2**  By the word of Indra, of Mitra, and of Varuṇa, and by the word of all the gods, we ward off the disease from you.

**19.6.3**  Just as Vṛtra held back these waters striving to flow in every direction, so I ward off your disease with Agni Vaiśvānara.

**19.6.4**  With a firm oblation we anoint the firm Soma; here let Indra make the clans, the tribute-bearing ones, yours alone.

**19.6.5**  I have brought you here; you have become present within, firm, standing unshaken; may all the clans come to you — let not the kingdom slip away from you.

**19.6.6**  Be here, do not go away, standing unshaken like a mountain; be firm here, O Indra — hold the kingdom firm here.

**19.6.7**  Indra made this one firm with a firm oblation; in his favor Soma spoke out, and so did Brahmaṇaspati here.

**19.6.8**  May the divine Savitṛ hold your realm firm, may divine Bṛhaspati hold it firm; may Indra and Agni hold your realm, the unshaken, firmly in place.

**19.6.9**  Firm is the sky, firm is the earth, firm are the oceans and the mountains; firm indeed by sacred law, firm — firm is this king of the clans.

**19.6.10**  The bull of Indra, the bull of the sky, the bull of the earth is this one; bull of all that exists — become the sole bull.

**19.6.11**  The ocean rules over the flowing waters; Agni is the lord of the earth; the Sun rules over the constellations; you [rule over ...] [Lacuna].

**19.6.12**  You are the sovereign of the Asuras, the chief among human beings; you are a half-sharer among the gods — become the sole bull.

**19.6.13**  Indra and Pūṣan have run around the paths of these enemies; may those armies of the foes yonder be confused today and henceforth.

**19.6.14**  Bewildered, may the foes wander like headless serpents; then, of these whose fire is blocked, let Indra smite the best one after another.

**19.6.15**  Bind the bull's hide upon these enemies; strike terror as of the antelope into them; may the foe rush away in retreat, and may the cow approach toward us.

**19.7.1**  O mighty Agni, you yoke all things together for the lord; you are kindled in the seat of refreshment — bring us riches.

**19.7.2**  Be of one mind, be united, may your minds be in accord; as the gods of old, in concord, attend to their share.

**19.7.3**  Common is your prayer, common your assembly, common your mind — your thoughts together; with a common oblation I offer for you, that you may be of one accord.

**19.7.4**  The sun rises across the sky, having mounted all the regions before; Āditya passes over the mountains, seen by all, the destroyer of the unseen evil.

**19.7.5**  I have brought the herb of Kaṇva, knowing the life-force, renowned, the all-healing plant — quell this person's unseen afflictions.

**19.7.6**  The cows have settled in the cowpen, the wild beasts have rested; the banners of the people have been furled, the unseen evils have been checked.

**19.7.7**  You flow forth from the Himālaya and make your way to the Sindhu; those divine waters have surely given me that heart-illumining medicine.

**19.7.8**  Whatever flashed through the eyes, through the heels, and through the heart — the waters have expelled all that, as Tvaṣṭṛ removes a defect from a cart.

**19.7.9**  All you rivers, queens of the Sindhu, consorts of the Sindhu — give us the medicine for that; with it we shall enjoy your gift.

**19.7.10**  May the divine hosts purify me, may the Manus purify me by thought, may all beings purify me — may the purifying stream purify me.

**19.7.11**  May the purifying current purify me for will, for power, for life, and for long beholding of the sun.

**19.7.12**  O divine Savitṛ, purify us with both — with the filter and with your impulse — for clear sight.

**19.7.13**  The foremost dart of jealousy, the lowest and the highest — the wrath that dwells in the heart, the grief — these we charm out of you.

**19.7.14**  That which is lodged in your heart, tending toward the mind, about to fly — from that I release your jealousy, as steam is released from a skin.

**19.7.15**  As the earth is dead in mind toward the dead, more dead in mind than the dead — as the mind of a corpse is dead — so let the mind of jealousy be dead.

**19.8.1**  May Heaven and Earth, the two all-knowing ones, may the bright and great South wind carry me across; with awareness let Soma, Agni, Vāyu, Savitṛ, and Bhaga come along with the self-law.

**19.8.2**  Mind has come back to us, life has come back, sight has come back, intention has come back; Vaiśvānara, undeceived, protector of the body, stands within, guarding from misfortune and from blame.

**19.8.3**  We have come together with radiance, with vital sap, with our bodies, with the auspicious mind; may Tvaṣṭṛ, well-shaped, make wide room — let him fashion the body's injury into wholeness.

**19.8.4**  This is that superior yoke by which I adorn Indra for the conquest; increase his dominion, his glory, his greatness — as rain increases the grass.

**19.8.5**  O Agni and Soma, increase his sovereignty, increase his wealth; and for the supremacy of his kingdom make the yoke highest.

**19.8.6**  Both the one with kin and the one without kin, the one born here and the one born outside — all of them you deliver to us, for the sacrificer who presses the soma.

**19.8.7**  May great fame, driven by Indra, thousand-peaked, well-wrought, powerful, increase; flowing forth for long life — increase the oblation-bearer for all-round completeness.

**19.8.8**  Indra was born with glory, Agni with glory, Soma was born with glory; of all that exists I am the most glorious.

**19.8.9**  We, glorious, serve you, O Agni, the glorious one, with glories and with this oblation; grant us the kingdom driven by Indra — may we be under your protection, under your advocacy.

**19.8.10**  I draw your wrath from your heart as one draws the bowstring from a bow; then, becoming of one mind, we two shall go together like friends.

**19.8.11**  We lead your anger away so we two may go together like friends; with a heavy stone we press your wrath down below.

**19.8.12**  I tread upon your anger with both heels and forefeet; I drive away from you the rod-worthy blow, I drive away your wrath.

**19.8.13**  The path was golden, the oars were golden, the boats were golden by which I brought kuṣṭha here.

**19.8.14**  Born on the mountain where the suparṇa bird cries, born around the Himālaya — they come famed with riches: O kuṣṭha, come here, destroyer of fever.

**19.8.15**  You who are born in the mountains, mightiest of herbs — O kuṣṭha, come here, destroyer of fever, driving fever away from here.

**19.9.1**  Make great obeisance to the Idāvatsara, the Parivatsara, and the Saṃvatsara; may we be in the good favor of those worthy of sacrifice, in their auspicious goodwill.

**19.9.2**  Your many god-trodden paths that pass along Heaven and Earth — whichever of these may bring us victory, may all the gods surround us for that one.

**19.9.3**  Autumn, winter, the cool season, spring, summer, the rains — set us in good course; grant us a share in cattle and in plants — only in a sheltered place may we dwell in your shelter.

**19.9.4**  Let Vaiśvānara come forth to us with help from afar; may Agni with hymns free us from distress.

**19.9.5**  May Vaiśvānara come to us, together attending this sacrifice and my praise-song.

**19.9.6**  May Vaiśvānara along with the Aṅgirasas fashion our praise-song and sacrifice; may he seize brilliant light and the sun among these.

**19.9.7**  I have conquered you when scratched, I have conquered you when blocked; as a wolf churns a sheep, so I churn what you have done.

**19.9.8**  As a thunderbolt always strikes a tree without resistance, so may I strike that gambler with the dice, without resistance.

**19.9.9**  From the swift and the slow, from the clans that seek the gods — may fortune come together from all sides; what is made is in my hand.

**19.9.10**  As long as the sky endures, as long as the earth, as long as the sun shines — for that long I release poison into you, as long as the rivers flow.

**19.9.11**  They rise up breathing out like cattle from the stall; of the snakes that are seen, the poison has no one to speak it away.

**19.9.12**  The speckle-bowed one struck at the junction of the arteries; the poison leapt to the head and scattered through the limbs.

**19.9.13**  May the serpent not slay us, O gods, us with our offspring, together with our men; let it not spread what is closed, let it not close what is open.

**19.9.14**  I set tooth against your teeth, I set jaw against your jaws; I set tongue against your tongue, I set mouth against your mouth.

**19.9.15**  Homage to the dark serpent, homage to the king who goes sideways; homage to the coiling tawny one, homage to the divine folk.

**19.10.1**  To the mind, to consciousness, to thought, to intention, and to understanding; to wisdom, to learning, and to sight — we pay homage with our oblation.

**19.10.2**  To the in-breath, to the out-breath, to the diffused breath, to the abundantly seed-bearing; to Sarasvatī of wide extent — we pay homage with our oblation.

**19.10.3**  May the divine seers, protectors of the body, the pure ones, born of austerity, not abandon us. O immortals, accompany us mortals; grant us further lifespan to live.

**19.10.4**  This indeed is the remedy, this is Rudra's remedy, by which they warded off the arrow of single point and hundred barbs.

**19.10.5**  Sprinkle us with the jālāṣa water; moisten us with the jālāṣa. Grant us that jālāṣa, the auspicious remedy, to keep us alive.

**19.10.6**  May there be ease for us and joy for us; let nothing whatever afflict us. May earth cleanse away disease; may all be a remedy for us.

**19.10.7**  May Indra the bountiful make me glorious; may Soma, Varuṇa, Vāyu, and Agni make me glorious. May the god Savitṛ make me glorious; may I be dear to the giver, beloved of the sacrificial fee.

**19.10.8**  As Indra is glorious between heaven and earth, as the waters are among the plants — as he is among all the gods — so may we be glorious among the gods.

**19.10.9**  Glorious ourselves, with glories we approach glorious Indra; we honor him with this oblation. May he establish for us the sovereignty driven by Indra; may we be under his protection, his special pleaders.

**19.10.10**  Let Varuṇa come here; let Soma, Agni, and Bṛhaspati come here together with the Vasus. O kinsmen, all of one mind, come together to share the glory of this fierce and discerning one.

**19.10.11**  Come here, do not depart from us. May Pūṣan make for you a pathless way if you go beyond. The lord of the homestead has called you here; may your delight, O kinsmen, rest in me.

**19.10.12**  Whatever vigor is within your hearts, whatever intention has entered your minds — that I attune with oblation and clarified butter; may your delight, O kinsmen, rest in me.

**19.10.13**  Come to my thought, all of you, at the moment of departure and departure, as to the invocation of Indra and Agni. May Bṛhaspati yoke you to me; be of one mind and follow my speech.

**19.10.14**  I seize your minds with my mind; come to my thought with your thoughts. I make your hearts subject to my will; come following in my path, come to me.

**19.10.15**  Let your eye come to me, let your breath come to me; may the Dhātṛ make your path follow my footstep. Whether your elders or your young ones — come, following in my path, come with those who follow.

**19.11.2**  You were born above the gods; you are placed as a friend of Soma. As such, be gracious for the in-breath, the out-breath, and the sight of this one.

**19.11.3**  You were indeed struck against the iron post, bound by the thousand deaths. May Yama give you back to me; to that god we pay homage with oblation.

**19.11.4**  The bond that the divine Nirṛti has bound upon your neck, the unbreakable fetter — that I loose from you for life and for strength. Being released, eat untroubled food.

**19.11.5**  Homage to you, O Nirṛti who possesses all good things; loosen this iron bond. You, being in accord with Yama and the Fathers, cause this one to ascend to the highest vault of heaven.

**19.11.6**  Offer this well into the inner fire, with clarified butter, as the destroyer of sorcerers. O Agni, burn the demons from afar; let them not scorch our bodies.

**19.11.7**  Śarva has broken your necks, O Piśācas; may Agni crush your ribs. The all-potent plant has united you with death.

**19.11.8**  Make us free of Piśācas below, free of Piśācas above; O Indra, make us free of Piśācas behind, free of Piśācas in front.

**19.11.9**  Indra first made the Asuras handless. We too cut off the arms of our enemies with this oblation.

**19.11.10**  Let the enemy be handless, powerless against us — those who come against us in battle with their armies. Strike them together, O Indra, with a great blow; let the sin-slayer, piercing them, make them flee.

**19.11.11**  Let their fury sink down, let their two arms yoked to the mind sink down. O Parāśara, crush their outward-turned vigor; bring the wealth here toward us.

**19.11.12**  You who stretch your bows, who draw them back, who shoot and run — stand there as enemies made handless; today Indra has dispersed you.

**19.11.13**  Let the enemies be handless; we wither their limbs. O Indra, may we divide up their possessions among our herds.

**19.11.14**  The handlessness you gods shoot as an arrow upon the handless — may my warriors conquer with steadfast Indra as their ally.

**19.12.1**  The aśvattha tree has climbed the śamī tree; there the rite for begetting a son is performed. That very thing is his remedy — what they bring forth in women.

**19.12.2**  The seed truly arises in the male and is poured into the female. That indeed is the means of obtaining a son — so spoke Prajāpati.

**19.12.3**  Prajāpati reflected and Sinīvālī fashioned it: placing the female nature elsewhere, and placing the male nature here.

**19.12.4**  "The plants over which Soma is king" — thus one verse [cited by reference].

**19.12.5**  May they release me from the curse-born sin, and from that which belongs to Varuṇa; and from the snare of Yama, from all evil done against the gods.

**19.12.6**  Whatever sin we have committed with the eye, with the mind, or with speech, whether waking or sleeping — from that sin may wise Soma purify me by his sovereign power.

**19.12.7**  Let the sacrifice be all-conquering; let Agni be all-conquering; let Soma be all-conquering; let Indra be all-conquering. So that this one may overcome all battle hosts — thus we offer this oblation at the Agnihotra.

**19.12.8**  O Mitra and Varuṇa, may there be self-power; swell here the progeny-rich sovereignty with sweetness. Drive hatred and Nirṛti away; bestow upon this one sovereignty, brilliance, and vital force.
