**20.1.1**  Those who by thought led speech to its summit, or who by mind declared the truths — waxing great through the third sacred utterance, by the fourth they perceived the name of the milch-cow.

**20.1.2**  He, the son, came to know his father; he, the son, came to know his mother; he became a son, he became again a munificent one. He disclosed the sky, he the atmosphere, he the sun; he became all the worlds — he came into being.

**20.1.3**  The Atharvan as father, all-divine; the embryo of the mother, the father bringing together — he who discerned the abode of immortality, who saw the boundaries of eternal wealth.

**20.1.4**  Some of your forms are auspicious, some are inauspicious — you bear them all without anger. Three forms of speech are hidden in the secret place; from one of them all this world has spread forth.

**20.1.5**  Aditi is the sky, Aditi the atmosphere, Aditi is the mother, he the father, he the son; all the gods are Aditi, the five peoples are Aditi; Aditi is what has been born, Aditi is what shall be born.

**20.1.6**  I have made praise for the sons of Aditi, the great sheltering ones, the great and unharmed ones. Their abode indeed is fathomless, reaching the sea; beyond their trembling power there is nothing whatsoever.

**20.1.7**  At the starting of the vigorous race we shall invoke by speech the great mother, Aditi by name, in whom all this universe has entered — may that goddess, easy to invoke, bestow shelter upon us.

**20.1.8**  The great mother of those of excellent vows, the wife of cosmic order — we would invoke her for aid: Aditi, of mighty dominion, ever-young, wide-reaching, granting fine shelter, of good guidance.

**20.1.9**  The well-protecting earth, the sinless sky, Aditi of fine shelter and good guidance — may we ascend the divine boat, well-oared, faultless and never leaking, for our well-being.

**20.1.10**  O Vāyu, well-invoked, you travel with one and with ten, with two and twenty for the sacrifice, with three and thirty — unyoke those here, release them now.

**20.2.1**  By this pervasion he generated glorious deeds; for that radiance is broad for choosing a path. Varuṇa rose up toward the summit of sweetness when he set his own body in motion within himself.

**20.2.2**  The gods worshipped sacrifice with sacrifice — those were the first cosmic laws. Those great ones attend the vault of heaven where the ancient Sādhya gods abide.

**20.2.3**  The sacrifice came into being; it fully came into being; it became lord over the earth. It was born, it grew again — may it establish wealth in us.

**20.2.4**  When the immortal gods worshipped the immortal gods with an oblation, worshipped the deathless with a deathless mind — let us speak of that in the highest heaven, let us utter it at the rising of the sun.

**20.2.5**  The enchanted gods also worshipped with a dog, and worshipped with the limbs of a cow in many ways. He who by mind discovered this sacrifice — may he proclaim it to us; let us speak of him here and now.

**20.2.6**  When the gods extended the sacrifice with the primordial being as the oblation — who indeed today could tell us of that, if the oblation-offerers were actually set in motion?

**20.2.7**  May Indra, the good protector, self-strong, with his aids, gracious and all-knowing, be favorable; may he thrust away enmity and make us fearless — may we be lords of heroic strength.

**20.2.8**  May that good-protecting, self-strong Indra keep enmity far away from us, away into the distance. May we be in the favor of that worshipful one, abiding in his auspicious goodwill.

**20.2.9**  Your broad thunder, your towering form, your divine banner that adorns all this world — be merciful to us, O god, with lightning for the crops, and do not strike us down with the rays of the sun.

**20.2.10**  That breast of yours, O Sarasvatī, that is richly nourishing, that brings delight, that is grace-giving, easy to invoke and full of skill — the one with which you win all desirable things, set that here for giving nourishment.

**20.3.1**  This oblation of yours, O Sarasvatī, mixed with ghee — this is the mouth-offering belonging to the ancestors. These most beneficent utterances of yours that have been proclaimed — by these may we become wealthy patrons.

**20.3.2**  Pūṣan was born on the foremost of paths, on the foremost path of heaven, on the foremost path of earth. Knowing the way, he travels to and fro between both most beloved abodes.

**20.3.3**  Pūṣan knows all these directions; may he lead us by the most fearless path. A giver of well-being, shining bright, with all heroic power, never neglectful, may he go before us, ever knowing.

**20.3.4**  May Pūṣan follow our cattle; may Pūṣan protect our horses; may Pūṣan win strength for us.

**20.3.5**  O Pūṣan, under your vow may we never come to harm at any time. We are your praisers here.

**20.3.6**  One of your forms is bright, another is worthy of worship; you have two forms of different appearance — you are like the sky. For you protect all creative powers, O self-powerful one. May your bountiful gift be here, O Pūṣan.

**20.3.7**  May Dhātṛ bestow on the pious man an undiminished, forward-moving life. May we hold in mind the good favor of that god whose law is truth.

**20.3.8**  May Dhātṛ bestow all things upon the pious man, upon the pious man desiring offspring, in his house. May immortal offspring clothe themselves about him; may all the gods and Aditi, with one accord, be favorable.

**20.3.9**  May Dhātṛ, Rāti, and Savitṛ be pleased with this; may Prajāpati, lord of treasures, our Agni — may Tvaṣṭṛ, delighting in offspring together with Pūṣan, bestow wealth upon the sacrificer.

**20.3.10**  Dhātṛ rules over offspring and over wealth; Dhātṛ generated this entire universe. May abundant nourishment be conveyed to the pious man — to that god let us offer homage with an oblation.

**20.4.1**  For you, O god, have set forth for the primal Father his full stature and his breadth. Now, O Savitar, press out for us desirable gifts twofold, along with abundant cattle.

**20.4.2**  Go forward from the good to the better; let Bṛhaspati be your forerunner. Then make this man, in the choicest part of this earth, one who has driven off enemies, possessed of all heroic men.

**20.4.3**  The god Savitar, dwelling in the house, most excellent, bestowing wealth, vigor, and long lives upon the fathers — may he drink soma, may he exhilarate this man at the sacrifice; even the wide-roaming one delights in his holy ordinance.

**20.4.4**  That brilliant gift of Savitar of true might, of the god, at his impulsion, do we win — that which Kaṇva milked for the great bull, the full-uddered cow of thousandfold streams, for the sustaining.

**20.4.5**  Who among the nobles, desiring better fortune, will lead us out of this blameworthy hostility? Who desires the fulfillment of vows? Who desires sacrifice? Who wins long life among the gods?

**20.4.6**  Who, milking by friendship with Bṛhaspati the dappled cow given by Varuṇa to the Atharvan — easy to milk, ever with her calf — shall make the body prosper according to his will?

**20.4.7**  Heat has not scorched him, nor has cold struck him down; the earth, the swift-flowing, streams forth for him. Even the waters flow for him unceasingly; where soma is, there is blessing evermore.

**20.4.8**  In whose quarter of the sky this shines forth, toward Anumati do the descendants adorn themselves; whose lap is the wide atmosphere — may she grant us abundant shelter.

**20.4.9**  May Anumati today guide our sacrifice to the gods; may Agni, the carrier of oblations, be a source of joy for the one who has given.

**20.4.10**  For truly, O Anumati, you will consent; bring welfare to us. Bestow nourishment upon our offspring; extend our lifespans for us.

**20.5.1**  May the consenting Anumati consent — the nourishment rich in progeny, never diminishing. May we not fall into her wrath; may that goddess, easy to invoke, grant us shelter.

**20.5.2**  O Anumati, consent to those deeds of ours — whatever we have done with our bodies; whatever has been done regarding offspring, regarding bodies, regarding progeny, among cattle, among herbs, within the waters.

**20.5.3**  May divine Anumati, well-born, possessed of good dominion and heroic power, come to us. For her guidance has been benevolent; may she protect this sacrifice beloved by the gods.

**20.5.4**  Anumati begot all this — whatever trembles, whatever moves, and whatever stands still. May I be in the good grace of her, O goddess Anumati; for you, O Anumati, will indeed consent to us.

**20.5.5**  By that name of yours which is easy to invoke, O well-guiding Anumati, agreed upon and good to bestow — by that name, O goddess, swell nourishment for us, all-desired and rich in heroes, with benevolent thought.

**20.5.6**  I consecrate you for progeny; may progeny breathe after you; breathe life into the progeny. I buy your brightness with brightness, your radiance with radiance, your immortality with immortality.

**20.5.7**  You are the body of austerity, the color of Prajāpati. You are purchased with the supreme sacrificial animal.

**20.5.8**  I praise that god Savitar, of poet-wisdom, hidden within the two woolen strands, of true impulse, the bestower of gems, with a beloved thought.

**20.5.9**  He whose upward-shooting thought blazed with light at his impulsion — golden-handed and of excellent resolve, he measured out the sky with grace.

**20.5.10**  This one, the thought of seers, measuring out a thousand, the light in the ordinance — may the radiant one drive together the meeting dawns.

**20.6.1**  Sinless, like-minded, on the day of Svā, most full of spirit — at the recognition of the cow.

**20.6.2**  Come together, all, with might toward the lord of heaven, who alone has truly been the guest of peoples. That ancient one, making the new shine forth — the track has turned back around him alone, everywhere.

**20.6.3**  I call with repeated invocation the divine Kuhū, the immortal, the skilled in work, the easy to invoke, in this sacrifice. She who gives us the hearing of the ancestors — to you, O goddess, we render worship with oblation.

**20.6.4**  May Kuhū, consort of immortality among the gods, hear our oblation of this offering. Abundantly nourished, may she pour forth blessings upon the one who gives; may she bestow flourishing of riches upon the wise one.

**20.6.5**  Come to agreement together; come to union together; let your minds know one another. May Mitra unite all of you together; may there be concord among you with me.

**20.7.1**  Whatever Agni has granted us, whatever Indra, whatever all the gods, the Maruts bright with light — may Savitā of true ordinance, Sarasvatī, and Anumatī bestow that upon us.

**20.7.2**  O gods, for the sake of both kinds of welfare, grant shelter to our two-footed and four-footed creatures. One who eats, who drinks, who is nourished with strength and is well-fed — establish for us that state of well-being and ease, free from affliction.

**20.7.3**  O Bṛhaspati, O Savitā, awaken him; though already sharpened, whet him yet more keenly. Make him grow for great good fortune; may all the gods rejoice after him.

**20.7.4**  May he thrive yonder beyond what Yama holds; Bṛhaspati has released him from the accusation. May those two Aśvins, the healers of the gods, push back death from him, O Agni, with their skills.

**20.7.5**  O Indra and Varuṇa, you two who are drinkers of the pressed, drink this pressed, intoxicating soma, you who hold firm to your vows. Your chariot comes to the rite for the delight of the gods, comes at daybreak for the drinking.

**20.7.6**  O Indra and Varuṇa, you two bulls, drink your fill of the most sweetly intoxicating, vigorous soma. This drink has been poured out for you two among us; be seated on this sacred grass and be exhilarated.

**20.7.7**  Step wide, O Viṣṇu; make a broad dwelling space. Drink the ghee, O you born from ghee; lead forward, forward the lord of the sacrifice.

**20.7.8**  From the sky, O Viṣṇu, or from the earth, or from the wide, great middle-space — fill both your hands with sweetness; reach them out, from the right and from the left.

**20.7.9**  Now let me proclaim the heroic deeds of Viṣṇu, who measured out the earthly realms, who propped up the upper abode as he strode forth — the wide-pacing one who strides in three ways.

**20.7.10**  Viṣṇu proclaims those heroic deeds: like a fearsome wild beast that roams and dwells on the mountains — in whose three wide strides all the worlds abide.

**20.8.1**  O Agni and Viṣṇu, great is your dear abode; guard the secret names of ghee. Bearing the seven treasures in house after house, may the tongue move toward you laden with ghee.

**20.8.2**  O Agni and Viṣṇu, great is that greatness of yours; enjoy the secret gifts of ghee. Growing with excellent praise in house after house, may the tongue rise up to you with ghee.

**20.8.3**  Raise up the earth; pierce open this heavenly cloud. O Dhātṛ, lord and sovereign of the heavenly udder, open wide the aperture for us.

**20.8.4**  Moving away from the human realm, praising the divine might — turn back toward our leadings, O god, enjoying the friendship of the gods.

**20.8.5**  I worship the lord of nourishment through nourishment — Dhātṛ, the ordainer, in these worlds. By the half-measure be sweet for me here; by the half-measure be the equator now for that one yonder.

**20.8.6**  This nourishing one has been brought with nourishment beyond; let this one hear what I say. By the half-measure be sweet for me here; by the half-measure be the equator now for that one yonder.

**20.8.7**  Here seize him, O Nirṛti; here destroy him, O Nirṛti; here cut him entirely away. By the half-measure be sweet for me here; by the half-measure be the equator now for that one yonder.

**20.8.8**  Whatever I invoke of you, O Nourishment, hear that for me; be one to be invoked in this call of mine. Nirṛti, conspiring together with the gods, severs that one yonder from the snare of Varuṇa.

**20.8.9**  Whatever sin we have committed through forgetfulness, whatever we have come upon with our steps, O Jātavedas — from that protect us, O wise one, for good fortune; lead us and our friends to immortality.

**20.8.10**  O Agni, overseer, come to us; protect us from misfortune, protect us from evil dreams. Protect us from disease of the mouth and from all other afflictions.

**20.9.1**  You are radiance; may we become full of radiance — possessed of vision, rich in cattle, rich in progeny, and full of splendor.

**20.9.2**  The milk you collect by day, the milk you carry in your udders — that milk of yours, O all-formed ones, I take away with a herb.

**20.9.3**  The sweetness you cows gather from herbs, from waters — that milk of yours, O all-formed ones, I take away with a plant.

**20.9.4**  You who press down upon one — seek out a sleeping man, one who lies there listless. May Varuṇa enfold us with armor of iron, with armor of stone.

**20.9.5**  Those who, considering themselves Brahmins in our house, speak cruel words falsely — may they become laden with sin, laden with filth; may they carry away from us the evil dwelling.

**20.9.6**  This Agni, lord of truth, of ancient virile strength, like a charioteer has generated for the lord as his domestic priest. Placed at the navel of the earth, blazing forth — may those who fight against us be trampled underfoot.

**20.9.7**  O Agni, roar out for great good fortune; let your glories be the highest. Bring together a well-ordered household; advance against the great powers of those who act as enemies.

**20.9.8**  You have been seen, O lord of the house, as the guest in the hall; come to this sacrifice of ours, O knowing one. For, O Agni, having struck down all attackers, bring the riches of those who act as enemies.

**20.9.9**  O Agni, drive away our rivals who have been born; O Jātavedas, drive back those not yet born. Speak on our behalf with benevolence; grant us shelter, the three-folded protection that bursts forth.

**20.9.10**  Overpower the other rivals with might; O Jātavedas, strike down those not yet born. Fill this realm for good fortune; may the Vasus, the gods, rejoice following you.

**20.10.1**  O Bṛhaspati, whoever rival assails us — Mitra and Varuṇa — or whatever enemy, puffed up with pride: cast him down entirely beneath us, O Agni; may I, like Indra, be perceived as the highest among them.

**20.10.2**  May I be recognized as the foremost among them; may all stand under my sway. O Jātavedas, butter-backed and radiant, blazing with oblations offered, shine in the midst of riches.

**20.10.3**  O Agni, cast that puffed-up rival down beneath us; destroy him utterly. Those who seek to harm us — let them fall lower; crush all hostilities on every side.

**20.10.4**  You are the head of heaven, the unwearied force of the earth; may all peoples wait upon you bearing tribute. Among them may I stand as the mighty one in their midst; rule wide in your own domain, like Savitṛ.

**20.10.5**  We call hither Sarasvant, who bestows upon the worshipping sacrificer, the lord of nourishment and of riches — clothed in swelling wealth and glory — the seat of riches, to come to us face to face.

**20.10.7**  O Sarasvant, those waves of yours that are honey-sweet, dripping with ghee — with those be our protector.

**20.10.8**  The celestial ocean, great in milky abundance, the embryo of the waters, the bull of the plants — him who satisfies the devotee with riches, Sarasvant the richest — establish here.

**20.10.9**  You are the belly of Indra, the vessel of Soma, the self of the gods, and all-formed. Generate here the progeny that belongs to you; those who are elsewhere — let them here sing your praises, O self-sustaining one.

**20.10.10**  The divine falcon, fair-winged, man-seeing, thousand-footed, hundred-born, bestower of strength — may he bring down to us the wealth that has been carried away; let it rest with us among the fathers, rich in its own power.

**20.11.2**  The god Savitṛ, sustainer of all, of vast light, has come, unsubdued, with homage. May we mount the great boat of Aditi, where Soma is — there, ever, is blessing.

**20.15.1**  Whatever manifold food of varied kinds I eat — gold, horse, cow, goat, sheep — whatever it may be that I have accepted: may Agni make all of that free from sickness.

**20.15.3**  Whatever sin we incurred as we went about seeking to win tribute and accepting offerings, O Agni — free us from that sin, O Vaiśvānara; receive in return the oblations.

**20.15.4**  From the snares of the eye, and from the snares of the mind, from the snare of jealous gazing, and from the bonds of the powerful ones — from that free us, O Viśvāvasu, so that we may enjoy the gifts of the givers.
