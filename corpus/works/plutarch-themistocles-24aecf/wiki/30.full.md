But while Themistocles was asleep at midday before, it is said that the Mother of the Gods appeared to him in a dream and said: O Themistocles, shun a head of lions, that thou mayest not encounter a lion. And for this service to thee, I demand of thee Mnesiptolema to be my handmaid.
