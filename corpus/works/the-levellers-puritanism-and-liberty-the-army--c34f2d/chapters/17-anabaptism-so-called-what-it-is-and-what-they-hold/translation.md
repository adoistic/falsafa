The Church of Christ are a company of baptized believers, and whatsoever disciple can teach the Word or make out Christ, may baptize or administer other ordinances. That the church or body, though but of two or three, yet may enjoy the Word and ordinances by way of an administrator, or one deputed to administer, though no pastor. That none are to be baptized but believers. That those commonly called church-officers as pastors, &c., are such as the church or body may be without. That none are to be called brethren but baptized believers. All administration of ordinances were given to the Apostles as disciples; not so under the notion of church-power as is pretended. That none ought to communicate in the ordinances of Christ till first baptized. * * *
