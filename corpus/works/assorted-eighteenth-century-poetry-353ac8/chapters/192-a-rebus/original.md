A Rebus.

I'M pretty and useful in several ways,
 Yet many by me often shorten their days,
 Take one letter from me, and I shall appear
 What young men are fond of all days in the year;
 Take two letters from me, then I'm without doubt
 What you likewise are, if you don't find me out.
