That it may then be the same idea which it was before, we must exclude from it the elemen which we see there at present, or if those which are repugnant Edition: current; Page: [7] to it, are misplaced in this idea, they must themselves be excluded from it; that is to say, it must be rendered such as it was, when they were erroneously admitted into it, which is to restore it again to the same state in which it was, before it was changed by a false judgment, without our perceiving it.
