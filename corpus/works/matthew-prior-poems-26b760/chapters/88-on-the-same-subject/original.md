On the same Subject.

In a dark Corner of the House,
 Poor Helen sits, and sobs and cries:
 She will not see her Loving Spouse,
 Nor her more dear Picquet-Allies:
 Unless She finds her Eye-brows,
 She'll e'en weep out her Eyes.
