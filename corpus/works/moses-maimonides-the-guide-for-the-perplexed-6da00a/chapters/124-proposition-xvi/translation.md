Incorporeal bodies can only be numbered when they are forces situated in a body; the several forces must then be counted together with substances Edition: current; Page: [147] or objects in which they exist. Hence purely spiritual beings, which are neither corporeal nor forces situated in corporeal objects, cannot be counted, except when considered as causes and effects.
