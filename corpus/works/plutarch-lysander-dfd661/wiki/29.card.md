## Closes
> [p-6d7ea4] It ran as follows:— When thou huntest the wolf with the spear, watch closely the border, Orchalides, too, the hill which foxes never abandon. Now by border, the god meant the region about Delium, where Boeotia is conterminous with Attica; and by Orchalides, the hill which is now called Alopecus, or Fox-hill, in the parts of Haliartus which stretch towards Mount Helicon.
